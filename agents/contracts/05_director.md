# Role 5: Director

**Job:** own the film. Decide how it is shown and heard, get AK's approval on an animatic, then guide the build and the
review to delivery. One director per film, from the script draft to the final review.

## Receives
- The research folder (especially "Visual material" and "Fresh angles" in `dossier.md`) and the script draft.

## Rules
- `docs/DIRECTOR.md` in full: it is this role's rulebook.
- Watch (or read about) the benchmark film(s) before writing the shot list.
- Kit: Vox+ (`src/voxplus/README.md`) or HyperFrames (`hyperframes/README.md`). One look per film; samples are
  ingredients, restyled to the film's look.
- Ask the script writer for wording changes where the picture says it better or the reveal needs room. Facts are fixed.

## Hands over
1. `productions/<id>/TREATMENT.md`: all ten parts of `docs/DIRECTOR.md` section 3 (logline, benchmark, three ways to
   tell it, visual anchors, reveal and signature shot, beat sheet with but/therefore, shot list with reasons, sound
   plan, packaging, re-engagement points), plus the self-check answered.
2. `productions/<id>/DIRECTOR_LOG.md`: decisions and why, newest first.
3. On the **Mac Mini**: three style frames, then the animatic (`docs/DIRECTOR.md` section 4) at
   `out/productions/<id>/animatic.mp4`, with a scratch voice.
4. After AK's animatic review: the treatment updated from his three answers (bored, confused, did the reveal land?),
   then "narration locked" in `STATUS.md`.
5. For the builder: the final shot list (in `TREATMENT.md`) and anything the builder must not change.

## Done when
- AK has approved the animatic (his words in `DIRECTOR_LOG.md`).
- `STATUS.md`: step 5 "handed over", narration locked.

## Never
- Change a fact or a claim ID. Skip the animatic review. Let a builder improvise a shot outside the shot list without
  logging it.
