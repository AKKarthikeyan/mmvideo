# Role 7: Reviewer (Mac Mini; also reviews the animatic)

**Job:** see the film as the audience will, find what is confusing, flat or wrong, and get it fixed in one round
before AK's final review. Run in a **fresh session** that did not build the film.

## Receives
- The animatic or Short key-frame board (first review, alongside AK) or the rendered film, plus `TREATMENT.md`, `SCRIPT.md` and the ledger.

## What you can and can't judge
You work from frames, the transcript and timings: you cannot feel a film at full speed with sound. So judge what the
evidence shows, and leave feeling and rhythm to AK, flagged as questions for him.

## Checks, in order
1. **Story** (from the transcript and beat sheet): does every beat link by "but" or "therefore"? Is the question clear
   by 30 s? Is the reveal held back? Is the ending a callback, not admin?
2. **First-time viewer:** read the transcript as someone who knows nothing about the topic. List every "I was confused
   here", with the timecode.
3. **Picture** (contact sheet: one frame every 2–3 s, plus 0.2 s strips around the signature shot and each cut): one
   look throughout? Subject big enough? Text readable at phone size and held long enough (`docs/DIRECTOR.md`
   section 5)? Any empty frames? Anything in the bottom 12%?
4. **Flatness list:** each item of `docs/DIRECTOR.md` section 8, found or not.
5. **Sound** (from the mix and the plan): every row of the sound plan present? Two silences? Loudness about −14 LUFS?
6. **Evidence:** every on-screen number matches the ledger and appears only when spoken; labels for our arithmetic and
   illustrations; every `self_claim` line said as "<who> says"; no unspoken or disputed figure visible on a document
   close-up (`agents/COMMON.md` section 11); every `script.json` line labelled "our reading" is not stated as fact.
7. **Packaging:** does the first 30 s pay off the draft title and thumbnail? For a Short: does frame 0 carry the hook,
   with the reveal still held (`COMMON.md` section 10), and does the length meet `docs/SHORTS_RULES.md`?

## Hands over
- `productions/<id>/REVIEW.md`: findings by check, each with timecode, severity (must fix / should fix / question for
  AK) and the suggested fix.
- After the builder's one fix round: a short re-check, then "ready for AK" in `STATUS.md`.

## Never
- Fix the film yourself. Pass a film with a fact error. Claim to have judged feeling or rhythm you could not see.
