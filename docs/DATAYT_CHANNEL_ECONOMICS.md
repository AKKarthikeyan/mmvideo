# Data YT: channel performance and earning potential (Shorts only)

Researched 9 Oct 2026. These are planning estimates, not forecasts. The rate sources are mostly creator blogs with
unpublished methods. Replace them with our own YouTube Analytics numbers once the channel is live.

## How Shorts make money
- **Partner Program (YPP), ad revenue:** needs 1,000 subscribers **and 10M public Shorts views in the last 90 days**,
  or 4,000 long-form watch hours (not available to a Shorts-only channel). Creators get **45%** of the Shorts ad pool,
  split by share of views. Long-form pays 55%.
- **10M in 90 days ≈ 1.1 lakh Shorts views per day.** At 1 Short a day, that means about 1 lakh average views per
  Short, so a few viral hits are needed to unlock ads.
- **Shorts RPM in India (2026, blog estimates):** about **₹5-30 per 1,000 views**. Education and finance sit at the top
  of that range. Long-form is ₹25-150, so Shorts earn roughly 5-10× less per view.
- **Inauthentic content rule (enforced since 15 Jul 2025):** templated, repetitive, mass-produced Shorts can be
  refused monetisation. Our Shorts are template-built, so every Short needs visible original value: a different
  question, original analysis and insight lines, and varied formats. A voiceover in Tamil or English would help most.

## Benchmark
- **India in Pixels** (Indian data visuals, mostly long-form): about 7.7 lakh subscribers, 87 videos, about 4.6 crore
  views (roughly 5 lakh per video). The creator says India-data content stands out because "so little work is done
  condensing information" for India. There is clear demand for India state and data content.

## 12-month scenarios (1 Short a day, about 365 Shorts)

| | Slow | Steady | Breakout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average views per Short | 5,000 | 40,000 | 2,00,000 |
| Views in the year | ~20 lakh | ~1.5 crore | ~7-8 crore |
| Subscribers | 3-8K | 30-80K | 3-5 lakh |
| Ads unlocked (10M / 90 days)? | No | Maybe, months 8-10, if hits come | Yes, by months 3-5 |
| Ad revenue (year 1) | ₹0 | ₹0-2 lakh | ₹10-20 lakh |
| Sponsorships (year 1) | ₹0-50K | ₹2-6 lakh | ₹10-30 lakh |
| **Total (year 1)** | **≈ ₹0-0.5 lakh** | **≈ ₹2-8 lakh** | **≈ ₹20-50 lakh** |

Most new faceless channels end up in Slow. Steady is realistic if the hooks work. Breakout needs repeated viral hits.

## Where the real money is
1. **Sponsorships** pay more than Shorts ads. Indian benchmarks for 10K-100K channels are about ₹5,000-25,000 per basic
   integration and up to about ₹1 lakh in finance or education niches. Natural fits: exam-prep apps (TNPSC/UPSC, since
   NFHS facts are exam GK), health insurance, fintech and edtech.
2. **Tamil-first plus English:** Tamil viewers are less contested and more loyal, and TN or South comparisons get
   strong comments.
3. **Cross-post the same files** to Instagram Reels and Facebook for extra reach and brand-deal leverage, at near-zero cost.
4. **Data products later:** state "fact cards" or posters for coaching centres and teachers.

## Costs
Near zero in cash: our own engine (`mmvideo`), our own code-composed music, public NFHS data. The real cost is time.
With the template, a Short takes about 15-30 minutes once the data is ready.

## Takeaways
- A Shorts-only data channel is a **reach and brand play first and an ad-income play second**. Plan on sponsorships,
  not AdSense.
- Win the first 90 days on **hooks and consistency**, with daily posts and the Map Reveal format.
- Don't let it look like a factory. Vary the questions, formats and voice so the channel stays monetisable.
