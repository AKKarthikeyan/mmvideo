# Data YT: inspiration from Visual Capitalist

Reviewed 9 Oct 2026 from their 60 most recent posts (RSS feed). These are ideas to adapt, not graphics to copy: use our
own data, design and brand.

## What their headlines look like (60 posts)
- **"Ranked: …" 26 (43%)**, **"Mapped: …" 14 (23%)**, plus a few "Visualized: …" and "Charted: …".
- The rest are questions or framings: "Which Indian States File the Most Patents?", "Does Your State Trade More With
  China or the EU?", "Who Controls…", "Where the Richest 0.1% Hold More Wealth Than the Bottom 80%".
- Recurring series: **"… in Every State"** (signature ice-cream flavour, most expensive ZIP code, best employer),
  **then vs now** ("2010 vs 2025", "2006-2026"), **X vs Y** ("Inflation vs Consumer Confidence").
- India content gets made, and Tamil Nadu tops it: "Mapped: Which Indian States File the Most Patents?" (TN #1 with 91,988
  filings, 2010-2026, IP India). It was **sponsored by Adobe**, so brands pay for data maps.

## How each post is built
1. A one-line title with a prefix (Ranked / Mapped).
2. **Key Takeaways**: 3 bullets, each with one sharp comparison ("48% of GDP… more than six times the global
   average"; "the only state where…").
3. **Map + ranked list together** in one graphic.
4. Source always named, plus logo and designer credit, in a consistent frame you recognise in a feed.
5. "Over-indexing": the ice-cream map shows the flavour each state orders *more than the national average*, not
   the most-ordered one. Comparing against the average is what makes it interesting.

## What we take for Data YT Shorts
1. **Title chips:** start every Short with a prefix chip, RANKED / MAPPED / VS / THEN vs NOW / GUESS, so the series is
   recognisable in the feed.
2. **"× India" as the hero number:** "Arunachal: women drink at **21× India's rate**" hits harder than "23.2%".
   Show the multiplier big and the % small.
3. **3 takeaways at the end:** replace the plain end card with 3 punchy one-liners (the #1, a surprise, your state),
   held 3-4 s for screenshots.
4. **Poster final frame:** map + top-5 list + takeaways in one still. It works as a screenshot, a Community post and an
   Instagram post.
5. **New series, "Mapped: What Every Indian State Is #1 At"**: one map, one record label per state. It gets shares
   from every state. Rank-based, so tiny-base indicators don't dominate: raw "× India" picks women's alcohol and child
   overweight almost everywhere. Spin-offs: "Every state's biggest health red flag" and "Every state's best result".
6. **Second-person titles:** "Does your state drink more than Gujarat?", "Is your state heavier than India?"
7. **Then vs now on one screen:** 2019-21 → 2023-24 bars side by side ("Ranked: Where India got heavier since 2019").
8. **Sponsored maps later:** "This map is presented by …" (the Adobe model). Fits the sponsorship plan in
   `DATAYT_CHANNEL_ECONOMICS.md`.
9. **Data beyond NFHS** (VC shows India-state demand): patents by state (IP India), GSDP per person, EV sales, UPI
   use, exam pass rates. Use our own source pulls and our own designs.

## Don't copy
Their graphics, layouts, colours, logo or wording. Their data is often licensed or cited from third parties; always go
to the original public source (NFHS, IP India, MoSPI, RBI) and cite that.
