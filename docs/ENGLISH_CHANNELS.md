# English-language channels in this niche: worldwide and India

Prepared 21 Oct 2026 from vidIQ (channel search for English-only channels, breakout English channels created since June 2025, the top English-titled videos of the last six months, the competitor set of The East Made Simple, keyword markets) and third-party channel trackers. Numbers are vidIQ estimates as of 21 Oct 2026 unless a source is named. Companion to `SPIRITUAL_CHANNEL_RESEARCH.md` (the India view) and `NICHE_GAPS.md`.

---

## 1. The short version

**English is where the money and the diaspora are; India is still most of the search.** For the text-specific terms ("bhagavad gita", "hinduism", "sanatan dharma") India is 40 to 73% of global searches and the United States is the second market at about 12%, with the UK, Canada, Australia and the Gulf a few percent each. For the register terms ("consciousness", "spirituality", "ancient wisdom", "philosophy") the United States, Brazil, Vietnam and Pakistan are as large as India. Anglophone ad rates run 5 to 10 times India's, so a video with a third of its views from the US, UK, Canada and Australia earns several times one watched only in India.

**The English landscape has four tiers and a hole in the middle.** At the top: personalities (Sadhguru 12.7M, Gaur Gopal Das 5.2M, Jay Shetty 4.9M) and institutions (Vedanta Society of New York 401K, ArshaBodha 199K, Chinmaya 1M) that lecture to camera for an hour. Below them, a Western "Eastern philosophy" explainer cohort that is mostly Tao and Zen, growing 30 to 200% a month, with the Indian texts thinly covered. Beside it, a new AI-slideshow cohort doing Indian texts in English (The East Made Simple, Advaita Awakenings, Cosmic Insights, Mystic Sanatan), nearly all AI-labelled. Nobody in English does the Indian texts in a drawn, sourced, School-of-Life register. That is the slot.

**What wins in English is different from what wins in Hindi.** In Hindi the breakouts are devotional and motivational. In English they are non-dual and philosophical: Ashtavakra Gita (three channels at 234K to 548K views), the Upanishads (348K), Ramana (358K), Nisargadatta (209K), Advaita framed as "the most dangerous book about reality" (305K in a week), Swami Sarvapriyananda on consciousness (476K on Closer To Truth). The best English keyword in the whole set is "hinduism explained": 31K searches a month, growing 52%, competition 38.

**Positioning follows.** English original, Sanskrit term in the title, consciousness-and-mind framing rather than devotion, the source-honest version of the "what the text actually says" hook, Hindi as an audio track with a translated title for India, and publishing in the India-evening, America-morning window.

The English cohort's two weaknesses are also the policy exposure: most of it is AI-labelled slideshows, and most titles promise secrets. A drawn, sourced channel with the same curiosity in its titles and none of the "secret" in its claims is the differentiated position, and the one the July 2026 inauthentic-content rules favour.

## 5. The audience and the money

- **Diaspora.** Indian-origin population: United States about 5.2 to 5.4 million, United Kingdom 1.9 million, Canada 1.9 million, Australia 0.85 million, the Gulf over 8 million (Indian government figures as of early 2025, reported by Factly and Dataful). United States Hindus are about 1% of adults and the most educated religious group in the country, 70% with a bachelor's degree (Pew Religious Landscape Study 2023-24).
- **Ad rates.** Every source ranks the Anglophone markets far above India, though the absolute numbers disagree: upGrowth's 2026 estimates put Australia, the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom at $24 to $36 CPM against about $0.70 for India; other sources give lower absolute numbers with the same ordering. Education sits mid-tier globally ($5 to $18). The practical rule from every source: a channel watched mostly in India earns 5 to 10 times less per view than the same channel watched in the United States.
- **The benchmark pair.** Vedanta Society of New York (401K subscribers, American audience) is estimated at $5,001 a month; Chinmaya Channel (1M subscribers, Indian audience) at $872. Same subject, same format, different geography.
- **Growth mechanics.** The English cohort grows on browse, not search: channels with 3K to 6K subscribers are adding 30 to 200% a month and single videos reach 200K to 700K. The title and thumbnail do the work; the Sanskrit term in the title does the search work.

## 6. What this changes for the channel

1. **English original stays the rule, and the title carries the Sanskrit term in English spelling** ("Upanishads explained", "Bhagavad Gita 2.47", "Ashtavakra Gita"), never a Hindi transliteration ("geeta saar") in the English title. The Hindi audio track plus a translated Hindi title and description reach the Hindi search ("bhagwat geeta", 112K Indian searches a month) on the same video.
2. **Lead with the mind, not the deity.** The consciousness and non-dual register is what the English audience clicks: the pilot's chariot, two birds and "a mind that is full and still" are already in that register. Add an Ashtavakra and a Mandukya video early.
3. **Own "hinduism explained" and "every Upanishad explained."** Both are growing, low-competition English searches with no sourced owner. One long video each in the first month, cut into Shorts.
4. **Source-honest curiosity in titles.** Keep the question and the stakes of the breakout titles; replace "secret" and "dangerous" with "what the text actually says." Score two or three title variants with vidIQ before upload and run three thumbnails in Test and Compare.
5. **Publish in the window both audiences share.** Around 8 to 9 pm IST is 10:30 to 11:30 am in New York and 3:30 to 4:30 pm in London; weekend mornings in the United States are India's Saturday and Sunday evenings.
6. **Track the English competitor set in vidIQ**, not the Hindi one: Vedanta Society of New York (benchmark), The East Made Simple, Advaita Awakenings with Gaurav, The Inner Archive, Teachings of the East, Cosmic Insights, God in the Loop, The Eastern Lens, Mystic Sanatan, Let's Talk Religion (ceiling).
7. **The India-based English tier is podcasts and AI slideshows.** An India-based English channel drawn in code with sources has no direct peer; the nearest is The Eastern Lens (biographies, 34K, est. $293 a month).
8. **Sleep readings in English are a real market** (The Inner Archive's 2.5-hour Ashtavakra at 406K, AkashicVedas's 90-minute Vedas). Public-domain translations and the tanpura bed make these nearly free to produce.
