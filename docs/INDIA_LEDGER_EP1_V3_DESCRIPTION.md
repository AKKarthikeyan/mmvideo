# India Ledger · Episode 1 · V3 · YouTube description (draft for AK)

**Title:** The Govt Bet Against Gold and Lost. You're Paying for It.

---

In 2015, the Government of India made a bet against gold. Gold went up five times. Last week, one batch of its gold bonds paid back 4.4 times the money. The extra cost of the bet comes to about ₹690 for every Indian.

This is the story of Amma, who wouldn't hand over her mother's bangle, and Ravi, who bought gold that didn't exist. It's also the story of a reasonable bet that lost.

Amma and Ravi are illustrations, not real people. The voice is AI-generated (MiniMax).
