# The Inner Chariot: content map

What the channel should cover: the important and gripping scenes and teachings of the Indian texts, each with the life question it answers today. Prepared 24 and 25 Oct 2026 by five research passes, one per family of texts, plus a problem-to-verse map for the Gita checked against our own corpus. The series and episode shapes named here are defined in `INNER_CHARIOT_CONTENT_PLAN.md` (section 5) and `INNER_CHARIOT_STYLE_GUIDE.md` (section 3).

**Citations.** **V** means the passage was found at that location in the public-domain translation we would quote. **V-range** means the range was confirmed by its opening lines but not read in full. **UNVERIFIED** means it could not be checked. Critical Edition (CE) numbers for the Mahabharata come from memory, so check them on the BORI tool before any goes on screen. Every quote still goes through the corpus checker and the reviewer before upload.

**Shapes:** Case, Mystery, Dialogue, Journey, Countdown, Two Versions, A Day in the Life (Day), Letter.

### Corrections this research made to popular belief (each is an episode or a Short)
- **No Lakshman Rekha in Valmiki.**
- **Ahalya is not a stone** there, and **Shabari's tasted berries** are a later tradition.
- In Ganguli's Mahabharata, **Draupadi is protected "by Dharma"**; Krishna is not named in the disrobing.
- **Holika is in neither the Bhagavata nor the Vishnu Purana.**
- **The child on the banyan leaf is not in the Markandeya Purana.**
- **Ekalavya is a prince** in the text.
