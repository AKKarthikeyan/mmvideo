## How each title is tagged

| Tag | Meaning | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Curiosity** | A surprise or a myth the text corrects ("Ahalya was never a stone") | Earns the click |
| **Interest** | A gripping story or idea, worth watching for its own sake | Earns the watch time |
| **Engagement** | Asks the viewer something: a riddle, a debate, "have you ever…?" | Earns comments and shares |
| **Demand** | Tied to what people are searching for now: the film, a festival, a trending worry | Earns reach from search and suggestions |
| **Gap** | A topic no one serves well in calm, sourced English | Earns a place nobody else holds |

Each title carries one main tag (listed first) and up to two more. Over the month the long videos are balanced across all five tags. Every Short also carries a hook question in its first two seconds.

- **Script status:** of the 8 long videos, **only L1 has a script** (draft 2, awaiting the voicing choice and the reviewer). L2 to L8 need scripts, quote checks and reviews.
- **Build status:**
  - L1's scenes and figures aren't built yet.
  - L3 reuses L1's Ramayana figures.
  - L5 and L8 reuse the Gita film's kit.
  - L2, L4, L6 and L7 need new scenes: the churning, Murugan's symbols (silhouette rules apply only to Krishna and the Ramayana figures, so Murugan appears as his spear, peacock and rooster), the pillar of light, the chariot board.
- **Rule 5:** Murugan, Shiva and Lakshmi are not drawn as figures. They appear as symbols and light, as the rule now says.
- **Corpus:** add Wilson's Vishnu Purana and Pope's Tirukkural (both public domain) before L2, L6 and the Kural Shorts. Shiva Purana and Kanda Puranam material is paraphrase only.
- **Order if time runs short:**
  1. **Protect first:** L1, L3, L5 and L8, which are evergreen or film-driven.
  2. **Then the festival videos:** L2, L4 and L6 lose most of their value if late, so they become Shorts rather than slipping.
  3. **L7 can move to December** without loss.
- **Shorts are the easy part:** 22 are standalone, one verse or one fact each, and can be batch-produced ahead of time from the corpus.
