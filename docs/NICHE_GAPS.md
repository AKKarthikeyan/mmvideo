# Other YouTube niches for India with demand well ahead of quality supply

Prepared 21 Oct 2026, same session and sources as `SPIRITUAL_CHANNEL_RESEARCH.md`. vidIQ data as of 20 to 21 Oct 2026.

**Method.** Four vidIQ pulls: (1) the top 100 YouTube searches in India; (2) rising searches this week where India is a top market; (3) breakout channels based in India, created in 2026, English and Hindi; (4) the outlier feed: videos over 150K views in the last three months from Indian channels under 20K subscribers, English-titled and Hindi-titled. The fourth is the demand-over-supply signal: when a 1K to 5K subscriber channel gets 500K views, the audience wants the topic more than the supply satisfies.

**Filter.** A niche only makes the list if the engine can serve it (faceless, code-drawn illustration, sourced documents, English original with a Hindi audio track) and it does not depend on copyright, superstition, news, or kids-content rules.

**The macro signal first.** Five explainer brands sit in India's top-100 YouTube searches: Dhruv Rathee (5.0M a month), Ink Explainer (2.8M), Nitish Rajput (1.6M), Think School (1.3M), PJ Explained (1.0M). The explainer is a mainstream genre in India, in Hindi, and a sourced, illustrated explainer brand is a durable asset. That is what every niche below trades on.

## Recommendation

1. Launch the spiritual channel as planned. Its calendar now carries the Ramayana release (5 Nov 2026, per the makers on 11 Oct) and Gita Jayanti (around 20 Dec 2026).
2. Niche 3 (finance rules in Hindi) is better run as a Moat & Margin series than as a new channel: it reuses the clip discipline, the compliance rules and the Hindi-track workflow, and finance carries the highest ad rate.
3. Niche 4 (psychology, sourced) is the spiritual channel's second pillar, not a sibling: the audience overlap is the point.
4. The next standalone channel, once the spiritual one is running, is niche 1 (how things work, in Hindi), with niche 2 (Indian history) as the alternative. Both are evergreen, both are what the engine draws best, and both have the widest gap between what people watch and how well it is made.
