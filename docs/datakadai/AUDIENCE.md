# Data Kadai: what the audience wants

This is a hypothesis built from public research on Indian YouTube viewers (sources at the end). Replace it with the
channel's own numbers as soon as the weekly listening loop (section 4) has four weeks of data.

## 1. What Indian viewers are telling us

| Signal | What it says | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 76 % of Indian Gen Z went to YouTube to understand what was happening around big moments in 2025 (YouTube Culture & Trends, Dec 2025) | People come for **context on the moment**, not only entertainment | Release-day Shorts and "the moment, in numbers" videos within 24 h of news or data |
| India's #1 "What is" search of 2025 was "What is Waqf Bill"; "What is ceasefire" topped meaning searches (Google Year in Search 2025) | News creates "what is X?" demand that peaks fast | A "What is X, in numbers" Short whenever a policy or event dominates search |
| 93 % of YouTube viewers in India prefer Indian-language content (Mindshare-e4m, 2024); 54 % preferred Hindi, 16 % English (Google, 2020) | English-only caps reach | **Hindi audio track on every long video from week 1**, then Tamil and Telugu |
| 77 % of Indian Gen Z watched content translated from another language in 2025 | Dubbed tracks work if the quality is good | Our own voiced tracks, not robotic auto-dub |
| IPL was India's #1 search of 2025; Gemini (AI) was #2 | Cricket and AI are mass-interest topics | "Cricket by numbers" and "AI and your job, in numbers" as recurring themes |
| Over half of connected-TV watch time in India is on videos 21 min or longer (YouTube, 2025) | Long, calm, high-quality data stories fit the TV screen | Monthly 20 to 25 min documentary |
| "Stocks" is India's top investing search category (BrokerChooser, 2025) | Money and markets pull, but advice is risky | Explain the data behind money topics; never tell people what to buy |

## 2. Five audience needs (every video must serve at least one)

| # | Need | Viewer's question | Content pillar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **My pocket** | "How does this change my money, prices, salary, EMI?" | Paisa Data: prices, gold, tax, UPI, inflation |
| 2 | **Explain the moment** | "Everyone is talking about this. What is actually going on?" | Release Day and The Moment in Numbers |
| 3 | **Things I use** | "The brands and apps I use every day: who wins, and why?" | Kadai Wars: brand and app battles |
| 4 | **My state, my India** | "How does my state compare? Where does India stand?" | India Map of the Day and Map Deep Dives |
| 5 | **Can I trust this?** | "Is this number real? Where is it from?" | Built into everything: Trust Stamp, source on screen, Data Receipt, corrections |

Plus two **interest passions** that widen reach: cricket and AI.

## 3. What viewers do not want (avoid)

- Clickbait titles the video does not pay off. Every title promise is answered in the first 60 seconds.
- Investment tips or "buy this" verdicts. We show the data; the viewer decides.
- Old data dressed up as new. The data rules in `DATA_KADAI_PLAN.md` apply.
- Political point-scoring. Policy topics get numbers from both the government and independent sources.
- Slow intros. The number lands in the first 3 seconds.

## 4. Weekly audience listening loop (every Monday, 30 min)

| Where in YouTube Studio | What to record | Decision it drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analytics → Audience → "Content your audience watches" and "Formats your audience watches" | Top 5 other channels and topics | New topic ideas; formats to copy or avoid |
| Analytics → Reach → Traffic source: YouTube search | Top 20 search terms that found us | Titles and topics people already search for |
| Analytics → Research tab (searches across YouTube by our viewers) | Searches with "content gaps" | Next week's release-day and "What is X" Shorts |
| Analytics → Audience → Returning vs new viewers, Engaged Views and retention per video | Which pillar brings people back | Shift the calendar mix toward that pillar |
| Comments (all videos, past 7 days) | Questions asked twice or more; corrections | "You asked" Shorts; pinned corrections |
| Community poll (one a week) | Vote on next week's Kadai Wars battle | Audience picks one video a week |

Log it in a sheet (date, pillar, video, engaged views, retention at 30 s, returning viewers, top 3 comment
questions). After four weeks, rewrite section 2 with the channel's own ranking of needs.
