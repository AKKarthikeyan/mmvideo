shows them (rounded), pulled 11 Oct 2026. Channels were hand-picked, differ in age and subscriber base, and views are
not adjusted for that, so read the table as direction, not proof. Frames were looked at for six of the top videos.

## What the numbers say
1. **Charts shown as video do badly.** The three channels that post animated charts or bar-chart races as Shorts are at
   the bottom: Visual Capitalist (median 2,300), Statista (644), RankingRoyals (469). Visual Capitalist's Short is a
   landscape chart with small labels inside a vertical frame.
2. **Story-led geography does well.** Forceman Big World (3.3M), hoser (339K), Geo Facts (204K), Geography Now (132K)
   and India in Pixels (112K) all lead with a claim or a question, then show one thing at a time.
3. **The formats behind the biggest map and data Shorts:**

| Format | Evidence (views) |
| --- | --- |
| Quiz with clues, one at a time | Geo Facts "Can YOU guess the country from these 5 facts?": 5 of its top 10 (0.7M to 1.6M each) |
| Then vs now, one place per beat | Forceman "Countries Then VS Now" 6.1M |
| What if / unite | Forceman "Neighbor Countries Unite" 4.3M; Geo Facts "What if the USA and Canada united?" 2.5M; Data Duck "What if South India became a country" 234K (its best) |
| "Why does X..." with a point of view | hoser "Why Saudi Arabia's Geography Sucks" 2.4M, "Why Brunei Exists" 2.3M |
| Scale made literal | ibx2cat "This American state has a larger GDP than 193 countries" 1.0M |
| Projection | Geo Facts "These countries will lose the most people by 2050" 1.0M |
| Head to head by category | Dataverse "India vs Russia" 913K (channel median 34K) |
| India, name or origin story | Finology Legal "This is how Indian states got their name" 3.9M |
| India, surprising trend | The Economist "India's surprising baby bust" 2.8M |

4. **What the top videos look like** (six looked at): full-height vertical frame; one object on screen at a time (one
   map shape, one flag, one number); labels of one to four words in very large type; a cut every one to two seconds;
   real imagery (flags, faces, footage) mixed with the graphic; narration throughout. Lengths were 20 to 45 seconds.
   None shows a dense table or a map with a number on every region.

## What it means for our templates
- Our Ranking, Change, Face-off and Mapped templates put 34 to 36 numbers on screen at once. That is closer to the
  low-performing "chart as video" style than to the high-performing one. The all-states map and the full list are
  better as the last beat and as chart posts than as the body of the video.
- Guess the State and Myth-buster are closest to what performs. The clue-by-clue quiz is the best-evidenced format
  we do not have yet.
- New templates worth building, in order of evidence: (1) Guess the state from 5 clues; (2) one state per beat
  (zoom to the state, one big number) for Then vs Now and Ranking; (3) State vs State across five categories;
  (4) "If this state were a country" (needs World Bank data); (5) "Why is X number one?" short explainer.
- This is a benchmark of other channels, not a test of ours. The honest check is to post both styles and compare
  "viewed vs swiped away" and average percentage viewed in YouTube Studio.

## Decision (AK, 11 Oct 2026): keep both styles
- The all-states templates stay (Mapped, Ranking, Change, Face-off, Myth-buster, Breakdown, Guess the State).
- A second family, "beat" Shorts, sits beside them: one thing on screen at a time, very large type, a cut every bar or
  two. Template `src/datayt/BeatStory.tsx`, builder `scripts/datayt/build_beats.py`, one config per story in
  `public/datayt/beats/configs/`. Three types so far:
  - `clues`: guess the state from five clues, then a 3-2-1 and the reveal (sample `clue01`, Kerala)
  - `countdown`: top five, one state per beat, with the all-states map as the closing beat (sample `cnt01`, C-section births)
  - `versus`: state against state over five categories with a running score (sample `vs01`, Tamil Nadu vs Kerala)
- The builder checks every "highest" or "lowest" clue against the data and refuses a tied category.
- Not built: "If this state were a country" (needs World Bank data and a sound way to convert state GDP) and the
  "Why is X number one?" explainer.
- Compare the two families in YouTube Studio on viewed vs swiped away and average percentage viewed.
