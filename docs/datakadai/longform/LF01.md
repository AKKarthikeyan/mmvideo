## How it was made

1. **Sources read at the publisher.** The PIB release (state table, divisions, groups, items, monthly series) and MoSPI's eSankhyiki portal on 14 Oct (`cpi2024_tables.py`); on 15 Oct every all-India item from MoSPI's data service, the RBI's resolution, Governor's statement and August minutes (rbi.org.in, in the browser) and the Consumer Affairs onion release (pib.gov.in, in the browser), written to tables by `scripts/datayt/lf01_sources.py`. Each number typed from a sentence is checked against that sentence when the table is written. Raw reads: `research/2026-10-14/longform/raw/` and `research/2026-10-15/longform/raw/`.
2. **Script as a config** (`public/datayt/long/configs/lf01.json`): every figure on screen or in the voice is a named value read from a table and tested; quotes are checked word for word against the saved text.
3. **Two independent editors** (numbers; viewer and channel rules) read the first draft. Both said "ready after fixes". Fixed: the new series began in January 2025 (only its yearly rates start in 2026); the 2012 food weight is the old basket regrouped into the new divisions, and is labelled so; the earlier repo rate now comes from the August minutes; rounding of exact halves (rice 8.3%, not 8.2%); food with and without drinks told apart; CPI and the index explained; onion events in date order with the RBI's "sharper than the usual seasonal pick-up"; the question is the last line; "provisional" on every chart.
4. **Trimmed** from 8:45 to about 7:20 (estimate): the RBI's food quote and the onion-by-state map came out (MoSPI cautions that state item figures can rest on thin samples), and most scenes lost a line.
5. **Voice, render, frame check.** The first render had three layout faults (a chart label on the zero line, tile captions running out of their box, the two figures of a comparison at different sizes); the template was fixed and the video rendered again.

## Builder and template changes

- `build_long.py`: a rupee prefix for figures ("₹35", said "thirty-five rupees"); halves round up, on screen and in the voice (Python rounds 8.25 to 8.2); numbers said the Indian English way ("one hundred and eight") and without trailing zeros ("five point five", not "five point five zero"); `expect_bottom` for bar charts; a refusal of any tobacco row by its code; the thumbnail's own words (`thumb`).
- `LongStory.tsx`: one figure size across a comparison; mark labels clear of the axis; taller tiles.
- New `LongPosts.tsx` and `scripts/datayt/long_posts.py`: thumbnail, X image, map and list with every state, carousel slides from the video's own frames, and `captions.md` with the chapter times and sources.
