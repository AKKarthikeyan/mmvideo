# Data Kadai: festive money and investment Shorts, Dussehra to New Year 2026

AK, 17 Oct 2026: "Shorts are repeating the same topics retry with more interesting topics with money, investment more related to festivals related."

Five researchers looked for new money topics: markets, funds, borrowing and payments, savings and money rules, and state maps. Every topic LF02, LF03 and fs01–05 already use was ruled out. A separate checker then reopened each figure: 137 confirmed, 22 only inside PDF or Excel files (none opened or downloaded), 6 different from what the researcher wrote, 3 not on an official page. Raw results: `DarwinSSD/DataKadai/research/2026-10-17/holiday/money_result.json`.

These are data stories, not advice. Every market Short says "what happened", shows the bad years too, and has no "should you".
