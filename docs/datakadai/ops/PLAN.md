# Data Kadai: the themed weeks

The rule (AK, 13 Oct 2026): every week has 14 Shorts (two a day); every day keeps to one theme; two of the seven days carry finance data.

This page is the posting order. It replaces the orders in `WEEK-01.md`, `WEEK-02.md` and `WEEK-03.md`, which stay as the record of each build round. Nothing is posted: AK approves each pack and posts it.

## The seven days

| Day | Theme | Finance | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Money | yes | banks, loans, deposits, savings, pensions |
| 2 | Farm and food |  | crops, yields, fish, livestock, land |
| 3 | Women |  | women's work, pay, phones, internet, property |
| 4 | Economy | yes | state income and output, prices, wages, state budgets, exports |
| 5 | Power and connections |  | electricity, renewables, telecom, water |
| 6 | School and health |  | schooling, children's health, hospitals, the body |
| 7 | Know your states |  | a game, a place story, or what a state is known for |
| | Markets | yes | share-market investors, mutual funds, state bonds (only in a week that lists it) |
