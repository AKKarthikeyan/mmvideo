## Why this order

From `docs/datakadai/VIDEO_TEMPLATES_AND_YT_RULES.md` and the script reviews:
- No format runs twice in a row, and the two Shorts of a day are different formats.
- The three paradox Shorts are on days 1, 3 and 6. Two of them share the "same income" idea, so they are three days apart.
- The three then-and-now Shorts have at least four other uploads between them.
- The two race Shorts and the two 100-square Shorts are three days apart. The research allows two 100-square Shorts in 60 days: these are the two.
- Rice appears in three (Telangana, one round of the game, Chhattisgarh): they are on days 1, 3 and 4, never next to each other.
- Bihar 5G is first: it scored highest in the ideas review and every reviewer kept it. Groundwater is late because it is the most sensitive.
