## YouTube Shorts
**Title** (58 characters): In 3 States, More Groundwater Is Taken Out Than Goes Back In

**Description:**
Groundwater taken out in a year, per 100 estimated as extractable in that year (2025 assessment): Punjab 156, Rajasthan 147, Haryana 137. The next highest is Delhi at 92. India as a whole: 61.

In these three states the year's extraction is also above the year's total recharge: Punjab 26.27 against 18.60 billion cubic metres, Rajasthan 17.10 against 12.87, Haryana 12.72 against 10.27. In no other state or UT is that so.

Our sum from the same table: the three have about 9% of India's extractable groundwater and about 23% of the groundwater taken out.

What the number is: annual groundwater extraction for all uses, divided by the annual extractable resource (recharge minus natural discharge), times 100. These are state-wide estimates for one assessment year; areas inside a state differ. It is not a forecast, and the table shows how much, not why.

Earlier assessments, same measure (2017 / 2023 / 2025): Punjab 165.77 / 163.76 / 156.36. Rajasthan 139.88 / 148.77 / 147.11. Haryana 136.91 / 135.74 / 136.75. Estimates are revised each year.

Source: Central Ground Water Board and State Governments, Dynamic Ground Water Resources of India, 2025; state-wise table laid in Lok Sabha by the Ministry of Jal Shakti on 18 December 2025.
