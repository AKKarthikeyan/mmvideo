**Title** (57 characters): Bihar: Lowest Income Per Person, 5G in 96 of 100 Villages

**Description:**
Bihar has the lowest income per person of the 25 states and UTs with 2024-25 data: ₹69,321 a year. Yet 5G services have reached 95.6% of its villages (42,902 of 44,888), against 57.0% for India. Telangana's income per person is over five times Bihar's, and 5G has reached 19.1% of its villages. Across those 25 states, income and 5G reach are only weakly linked (rank correlation 0.19, our calculation).

What the number counts: villages brought under 5G services, as stated by the Department of Telecommunications. It does not measure speed, or how many people use 5G.
