**Title** (58 characters): Andhra Pradesh: More Fish Than Any 3 Other States Combined
Alternative: West Bengal and Andhra Pradesh Were Level on Fish. Then One Took Off

**Description:**
In 2010-11, West Bengal produced 14.4 lakh tonnes of fish and Andhra Pradesh, then including Telangana, 13.7. In 2023-24 (provisional), Andhra Pradesh produced 51.6 and West Bengal 22.0. Andhra Pradesh's figure is more than any three other states combined: West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka together come to 44.9.

West Bengal did not shrink. Its output is 1.5 times its 2010-11 level. Andhra Pradesh's is 3.8 times, even though Telangana has been counted separately since 2014-15. The table shows how much, not why.

What the numbers count: fish produced, from sea and inland waters together, not fish eaten. The 2023-24 figures are provisional and may be revised. One lakh is 100,000, so 51.6 lakh tonnes is about 5.2 million tonnes.

Source: Ministry of Fisheries, Handbook on Fisheries Statistics, as published in the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, Table 93.
