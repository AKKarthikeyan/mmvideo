## YouTube Shorts
**Title** (51 characters): Uttar Pradesh Grows Nearly Half of India's Sugarcane
Alternative: One State Grows Nearly Half of India's Sugarcane

**Description:**
Uttar Pradesh grew 220.8 million tonnes of sugarcane in 2024-25: 48.6% of India's 454.6 million tonnes. Maharashtra grew 24.2% and Karnataka 10.6%; every other state together, 16.7%.

Is that just because Uttar Pradesh is big? Its share of India's foodgrains is 17.8% and of milk 16.2%. In sugarcane its share is nearly three times that.

The reason is land, not yield. Uttar Pradesh has 49.9% of India's sugarcane area (2,720 of 5,450 thousand hectares). Its yield, 81.2 tonnes per hectare, is just under India's average of 83.4.

What the numbers count: tonnes grown, not output per person or per hectare. Figures for 2024-25 are as published in the RBI Handbook; we could not confirm whether they are final or advance estimates.

Sources
Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare, as published in the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States: sugarcane production (Table 65), area (Table 78), yield (Table 87) and total foodgrains (Table 62), 2024-25.
Milk: Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics (Table 91 of the same handbook), 2023-24.
