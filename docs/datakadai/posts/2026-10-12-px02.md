**Description:**
Kerala and Maharashtra had about the same state output per person in 2023-24 (₹2,81,269 and ₹2,78,681, current prices). Yet Kerala's average daily wage rate for men in rural construction was over double Maharashtra's: ₹894 against ₹390.
Across the 20 states and UTs with wage data, income and this wage tend to rise together, and Kerala sits far above the rest: 1.6 times the next highest, Jammu and Kashmir.
What the numbers count: a wage rate for one trade, men only, from the Labour Bureau's sample of villages. It is not income and not all jobs. The pooled average of the 20 is not a national wage. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Labour Bureau, Wage Rates in Rural India, and state domestic product, as published in the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, Tables 112 and 19 (2023-24).
