**Description:**
Telangana's share of India's rice production rose from 4.2% in 2014-15 (10th among the 31 states and UTs in the table) to 11.6% in 2024-25: 44 to 174 lakh tonnes. It has been in the top three in each of the last three years.
Of the extra rice India produced over those ten years, more than a quarter came from Telangana. 2014-15 was a low year for the state: it produced 58 lakh tonnes in 2013-14. A lower share on the map can come with more tonnes: West Bengal, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh and Punjab all produced more rice in 2024-25 than in 2014-15.
What the numbers count: rice produced, not rice eaten; state totals, not per person. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare (2024-25 final estimates), as published in the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, Table 58.
