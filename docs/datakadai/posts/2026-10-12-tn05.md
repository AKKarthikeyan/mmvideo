**Description:**
Chhattisgarh's rice yield rose from 1,660 kg a hectare in 2014-15 to 2,655 in 2024-25, up 60%, while India's rose 22.5%. Among the 31 states and UTs with a figure, it moved from 29th to 14th.
Its gap to India's average narrowed from 31% to 9%. Single years swing: the last three years were 2,602, 2,539 and 2,655 kg.
Yield is rice output per hectare of rice area, not the total harvest. The 31 include a few places with very little rice. The table shows how much, not why.
