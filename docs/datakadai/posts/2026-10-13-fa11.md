**Title** (60 characters): Maharashtra: Loans Approved ₹98, Used ₹86 per ₹100 Deposited

**Description:**
At end-March 2025, banks in Maharashtra had approved loans worth ₹98 for every ₹100 of deposits held in the state, while loans used inside the state came to ₹86. The gap, ₹12.4, was the biggest of 35 states and Union territories; Delhi's was 3.7.
A loan is counted once where it is approved (place of sanction) and once where it is used (place of utilisation). Maharashtra's gap was also the biggest in 2023 and 2024. Scheduled commercial banks, loans outstanding; a deposit is counted where the bank office is. A gap, not a verdict on lending.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25 (December 2025), Tables 153 and 154: credit-deposit ratio by place of sanction (where a loan is approved) and by place of utilisation (where it is used), scheduled commercial banks, loans outstanding at end-March 2025. Deposits are counted where the bank office is. Gap = approved minus used, in rupees per ₹100 of deposits (points).
