In the 2023-24 family health survey, 20.7% of people in Kerala's surveyed households were aged 60 or over and 20.3% were under 15: level. In India as a whole, 12.9% were 60 or over and 25.5% under 15, about two children for every older person.
Kerala's share aged 60 and over was the highest of 34 ranked states and Union territories (Goa next, 17.2%). In the earlier survey, 2019-21, Kerala had 18.6% aged 60 and over against 20.6% under 15. Survey estimates, not a census count.
