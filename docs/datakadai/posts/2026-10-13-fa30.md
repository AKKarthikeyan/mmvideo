**Description:**
At end-March 2025, regional rural banks held 33.4% of all bank deposits in Mizoram, the highest of 29 states and UTs with a figure; India's share was 2.9%. Regional rural banks are banks set up to serve rural areas. They were also 44.8% of Mizoram's bank offices (India: 13.4%).
Mizoram's share has been the highest in each of the seven years from end-March 2019 to 2025; at end-March 2018 Tripura's was higher, 27.0% against Mizoram's 25.4%. Scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks among them. A deposit is counted where the bank office is. State totals as shares, not per person.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25 (December 2025), Tables 160, 155, 163 and 152: deposits of regional rural banks, deposits of all scheduled commercial banks (regional rural banks are among them), and bank offices, as at end-March 2018 to 2025.
