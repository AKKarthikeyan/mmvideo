**Title** (59 characters): Himachal: 26.5% of India's Large-Hydro Electricity, 2024-25

**Description:**
In FY 2024-25 Himachal Pradesh generated 39,386 million units of large-hydro electricity, 26.5% of India's 1,48,634 million units and the highest of the states. Jammu and Kashmir had 10.2%, Uttarakhand 10.2% and Karnataka 9.5%; Madhya Pradesh followed with 5.1%.
Of all the electricity generated in India, Himachal made 2.3%: 42,534 of 18,24,214 million units, and 93% of its own was large hydro. Its share of India's large hydro was between 24% and 27% in each of the six years from 2020-21 to 2025-26. Large hydro here means plants over 25 MW. It is electricity generated in the state, not who owns or uses it, and not capacity. State totals, not per person.
