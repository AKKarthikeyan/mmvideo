On 31 August 2026 there were 24.7 NSE-registered investors per 100 projected population in Delhi (India: 9.5), the highest of the 25 states and UTs NSE lists. Chandigarh (20.6) and Goa (18.0) came next. On 31 March 2020 Delhi's figure was 9.2 and India's 2.3.
A registered investor is a PAN registered with NSE, traded or not; it is not only individuals, and the state is set by PIN code, not by who lives there. So this is not the share of Delhi's residents who invest. Delhi's rate was 4.0 times India's in March 2020 and 2.6 times in August 2026.
