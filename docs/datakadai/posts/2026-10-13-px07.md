**Title** (60 characters): Share of Women at Work: Tamil Nadu 1.8x Haryana, Same Income

**Description:**
Tamil Nadu and Haryana had level income per person in 2023-24 (₹3,13,329 and ₹3,19,363). Yet 41.5% of women aged 15 and over in Tamil Nadu were working, against 23.6% in Haryana: 18 points apart, or 1.8 times. India's figure was 40.3%. The gap was 2.2 times in 2021-22 and 2.0 times in 2022-23.
Workers include unpaid helpers in a household enterprise. Survey estimates (usual status). Income here means state output per person, not what residents earn. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Women at work: worker population ratio, women aged 15 years and above, usual status (principal and subsidiary), rural and urban areas together, July 2023 to June 2024 (shown as 2023-24), Periodic Labour Force Survey, National Statistics Office, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, read through the Ministry's data API (36 states and UTs; all-India 40.3%). Workers include unpaid helpers in a family farm or business. Income: per-capita net state domestic product, current prices, 2023-24, Reserve Bank of India, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States, Table 19 (33 states and UTs with data; National Statistical Office series).
