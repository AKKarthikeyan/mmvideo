**Title** (54 characters): Delhi: Women Hold 57% of Mudra Loans, 19% of the Money

**Description:**
Under the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana, from 8 April 2015 to 26 June 2026, women entrepreneurs held 56.9% of the loan accounts sanctioned in Delhi but 19.5% of the money sanctioned there. The gap between the two shares, 37.4 points, was the widest of any state or UT; India's was 24.1 points (66.2% of accounts, 42.1% of money).
Five states had gaps over 27 points: Delhi, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Maharashtra and Gujarat. These are cumulative loans sanctioned, as uploaded by lending institutions to the Mudra portal. A loan account is not a person. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is blank on the map (its gap was 23.0). The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Ministry of Finance, Lok Sabha unstarred question 2407, answered 3 Aug 2026, Annexure I: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana loan accounts and amounts sanctioned, cumulative, 8 Apr 2015 to 26 Jun 2026, in total and for women entrepreneurs, by state, as uploaded by lending institutions to the Mudra portal. Shares are women's accounts and amounts as a share of each state's total. A loan account is not a person. India is the answer's total row.
