**Title** (56 characters): Odisha: Women 15+ Usually at Work, 18% to 48% in 6 Years

**Description:**
In Odisha, 18.3% of women aged 15 and over were usually at work in 2017-18 and 48.0% in 2023-24. India's figure went from 22.0% to 40.3%, so Odisha moved from 3.7 points below India to 7.7 points above. In rural Odisha the 2023-24 figure was 51.5%, in urban Odisha 27.8%.
Usual status counts paid or unpaid work, including helping in a household enterprise. Survey estimates from the Periodic Labour Force Survey's first and last July-to-June annual rounds; the 2025 calendar-year round uses a new design and is not strictly comparable.

Source: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (National Statistics Office), Periodic Labour Force Survey, annual reports 2017-18 and 2023-24 (July to June; the first and the last of that series): worker population ratio, usual status (principal and subsidiary), women aged 15 years and above, rural and urban together. The ratio is the share of all women of that age who were working. Survey estimates.
