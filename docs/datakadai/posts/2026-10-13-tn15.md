**Title** (59 characters): Telangana: Personal Loans 19% to 42% of Bank Loans, 2015-25

**Description:**
Personal loans were 19.4% of the bank loans used in Telangana at end-March 2015 and 41.8% at end-March 2025: up 22.4 points, or 2.2 times the share. India's share went from 16.6% to 31.0%, so Telangana's rise in points was 1.6 times India's.
Personal loans are loans to individuals, such as for homes and vehicles. Scheduled commercial banks, amounts outstanding. A bank merger in July 2023 lifts the figures from March 2024. Shares, not rupee amounts, are compared across the years.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25 (December 2025): Tables 159 (personal loans), 155 (deposits) and 154 (credit-deposit ratio, place of utilisation), scheduled commercial banks, amounts outstanding at end-March 2015 and 2025. Share = personal loans ÷ credit used in the state (deposits × ratio). India: the all-India rows. Telangana is in the tables from 2015. A bank merger in July 2023 lifts figures from 2024. Not adjusted for prices.
