**Description:**
For every ₹100 of deposits held in Assam's banks, ₹37 was lent as loans used in the state at end-March 2015 and ₹66 at end-March 2025. India's figure went from ₹77 to ₹80, so Assam moved from under half of India's ratio to 82% of it.
The 2014 figure was ₹40, so 2015 was a low start; the ratio then rose in each of the last five years, from ₹44 in 2020. Scheduled commercial banks, by place of utilisation; a deposit is counted where the bank office is. A ratio, not a verdict on lending.
