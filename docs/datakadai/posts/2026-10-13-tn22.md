**Description:**
In rural Karnataka, a man working as a farm labourer earned ₹205 a day in 2014-15 against ₹286 for a construction worker: 72% as much. In 2024-25 it was ₹454 against ₹485: 94%. The 22-point gain was 2.6 times the all-India gain of 8.6 points.
The ratio rose in nine of the ten years and was level in 2020-21. Karnataka and Kerala (+21.2 points) gained most of the 19 states with both wages.
