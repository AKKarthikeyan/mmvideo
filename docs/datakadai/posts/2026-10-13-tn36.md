**Title** (60 characters): Odisha: Women's Share of Atal Pension Enrolments, 36% to 67%

**Description:**
Women were 36.1% of Odisha's Atal Pension Yojana enrolments in 2015-16 (39,987 of 110,846 women and men) and 67.2% in 2024-25 (307,791 of 458,317), a rise of 31.1 points. Women's enrolments multiplied 7.7 times in nine years; men's, 2.1 times. Women were more than half of Odisha's enrolees in each of the last four years.
In 2024-25 fewer men enrolled than the year before, 187,494 down to 150,526, a fall of 20%; women's enrolments also rose, 265,290 to 307,791, so both moved the share. A Lok Sabha answer gives 66.5% for Odisha in 2024-25 (India 54.6%) and 63.4% for 2025-26 to 31 January. The map is blank for 18 states and UTs where the Handbook's counts differ from that answer or the boundary changed; they are named in the source. Enrolments in a year; transgender enrolments are left out of the share.

Source: PFRDA Handbook of NPS Statistics 2024, via RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25, Table 180: Atal Pension Yojana enrolments in the year; share = women ÷ (women + men), transgender left out. Also Lok Sabha answer 2656, 9 Mar 2026.
