**Title** (54 characters): Tamil Nadu's Share of India's FDI Equity: 3.9% to 8.0%

**Description:**
Tamil Nadu's share of India's FDI equity inflow (foreign direct investment in company shares, as reported by state) was 3.9% in 2020-21 and 8.0% in 2025-26, about twice the share. In dollars, Tamil Nadu's inflow went from US$2,323 million to US$4,724 million while India's total was flat: US$59.6 bn, then US$58.8 bn.
The share was up in four of the five years after 2020-21 (5.1%, 4.7%, 5.5%, 7.4%). The six months before, October 2019 to March 2020, were 4.2%, so 2020-21 was a low start; from 4.2% the rise is still 3.8 points. Counted in the state where the inflow is reported, not necessarily where it is spent. Delhi is not listed separately. Dollars are not adjusted for prices or exchange rates, and recent years are provisional. A lower share can come with more dollars. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Ministry of Commerce and Industry (DPIIT), Lok Sabha unstarred question 328, answered 21 Jul 2026, Annexures III and IV: each state's share of India's FDI equity inflow, and the inflow in US$ million, by financial year, 2020-21 to 2025-26. Counted in the state where the inflow is reported, not necessarily where it is spent. Delhi is not listed separately (UTs and others). Recent years are provisional. The 4.2% is from DPIIT's FDI fact sheet, March 2020, Annexure C.
