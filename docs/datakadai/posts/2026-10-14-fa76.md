**Title** (56 characters): Madhya Pradesh: 21 of Every 100 Specified Dams, Apr 2025

**Description:**
Madhya Pradesh had 1,370 of India's 6,628 specified dams in the National Register of Specified Dams, April 2025: 21 of every 100 (20.7%), on 9.4% of India's land. The count includes dams completed and under construction.
Maharashtra had 2,696 (40.7%), about twice Madhya Pradesh's count, on the same 9.4% of India's land; Gujarat had 525 (7.9%). Madhya Pradesh's dams held 14.5% of India's live storage (265.5 billion cubic metres in all) and Maharashtra's 14.2%, about the same, so the average Madhya Pradesh dam held 28 million cubic metres of live water against 14 in Maharashtra, twice as much. These are state totals of dams, not of water and not per area, and the average is a mean of register totals. A specified dam is over 15 m high, or 10 to 15 m with size tests (Dam Safety Act, 2021). A Lok Sabha reply of 6 August 2026, counting completed and operational dams, again gives Madhya Pradesh 1,370, still 21 of every 100. The register is compiled from state returns and counts differ by a few dams between sources. The map leaves out Chandigarh, Delhi, Puducherry, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu and Lakshadweep (no row). The table shows how much, not why.

Source: National Dam Safety Authority, Ministry of Jal Shakti: National Register of Specified Dams, April 2025 (Central Water Commission site), state summary, page iv: 6,628 dams and 265.5 billion cubic metres of live storage, completed and under construction.
