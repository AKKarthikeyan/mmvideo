In Census 2011, 741,249 of Goa's 1,458,545 people (50.8%) said they knew three or more languages: their mother tongue plus two more. India's share was 7.1%. Goa's was the highest of the 35 states and UTs, 7.2 times India's.
Chandigarh (a UT) came next at 30.5%, then Arunachal Pradesh (30.2%) and Sikkim (29.7%); Goa led by over 20 points. In Census 2001 Goa was also the highest, at 47.8%, with Chandigarh next, near 38%. Shares are our division of Census Table C-17 by each place's population (Table A-01), and language knowledge is what people said they knew. Telangana and Ladakh sit inside Andhra Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir as counted in 2011, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu were two rows then, so the map leaves them blank. The table shows how much, not why.
