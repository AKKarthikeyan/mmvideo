**Title** (58 characters): Rajasthan Women: 52% in Labour Force, 21% of NSE Investors

**Description:**
In 2025 (January to December), 52% of women aged 15 and over in Rajasthan were in the labour force, meaning working (unpaid family work included) or seeking work; Maharashtra's rate was 40% and India's 40%. On 31 August 2026, women were 21% of Rajasthan's NSE-registered investors whose gender is disclosed; Maharashtra's share was 29% and India's 25%.
In March 2024 the NSE shares were 19% in Rajasthan and 26% in Maharashtra, and Maharashtra's was 1.4 times Rajasthan's. In 2023-24 (July to June) the labour force rates were 50.9% in Rajasthan and 40.1% in Maharashtra. These are a rate and a share, not counts. A state is set by PIN code, not by where investors live; the labour force rate counts work of any kind, unpaid included, and the NSE share counts registered investor accounts, so the two measure different things. The 2025 survey round is a new design. Across the 34 states and UTs in both tables, the two were not closely linked. The map shows the NSE share and leaves out Ladakh and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (not listed). The table shows how much, not why.

Women among investors: share of NSE unique registered investors with a disclosed gender, by state (PIN code), end-March 2024 and 31 Aug 2026, NSE Market Pulse, Sept 2026, Table 76.
