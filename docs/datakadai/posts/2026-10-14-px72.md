**Title** (56 characters): Telangana: 3× Kerala's Registered Co-ops, 7% More People

**Description:**
On 1 March 2026 the National Cooperative Database listed 60,858 registered cooperative societies in Telangana and 19,652 in Kerala (all sectors, functional or not): 3.1 times as many, so Kerala has about a third as many. Telangana had 38.6 million projected people and Kerala 36.2 million, so Telangana has 7% more.
Per lakh projected people, Telangana had 157.5 societies and Kerala 54.3 (1 lakh = 100,000). The multiple was at least 3 in each of three counts: 3.3 times in the Lok Sabha reply of 18 March 2025, 3.1 in the reply of 9 December 2025 and 3.1 in the reply of 10 March 2026. This counts registered societies, not members or money, and the counts change with each update. The population figures are projections from Census 2011, not a count; the map's seven north-eastern states other than Assam share one population projection. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Ministry of Cooperation, Lok Sabha replies No. 2939 (10 Mar 2026), No. 1500 (9 Dec 2025) and No. 2866 (18 Mar 2025): registered cooperative societies by State/UT, National Cooperative Database (all sectors, functional or not; 2026 table as of 1 Mar 2026). People: National Commission on Population, Projections 2011-2036 (July 2020 edition), 1 Mar 2026. Counts change with each update.
