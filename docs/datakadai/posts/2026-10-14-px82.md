In the All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) 2023-24, Rajasthan had 4,344 colleges and 93 universities, 47 colleges for each university; Gujarat had 2,521 colleges and 107 universities, 24 for each (India: 37). Rajasthan's ratio was about twice Gujarat's.
Gujarat had 15% more universities than Rajasthan, and Rajasthan had 72% more colleges than Gujarat, on 13% more projected population (1 March 2025). In 2022-23 the ratio was 43 in Rajasthan and 24 in Gujarat; Gujarat had 102 universities against Rajasthan's 91, and Rajasthan had 3,889 colleges against Gujarat's 2,470. These are institutions registered with the survey, state totals, not per person, and the ratio is of two counts, not a count of affiliations. Across the 33 states and UTs with both tables, universities per million projected people and colleges per lakh people aged 18-23 were not linked (rank correlation -0.03).
