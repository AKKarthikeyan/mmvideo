**Title** (58 characters): Bank Deposits in Haryana: 2.5% to 3.8% of India's, 2015-25

**Description:**
Banks in Haryana held 2.5% of India's scheduled commercial bank deposits at end-March 2015 and 3.8% at end-March 2025, a rise of 1.3 points; the 2025 share is 1.5 times the 2015 share. A deposit is counted where the bank office is, not where the saver lives.
Share is the state's deposits over the all-India total. Haryana's share of India's projected population (1 March) was 2.1% in 2015 and 2.2% in 2025, so its deposits per person (deposit share divided by population share) went from 1.2 times India's to 1.7 times. The share rose in nine of the ten yearly steps; the one dip, in 2024, was under 0.1 point. Karnataka's share rose 0.9 points, from 7.1% to 8.1%. A share can fall while deposits grow. The map leaves out Jammu and Kashmir (boundary changed in 2019), Ladakh (no 2015 figure) and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25 (December 2025), Table 155: deposits of scheduled commercial banks, as at end-March 2015 and 2025.
