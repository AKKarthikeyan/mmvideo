**Title** (60 characters): Odisha: Men's Rural Construction Wage, 72% to 89% of India's

**Description:**
Construction workers (men) in rural Odisha were paid an average of ₹199 a day in 2014-15, 72% of the all-India average of ₹275, and ₹393 a day in 2024-25, 89% of India's ₹441. Odisha gained 17 points on India, the second-biggest gain of the 19 states with both years, after Andhra Pradesh (+39); Tripura gained 13.
The wage is compared with India's average in the same year, in current rupees, not adjusted for prices. Odisha's wage as a share of India's was up in 8 of the 10 yearly steps, level in 1 (2018-19) and down 0.4 points in 1 (2022-23); it was 76% in 2016-17, 81% in 2019-20, 84% in 2022-23 and 85% in 2023-24. For rural men who are non-agricultural labourers, Odisha's wage as a share of India's went from 71.9% in 2014-15 to 90.8% in 2024-25. India's figure is the average of all quotations; 2020-21 state figures cover ten months, and recent years can be revised. The map leaves out Jammu and Kashmir (boundary changed in 2019) and states with no row; Andhra Pradesh has no separate Telangana row. The table shows how much, not why.

Source: Labour Bureau (Ministry of Labour and Employment), Indian Labour Journal, as published in the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2024-25 (11 Dec 2025), Table 112: average daily wage rates in rural areas, men, construction workers, current rupees. India: the average of all quotations. 2020-21 state figures cover ten months. Compared within each year, not adjusted for prices. Recent years can be revised.
