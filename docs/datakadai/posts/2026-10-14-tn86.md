**Title** (58 characters): West Bengal's Coastline: 157.5 km Old, 721 km Recalculated

**Description:**
In the 2025 recalculation of India's coastline, West Bengal's came to 721 km (721.02 km), against 157.5 km in the old official count: 4.6 times the old figure. That is the biggest multiple of the 12 states and UTs with both lengths; Gujarat's is next at 1.9 times.
After the recalculation, West Bengal's coast is 120.1% of Kerala's (721.02 km against 600.15 km); in the old count it was 27.6% of Kerala's (157.5 km against 569.7 km). India's total went from 7,516.6 km to 11,098.81 km, a 48% difference. These are two measurements, not two dates. The official note gives one general reason for every state, 'probable variation in scale and calculation methods', and none for West Bengal alone. The map leaves out states and UTs with no sea coast, and Daman and Diu, which has no old figure. The table shows how much, not why.
