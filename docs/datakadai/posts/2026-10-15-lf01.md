**Description:**
India's consumer prices rose 5.54% in the year to September 2026, up from 4.83% in August: the highest of the nine months the new index (base 2024) has measured so far. September's figures are provisional. Food prices rose 7.74%. Onion cost 108% more than a year before; in January it had cost 29.3% less.

Every figure was read at the publisher: MoSPI's release on PIB and its eSankhyiki data portal, the RBI's statements of 7 October and the Consumer Affairs ministry's onion release of 26 August. A reason is given only where an official source states one, and it is quoted. September's figures will be revised with October's release on 12 November.
