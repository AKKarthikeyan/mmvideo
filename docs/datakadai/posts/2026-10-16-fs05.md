**Title** (55 characters): Ramlila, Durga Puja, Garba, Deepavali: on UNESCO's List

**Description:**
Four of India's festival-season traditions are on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity: Ramlila, the traditional performance of the Ramayana (inscribed 2008; originally proclaimed in 2005), Durga Puja in Kolkata (2021), Garba of Gujarat (2023) and Deepavali (2025).
In UNESCO's words, Ramlila is 'performed across northern India during the festival of Dussehra'; Durga Puja is 'an annual festival celebrated in September or October, most notably in Kolkata, in West Bengal'; Garba is 'performed on the occasion of the Hindu festival of Navaratri'; and Deepavali 'falls on the new moon in October or November and lasts several days'. Deepavali was inscribed at the committee's meeting in New Delhi, 8 to 12 December 2025. All facts from UNESCO's own pages on ich.unesco.org, read 16 Oct 2026.

Source: UNESCO, Intangible Cultural Heritage, Representative List: Ramlila, the traditional performance of the Ramayana (inscribed 2008 at 3.COM, Istanbul, 4 to 8 November 2008; originally proclaimed 2005); Durga Puja in Kolkata (inscribed 2021 at 16.COM, held online, 13 to 18 December 2021); Garba of Gujarat (inscribed 2023 at 18.COM, Kasane, Botswana, 5 to 8 December 2023). Deepavali (inscribed 2025 at 20.COM, New Delhi, 8 to 12 December 2025). Element and session pages on ich.unesco.org, read 16 Oct 2026.
