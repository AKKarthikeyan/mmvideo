**Description:**
The RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund held ₹1,12,564 crore (₹1.13 lakh crore) on 31 March 2026, up from ₹97,545 crore a year before (RBI Annual Report 2025-26). Balances in bank accounts not operated for 10 years or more move to this fund, and the money still belongs to the customer, with no time limit to claim it.
By 28 February 2026, ₹5,777 crore had been returned on 22.95 lakh claims across banks, insurers and mutual funds (Finance Ministry, PIB). Unclaimed insurance with insurers: ₹8,974 crore; in mutual funds: ₹3,749 crore. The RBI's UDGAM portal searches unclaimed deposits across many banks at once.
