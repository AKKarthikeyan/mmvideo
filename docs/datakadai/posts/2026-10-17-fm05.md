**Title** (48 characters): Diwali Gifts and Tax: The ₹50,000 Rule (2026-27)
Alternative: Diwali Gifts: When Tax Applies Under the New Income-tax Act

**Description:**
Money gifts from non-relatives, friends included, are taxable in full once they total more than ₹50,000 in a tax year: the whole sum, not the excess (Income-tax Act, 2025; the department's own example taxes all ₹53,000 of two gifts). A gifted TV is not counted. Gifts from relatives, and money received on your own marriage, are not taxed; Diwali is no exemption.
From an employer, gifts or vouchers below ₹15,000 in a tax year are valued at nil (Income-tax Rules, 2026; an older department page still shows ₹5,000 for gifts in kind). Receiving ₹2 lakh or more from one person in a day, or for one occasion, is allowed only through a bank, and a penalty can equal the sum. These are the department's rules as printed, not advice on anyone's tax.

Source: Income Tax Department (incometaxindia.gov.in), read 17 Oct 2026: 'Tax treatment of Gifts Received by an Individual or HUF' (Income-tax Act, 2025, as amended by the Finance Act, 2026); Income-tax Act, 2025, sections 92, 186 and 451; Income-tax Rules, 2026, rule 15(5)(a), Table IV, item 4; threshold-limits page.
