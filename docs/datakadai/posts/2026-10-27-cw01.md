## YouTube Shorts
**Title** (41 characters): The ₹10 Cola War: Campa vs Coke and Pepsi
Alternative: Campa Doubled Its Share. Coke and Pepsi Fell to 85%

**Description:**
Coca-Cola and PepsiCo held 93% of India's soft drinks market in January to September 2024. In the same months of 2025 they held 85%, while Campa and Lahori Zeera together went from about 7% to almost 15% (NielsenIQ data cited by industry sources, reported 25 Nov 2025).
Reliance bought the Campa brand in August 2022 and brought it back in March 2023 at ₹10 for 200 ml. Coke and Pepsi answered with their own ₹10 packs, down from ₹12 and above. Shares are of all soft drinks, not cola alone.

Source: NielsenIQ data via industry sources, Storyboard18, 25 Nov 2025; GlobalData on the Campa relaunch. Read 25 Oct 2026.

Made by Data Kadai: data analysed and animated from the official source above. Narration is an AI voice.
