# DRAFT — BM01 long-form script v1: "Who Controls the Gate to the Red Sea?" (Bab el-Mandeb)

Indica Atlas · long-form · target 15–17 min · written 15 Nov 2026 · SCRIPT ONLY (no build, voice or render).
Fact source: `ledger.md` in this folder (CONFIRMED rows; CORRECTED rows in their corrected form only) plus cleared extras X1–X3. `[G]` = plain map geography. `[frame]` = framing line with no claim.
RE-CHECK before voicing: the territorial position on the coast, any new ship attacks, and any newer EIA STEO quarter.

## Format

**F8 Then and Now** (main), run on the Indica Atlas scale ladder (map → region → place → people/India → map). Why: the research's strongest signal is the same measure at several dates (EIA oil through the gate: 9.3 in 2023 → 4.1 in 2024; 5.4 in 4Q25 → 8.1 in 2Q26; Suez revenue 2023 → 2024), and the turn — the gate ships fled became the main road — is a then-and-now reversal, not a single puzzle; the title question stays open as the frame.

Number rules used throughout: annual EIA figures (H5) and quarterly EIA STEO figures (X1, H2) are never put in one comparison; every barrels figure names EIA and the year or quarter. Territorial claims are "reported" or attributed.
