# Indica Atlas: content refresh research (25 Oct 2026)

**Question (AK):** what kinds of geography and map videos do audiences want, and which videos reach 1M views on the big channels?

**Method:** I read YouTube's public pages for 21 big geography, map and history channels and took each one's "Popular"
list: the top 30 long videos and the top 48 Shorts. I also ran 22 YouTube searches sorted by views. View counts are
YouTube's own rounded lifetime numbers, read on 25 Oct 2026. Raw data and the script are in `data_2026-10-25/`.
vidIQ was not used: the account had no credits left.

## 3. Title patterns from the 1M+ list
1. **"Why … (It's Not the X)"**: the answer you expect is ruled out in the title (Map Pack: Alaska, Greenland, South America).
2. **"WAY bigger / further / deeper than you think"**: the viewer's own mental map is the target.
3. **"Why (almost) nobody lives in …"** plus a big area word: HUGE, empty, belt.
4. **"…is with… X!?"**: the surprise answer is in the title.
5. **"X explained in 60 seconds"** for a region.
6. Short and plain: most 1M+ Short titles are under 60 characters, with at most one emoji. Ours have two emojis plus a long
   explainer tail, e.g. *"…🛢️🚢 Strait of Malacca"*.

## 5. Limits of this research
- View counts are lifetime totals. Many 10M+ long videos are 5–10 years old. The best "now" signal is format A, which has
  new uploads reaching millions within weeks.
- The Shorts lists carry no upload dates, so recency is unknown for Shorts.
- The data comes from 21 channels and 22 searches, not the whole of YouTube. Atlas Pro, Map Men and Tom Scott could not
  be read: their handles were wrong or the page format differed.
