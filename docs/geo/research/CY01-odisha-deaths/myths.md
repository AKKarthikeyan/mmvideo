# CY01 Odisha cyclone deaths: the popular version, checked

The claims come from AK's working title and from news snippets seen in web-search results (Down To Earth, Al Jazeera, Reuters via Gulf Times, Hindustan Times, The Quint). Those pages were not used as evidence; nothing here enters the script except through claims.jsonl.

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | The 1999 super cyclone killed **about 10,000** people | Confirmed | C002 | Official counts 9,885-9,893 (IMD's Feb 2000 report: 9,887, plus 40 missing). "Nearly 10,000" is fair |
| M2 | Later cyclones killed **under 100** | Confirmed | C015, C016, C023, C031, C034, C037 | Every later Odisha count is under 100: Phailin 21 (72 with the floods after), Fani 64, Titli 72-77, Yaas 2-3, Dana 0. But see M3 |
| M3 | The later storms were **just as strong**, so the drop is all preparation | Embellished | C004, C014, C021, C028, C005, C058 | Not like for like, but the gap depends on the measure. Pressure at landfall: 1999 912 hPa; Phailin 940; Fani 952 (03 UTC). Surge: 1999 5-6 m; Phailin 2-3.5 m; Fani about 1.5 m. Wind: IMD's own reports disagree on 1999 (140 kt in 2000, 120 kt in 2024, K15), so Phailin's 115 kt is almost level. Say 'weaker' only for pressure and surge |
| M4 | In 1999 there was **no warning** | Contradicted | C010 | Verdict rests only on IMD describing its own warnings (Kalsi 2006); S01 has no warnings section and no independent record was found. IMD says it flagged the threat three days ahead and alerted Orissa from 27 Oct: attribute it. What was missing was places to go (C009, itself one source) |
| M5 | Odisha achieved **zero casualties** | Embellished | C020, C015, C023, C037 | "Zero casualty" is the standing order (Phailin 2013, Dana 2024). Phailin and Fani still killed 21 and 64. Dana, a much weaker storm, is the one with 0 |
| M6 | **1 million** evacuated for Phailin | Confirmed | C018 | 9.84 lakh for the cyclone (+1.71 lakh for floods) |
| M7 | **1.2 million** moved in 24 hours for Fani (news) / **1.5 million in 24 hours** (DLNA foreword) | Embellished | C025, C027 | 15,57,170 evacuated; the State's own account says evacuation ran from 1 May to late on 2 May, not 24 hours |
| M8 | OSDMA was **set up after** the super cyclone | Confirmed | C011 | 28 Dec 1999, two months later |
| M9 | The **UN praised** Odisha's zero-casualty policy for Fani | Confirmed (secondary only) | C046 | Quoted by IMD from UNDRR; UNDRR's own page not opened |
| M10 | Fani deaths were mostly from **falling walls and trees** | Unverifiable | — | Only in news (a mid-May 2019 official breakdown is reported but not found at source). Not narrated |
| M11 | Unofficial 1999 tolls of **50,000** | Unverifiable | — | News only. Not narrated |
| M12 | Phailin killed **44** | Embellished | C016, C017 | 44 was the Dec 2013 assessment; the State's final count is 21 cyclone + 51 flood = 72 |
| M13 | Hudhud and Amphan were Odisha successes | Contradicted | C035, C039 | Both crossed other states' coasts (Andhra Pradesh; West Bengal-Bangladesh) |
