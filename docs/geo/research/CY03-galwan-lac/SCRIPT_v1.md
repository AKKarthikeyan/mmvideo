## The title changed, and why
The working title ("Galwan Never Ended: Mapping the LAC Points Where India and China Still Face Off") **fails against the official record** (dossier section 1):
- **"Never ended" / "still face off":** India's External Affairs Minister told the Lok Sabha on 3 Dec 2024 that disengagement "has now been achieved in full in Eastern Ladakh", and no official text of 2025–2026 names a current face-off at any friction point.
- **"Mapping the LAC points":** there is no agreed line to put points on. India says the LAC has "no common understanding in some areas"; China says the western-sector LAC "is clear"; India's own Survey of India Ladakh map has no LAC in its legend. So the map shows labelled places only, never a line.
- **What is true:** the face-offs of 2020 are described as resolved, patrolling is restored at two places only, the massed forces are only at the stage of "discussing de-escalation", and the line has never been commonly delineated.

**Title taken for the build (28 Oct 2026, on AK's "resume"; AK can change it): "Galwan: What India Says Is Settled, and What Isn't" (option 1).**

## Every contested line is attributed
The voice never states a disputed thing as fact. Each government is quoted as that government:
- b2: "India says twenty personnel died; China says casualties on both sides." The two records stay separate and unreconciled (research K1, C033, C035). China's own count (four) is hedged in the research and is **not** used.
- b3, b4, b5: "India says…" every time (EAM, 3 Dec 2024; MEA texts). "Temporary and limited" is India's wording, quoted.
- b1 and b6 are treaty text and the 2026 agreed outcome text (the 1996 Agreement, Article X(1); the 25th Special Representatives talks).
- No side-taking verbs, no names of the dead, no "martyr", no "buffer zone" (no official text uses it), no "invaded", "intruded", "aggression".

## Words and facts left out on purpose
- **The Pangong north-bank "temporary moratorium on patrolling" (C051), Gogra, Hot Springs, Kongka La:** the Short says only what India says in general terms (C057). Naming a place as "temporary" would go beyond the official text, which does not name which of the "few other places" has which step.
- **China's own count of four (C070):** hedged, reaches us only through China Daily on a defence-ministry site. China's own words that the dead were "martyrs" (C071) are in the research for the pinned comment, not the voice.
- **The "no firearms" rule:** the treaty text bans opening fire near the LAC and force in a face-off, and no text bars carrying weapons (C005, C007). The EAM's own post could not be opened (C040). A separate Short.
- **The 2 Oct 2026 list of 28 Ladakh names and China's 13 Oct response (C020, C021):** live and two weeks old. See the hold below.
- **Yangtse (Arunachal, 2022):** the house outline puts the point on China's side (C085), so it can't be pinned.
