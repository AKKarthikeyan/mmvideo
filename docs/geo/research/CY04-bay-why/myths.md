# CY04: the popular version, checked

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | The Bay breeds cyclones because its water is **warm and shallow** | Embellished | C003, C004, C005 | Warm, yes. But the warm layer is deep, under a shallow fresh-water lid (post-monsoon north Bay). The shallow part is the coastal sea bed, which makes surges deadlier, not storms more frequent |
| M2 | The Bay gets **four times** as many cyclones | Embellished | C001 | Long record (1891-2024) 536 vs 139 is almost 4:1; since 1982 it is 120 vs 51, about 2:1 (CY02 M2) |
| M3 | The Bay is **funnel-shaped**, so surges pile up | Confirmed | C005 | IMD's journal lists "convergence of the Bay" and shallow coastal water among the main causes. "Funnel" is our analogy |
