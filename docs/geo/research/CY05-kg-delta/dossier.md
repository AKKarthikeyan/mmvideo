# CY05 The Krishna-Godavari delta: low coast, high surge (3 Nov 2026)

Short, made for the day after Cyclone Murasu's landfall. Research under AK's Shorts rule (1 Nov 2026): two original publishers,
**IMD** (FAQ Q57 surge zones, the Murasu National Bulletin No. 17, IMD's journal *Mausam* for the 1977 toll and its cause) and
**NDMA** (National Guidelines on Management of Cyclones, 2008: the 1990 standing water and the 1977 toll).

## Spine
- IMD rates Bapatla-Kakinada, the Krishna and Godavari estuaries, a very high risk zone: surge about 5-7 m possible [C101].
- For Murasu, IMD forecast a peak surge of 1.0-1.5 m, highest over Bapatla, Krishna and Prakasam [C102].
- Water lingers: standing water in the Krishna delta 11 days after the May 1990 landfall [C103].
- 1977 Divi Seema: about 10,000 dead (NDMA table; IMD journal), attributed to the surge [C104].

## Honesty notes
- The brief says "low-lying coast". No elevation figure was read at a primary source in this run, so the script shows it through
  the 1990 standing water instead of a height above sea level.
- 1.5 m is IMD's forecast for Murasu, not a measured surge; the script says "warned of".
- 5-7 m is IMD's zone figure (what is possible), not a forecast for any storm. NDMA's probable maximum (8.2 m) is higher; not used.
- District names follow IMD's 2 Nov bulletin (new districts: Bapatla exists since 2022).

G7: PASS. No earlier Indica Atlas Short covers the Krishna-Godavari coast. The angle is the gap between Murasu's forecast surge
(1.5 m) and what IMD says this coast can see (5-7 m), with 1977 as the proof.
