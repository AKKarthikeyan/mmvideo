# ON01 The Onion Paradox: dossier (13 Nov 2026)

G7: PASS

Fresh-angle facts (verified; popular versions leave them out or get them wrong):
1. Maharashtra's dominance is about land, not yield: 49% of onion area but 44% of output; its yield (13.9 t/ha) is far below MP (20.4) and Gujarat (23.4). (A-C04, A-C17)
2. ICAR's own 120-day trial: ordinary ventilated storage lost about 55%, worse than the "30-40%" usually quoted. Cold storage is no easy fix: bulbs sprout soon after leaving it. (A-C24, A-C25)
3. The only food out of sixteen with an election price cycle (Dar, Gupta & Verma) is the onion, and prices fall before polls. (C-C06, C-C08)
4. Bangladesh stayed India's biggest buyer in the restricted year FY24 (724,492 t), then fell to 86,688 t in FY26. (B-C14)
5. "Asia's biggest onion market" could not be found in any official record read. (L-C01)
