# ON01 popular claims tested

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | What the records say | Claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | "India grows onions everywhere" | Contradicted | Maharashtra 44% of production, 49% of area; top three states two thirds (2024-25) | A-C04, A-C16 |
| M2 | "Lasalgaon is Asia's biggest onion market" | Unverifiable | Govt: "key mandi", "source market"; APMC home page makes no such claim | A-C28, A-C29, L-C01 |
| M3 | "Onions toppled the Delhi government in 1998" | Embellished | BJP 49 seats (1993) to 15 (1998); a study says "partly"; cause not measurable | C-C01, C-C03, C-C10, C-C23 |
| M4 | "Production is 20% kharif, 20% late kharif, 60% rabi" | Embellished | ICAR: rabi about 60%; NHB 2016-17 split 14.7/19.8/65.4; 2018-19 21.2/9.4/69.4 | A-C19, A-C20, A-C21 |
| M5 | "Storage loses 30-40%" | Confirmed | ICAR wording; its own 120-day trial: about 55% in ventilated storage | A-C23, A-C24 |
| M6 | "Onions have been eaten in India for 5,000 years" | Unverifiable | Charaka Samhita mentions onion (no date established); Raja-Nal-Ka-Tila paper not opened | C-C21, C-C22 |

| M8 | "India is the largest onion exporter" | Unverifiable | Not checked at Comtrade; not narrated | C-C24 |
| M9 | "India is the world's biggest onion grower" | Confirmed | FAOSTAT: first in 2022 and 2023; in 2024 China's FAO estimate edges India | A-C32 |
| M10 | "The export ban is still on" | Contradicted | Ban 8 Dec 2023 to 3/4 May 2024; MEP removed 13 Sep 2024; duty gone 1 Apr 2025; no later notice to 10 Nov 2026 | B-C02, B-C06..B-C08, B-C16 |
