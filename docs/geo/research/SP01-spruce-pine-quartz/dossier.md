# SP01 Spruce Pine quartz: dossier (G6 verifier, 11 Nov 2026)

G7: PASS

Fresh-angle facts (verified; the popular versions leave them out or get them wrong). Judged against the myths lists in the three research logs, not against a survey of existing videos.
1. The famous quartz is the bowl (the fused-quartz crucible used in the Czochralski process), not the chip (A-C01; USGS A07, Sibelco A02, TQC A12). Usable as the hook / "why" answer.
2. Nobody publishes the 70-90% share: USGS says world production data "was not available" and names the US as estimated leader (V-01). The companies say only "world's leading source" (A04).
3. The rail story is more precise than "closed for a year": limited CSX service north of Spruce Pine from mid-January 2025; the Nolichucky gorge section took until 25 Sep 2025; spend about $520M (B-C14, B-C15, B-C17).
4. The mines came through in days to weeks, by the companies' own accounts; what broke was power, roads, water, rail (B-C09, B-C10, B-C13).
5. The "$700B chip industry" and "Taiwan 90%" figures are both off: $795.6B in 2025; the 90% is 2019 capacity under 10 nm (C-002, C-023).

The story in ten lines
1. Taiwan gets the worry about chips.
2. A mountain town in North Carolina, about 2,200 people, has two companies digging high-purity quartz.
3. A chip starts as a crystal of silicon grown in a quartz bowl, the crucible (Czochralski method).
4. The bowl must be extremely pure; the USGS says Spruce Pine HPQ is above 99.999% pure.
5. USGS: two US producers, no economic substitute for most uses; no published world share.
6. Company geology stories differ (dry versus slow-cooled).
7. Helene, 26-27 Sep 2024: about 23-24 in of rain at gauges 6-7 mi SW, both companies stopped 26 Sep.
8. Companies report minor damage, restart in about two weeks (Sibelco) and gradual restart by 23 Oct (TQC).
9. Rail: partial January 2025, full 25 Sep 2025, about $520M.
10. Others expand or try to catch up (Sibelco plans, a Chinese project, synthetic liners); India has approved twelve units.

Hedges that must be kept
- "The story goes" for the pen-in-tin tale (secondary only).
- "One company says" for purity mechanism and age; ingot and crucible figures are one company's page, headed as about solar wafers.
- Taiwan 90% = 2019 capacity under 10 nm logic, 2021 study.
- 70-90% is unverified; do not state it.
- Sibelco/TQC damage and stock statements are company claims.
- Rain "about 23 to 24 inches" at gauges 6-7 miles from town.
- Sibelco expansion 2 is a preliminary estimate.
- HPQ value is rounded USGS tonnage times top price: roughly a billion dollars, not a hard maximum.
- White House 2021 text reports industry comments on silicon-wafer inputs.
- India: only the silicon fab clearly needs silicon wafers; nine of twelve units are packaging.
