# Myths audit (popular version tested, never used as a source)

| ID | Popular claim | Verdict | Evidence / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | HPQ makes the crucible, not the chip | Confirmed | A02, A07, A12 |
| M2 | One crucible pulls about 8 ingots in 400 hours | Confirmed (secondary only) | TQC page only (company says); that page is headed "The making of a solar wafer" (A01) |
| M3 | A 300 mm ingot weighs 500+ kg and is 4.3 m long | Embellished | TQC: 500+ kg, 4.3 m, 25 cm diameter; 300 mm appears only in the separate 2-million-chips sentence |
| M4 | One 300 mm ingot makes about 2 million chips | Confirmed (secondary only) | TQC says it, no method |
| M5 | Pegmatite about 380 million years old, Devonian | Confirmed | Sibelco 380 Ma; UNC abstract 377-404 Ma, best ~377 (A19); TQC says Appalachians 350 Ma (A13) |
| M6 | Waterless magma cooled slowly, so the quartz is pure | Embellished | Two company accounts: Sibelco "lack of water", TQC slow cooling of melted ocean sediment; no peer-reviewed mechanism read |
| M7 | 70-90% (up to 95%) of world HPQ comes from Spruce Pine | Unverifiable | No primary gives a share; USGS says world production "not available" (V-01) |
| M8 | Both HPQ producers mine at Spruce Pine | Confirmed | USGS A07, company pages |
| M9 | The Quartz Corp is Norwegian-owned | Embellished | Created 2011 by Imerys and Norsk Mineral, HQ Drag, Norway; current owners unconfirmed (A10, A12) |
| M10 | Czochralski dipped his pen in tin instead of ink (c.1916) | Confirmed (secondary only) | TQC and a Kiel page retelling a web article; no document by Czochralski; paper printed 1918 (Crossref) vs 1917 (CHM) |
| M11 | Teal and Little ~1950 grew silicon crystals | Contradicted | 1950 was germanium; silicon at Texas Instruments, announced 1954 (A23, A24) |
| M12 | A 2008 fire at the Unimin plant shut supply for months | Embellished | ATF confirms fire 29 Nov 2008, cause undetermined, no foul play; supply effects only in unopened news |
| M13 | Pacific Quartz is building 60,000 t HPQ for CNY 3.2 bn | Embellished | 60,000 t/yr project named in annual report risk section; CNY 3.2 bn not found |
| M14 | Solar uses more HPQ than semiconductors | Unverifiable | No producer or government volume split |
| M15 | Over two feet (24+ in) of rain on Spruce Pine | Embellished | 23.31 in (NHC) and 24.12 in (NWS), gauges 6-7 mi SW of town |
| M16 | Both companies halted 26 Sep 2024 | Confirmed | Sibelco "as of September 26th"; TQC "stopped on September 26th in preparation" |
| M17 | Sibelco restarted after 15 days | Embellished | About 14 days (26 Sep to about 10 Oct); date from page metadata |
| M18 | CSX line severed for 364 days at Spruce Pine | Contradicted | Limited service north of Spruce Pine from mid-January 2025 (CSX B08); whole line back 25 Sep 2025 |
| M19 | CSX rebuild cost about $450M | Contradicted | About $50M (2024) + $470M (2025) = $520M (10-K); 10-Q "exceed $500 million" |
| M20 | Spruce Pine was reached by a single mountain road | Unverifiable | No primary basis found; NCDOT rows partial |
| M21 | 3 to 8 months of wafer inventory | Unverifiable | Analyst commentary only; TQC claims safety stocks |
| M22 | Sibelco's second expansion is approved and backed by prepayments | Embellished | Nov 2023 release: preliminary estimate "of the order of" $500M, study phase |
| M23 | Chips were $700B in 2025 | Contradicted | $795.6B (WSTS/SIA); $700B fits ICs only |
| M24 | HPQ market is $1.5-1.9B | Unverifiable | Vendor figures only (banned) |
| M25 | US government calls Spruce Pine a single point of failure | Unverifiable | USGS: two producers, no economic substitute; White House 2021 (reporting industry comments) says quartz crucibles are sole-sourced or not US-made; neither uses the phrase |
| M26 | Taiwan makes ~90% of advanced chips | Embellished | BCG/SIA 2021: ~90% of <10 nm logic capacity, 2019 data |
| M27 | Most wafers are grown by Czochralski | Confirmed (secondary only) | Siltronic: "a large portion"; no percentage |
| M28 | India will grow its own wafers for its new fabs | Unverifiable | No primary says any approved unit grows ingots/wafers |
