# Review pack: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu (DRAFT, 1 Nov 2026)

One CSV per language (opens in Excel / Sheets, UTF-8): every spoken line of both films with the English, the draft translation, the speed applied and a column for the reviewer.
Listen with the tracks in `out/sivakasi/audio/` (`sivakasi-<film>-<lang>-DRAFT.m4a`) or the multi-audio mp4 (switch the audio track); times in the sheet are the line's start in the film.

What to check, in order:
1. Rows marked CHECK FIRST (the 19 April 2026 explosion, the worker's quote, the makers' leader's quote, Nehru and Murugan quotes): faithful and neutral, same hedges as the English.
2. Numbers and years said right (1922, 1923, 1926, 1938, 1940, 1942, 1980, 2001, 2018, 2025, 2026; 189, 450, 6,000 crore; 3 lakh; 12; 13 percent).
3. Names: Sivakasi, Virudhunagar, Sattur, Ayya Nadar, Shanmuga Nadar, Purna Chandra Roy, Rajarathina Nadar, Arunagiri Nadar, Kaliswari, Rengaiah Murugan, CSIR-NEERI, SWAS / SAFAL / STAR.
4. Spoken style (Tamil and Telugu are colloquial on purpose; Hindi is conversational), and any line that sounds rushed (Fast? = FAST).
5. A line may be reworded, but it must still end in the same time: tell AK the new text and it will be re-voiced and re-fitted.

Rules: the English is the source of truth; no facts added; `reviewed_by` stays empty until AK names the reviewer.
