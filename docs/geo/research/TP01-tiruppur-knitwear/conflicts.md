- **FY26 exports, a fourth figure.** The Ministry of Textiles (PIB, 11 Aug 2026, DGCIS provisional) gives Tiruppur ₹38,677 crore for 2025-26, covering textiles and readymade garments including handicrafts [C504]. This joins TEA's ₹42,544 crore, ~₹46,000 crore and ₹36,891 crore. The scopes differ (district customs data vs the association's own series).
