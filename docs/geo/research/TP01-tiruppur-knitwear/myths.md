# TP01 Tiruppur knitwear town: the popular version, checked

Where these claims come from: the headlines, snippets and summaries of articles, explainers and trade pieces that came up in web searches in English and Tamil on 30 Oct 2026 (tier X: Wikipedia and its mirrors, Upstox, Whalesbook, The Wire, Frontier Weekly, YourStory, Dollar Business, Deccan Chronicle, Business Standard and Fortune India summaries, ANI/Zee5, historyrise, Medium and company blogs, among others). No YouTube video came up in any search, so the YouTube version is represented only by these written versions. They were skimmed only to list which claims circulate. Nothing here enters the script except through claims.jsonl. Verdicts rest only on our own research (RESEARCH.md, VERIFICATION.md).
