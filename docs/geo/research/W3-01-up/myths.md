# W3-01-up: the popular version, checked

The claims come from the topic's pitch and from search-result snippets about "if Uttar Pradesh were a country" (Wikipedia, The Globalist, VividMaps, Pew, Dhaka Tribune and others, all tier X; none opened, none used as evidence). Nothing here enters the script except through claims.jsonl.

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | If UP were a country it would be the **fifth** most populous in the world | Embellished | C008, C010, C011 | It is sixth in 2026 on every basis we tried. In 2011 it was fifth only if you scale UP up to the UN's higher India figure; on the plain census count it was sixth, because Pakistan (203.7 million, UN estimate) was ahead of 199.8 million. The popular "fifth" lists China, the rest of India, the US and Indonesia and leaves Pakistan out |
| M2 | UP has **more people than Brazil** | Confirmed | C009, C010 | 2011: 199.8 vs 195.3 million (UN estimate for Brazil). 2026: 243.5 million (projection) vs 213.6 million |
| M3 | UP has more people than **Pakistan** (a claim in some older rankings) | Contradicted | C009, C010 | Pakistan is ahead in both 2011 (203.7 vs 199.8 million) and 2026 (259.3 vs 243.5 million) |
| M4 | UP is as populous as **Nigeria**, "about 240 million" | Embellished | C009 | Close: 243.5 vs 242.4 million (2026), a 0.4% gap that is inside the error of both numbers. The 240 million figure printed in some articles is a projection, not a count |
| M5 | UP has "**240 million**" people | Embellished | C001, C006, C019 | The last count is 199.8 million (2011). 24 crore is a projection for 2026 |
| M6 | UP is "one of the **biggest** countries" | Contradicted | C012, C013 | By area it would be about 81st of 216, the size of the United Kingdom. It is big by people, not by land |
| M7 | UP is **larger than** the UK / **as big as** the UK | Embellished | C013 | Within 1% either way depending on the dataset (UK 243,610; UP 240,928; our own Natural Earth measure 242,737 vs 240,677). Say "about the size of" |
| M8 | The map of India **hides** how big UP is, or fools us about its size (the category label "Map is lying") | Unverifiable | C005 | Not tested, and the playbook bans saying a map fools people about size. What is checkable: UP is 7.3% of India's land and 16.5% of its people (C004, C005) |
| M9 | UP is **bigger than** a given country that is only a bit bigger by area, e.g. "bigger than Britain" | Embellished | C012, C013 | See M7 |
| M10 | UP has the **densest** population in India or the world | Unverifiable | C016 | Not tested for India's states or the world. What we found: among countries with over 10 million people only Bangladesh is denser (C016, mixed-basis, hedged) |
