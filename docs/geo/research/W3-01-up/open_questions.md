# W3-01-up: open questions and gaps

1. **Survey of India state area.** UP's 240,928 sq km is the Census 2011 figure (also in MoSPI's table from the 2009 Forest Survey of India report). Survey of India's own state-area record was not opened. The area-matching check with Natural Earth (C022) agrees within 0.11%. Not blocking.
2. **Countries' own censuses.** Pakistan's 2023 census (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics: connection refused) and Brazil's 2022 census (IBGE: HTTP 403) could not be read at source; Nigeria's census record was not searched. Country figures rest on UN DESA's harmonised WPP 2024, a data owner for its own estimates but not the countries' own counts. A checker with another network could read PBS and IBGE and compare with UP's NCP projection for the same year (Table 8, 1 March, 2023 and 2022). Not blocking, because the UP-vs-Nigeria position is a near tie in any case.
3. **Original MoHFW/NCP address for the projection report** was not reachable by script; the PDF came from a GiveWell file server. The checker should compare its first page (cover: "November, 2019", "National Commission on Population", "Ministry of Health & Family Welfare") with the ministry's copy if reachable.
4. **A complete list of landlocked countries** was not opened. C018 is worded against UNCTAD's list of 32 landlocked developing countries only; do not say "the biggest landlocked country in the world".
5. **UP has 80 of the 543 Lok Sabha seats**: widely stated, not researched (no primary opened). Drop or research before use.
6. **Authority for "official projection".** The NCP/Technical Group report says it revises the "existing official projection figures" (C020). Whether RGI's own SRS-based figures differ for UP was not checked.
7. **Hedges the script must keep:** "projected/about 24 crore" for 2026; "about level with Nigeria"; "about the size of the UK"; "sixth" not "fifth".

## Wording risks for AK
This topic is not flagged sensitive, but these wordings would break a rule or mislead:
- "fifth biggest country" (wrong; see M1).
- "bigger than Pakistan" (wrong; see M3).
- "the map fools you about size" (banned by the playbook; see M8).
- "UP is a big country" without saying by people, not land.
- Anything about UP breaking away or being a country in reality: it is a hypothetical comparison only.
- Politics/communities: none needed. Do not mention religion, caste, elections or fertility policy.
- Showing India's outline: use the house India point-of-view file; UP's Nepal edge is an international border and needs no label.
