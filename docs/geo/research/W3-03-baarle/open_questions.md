## Wording risks for AK (borders, neighbours, colonial)
- No dispute exists between Belgium and the Netherlands about this border. Say "enclaves", not "disputed". The 1959 case was a settled legal dispute over two plots; say "a court settled it".
- Say "Belgian enclave" and "Dutch enclave" with the owner's name; avoid "Belgian town in the Netherlands" (Baarle-Hertog is a municipality of 22 pieces plus mainland parts).
- Do not say "cafes", "the door decides the country" or "half the world's enclaves" (all unverified).
- Do not call the 1995 act a "treaty".
- Avoid ridicule ("absurd", "silliest"): both municipalities now govern jointly (since 2021, C023).
