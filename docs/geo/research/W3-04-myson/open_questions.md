# W3-04-myson: gaps, unreachable sources, wording risks

## Wording risks for AK (⚑ religion, minority, neighbour, war)
1. **"Hindu temples" is UNESCO's word.** The Cham kings' inscriptions name Shiva, Bhadresvara, Isanesvara; Cham communities and Vietnamese heritage workers may see the site as Cham, not "Indian" or "Hindu heritage in Vietnam". Say "Shiva temples" or "Hindu temples, as UNESCO calls them". Never "Indian temples in Vietnam".
2. **Colonisation language.** Do not say Indians colonised, conquered or settled Champa; the records opened do not say so (M1, K10). Majumdar's 1927 "Indian colonies" frame is an interested one. "Indianised" is a historians' word: avoid or explain.
3. **"Hindu kingdom".** Buddhism was strong and shared the sanctuary (C016, C017). Avoid.
4. **Today's Cham.** Do not say how many Cham are Hindu, Muslim or Buddhist; no census table was opened (gap 3). Do not imply the Cham vanished: ICOMOS says the kingdom was "absorbed by the growing power of Vietnam" (S01), a loaded phrase; do not use or paraphrase it.
5. **The sea.** UNESCO says "South China Sea"; Vietnam says "East Sea". Do not label it on screen or in the voice.
6. **War.** The 1969 bombing is in ICOMOS's passive voice [C034]. Do not name a bomber, a side, or compare with other wars.
7. **India's role.** India's ASI account is a Government of India press release; "confluence with India" and "magnificent" are its words. Say "ASI reports". Do not frame India as claiming the site; Vietnam's board corroborates the project [C027].
8. **Do not use "capital", "oldest", "destroyed", "largest"** (M4, M6, M8). **Do not give B1's date** (K4).
9. **Do not put the Kaundinya legend as history.** It is a royal myth on a stele [C023].
10. **A Short cannot say "why" with one cause.** The honest "why" is "its kings chose it; when and how the religion arrived is not recorded".

## Open gaps
1. **Persee (BEFEO) refused automated access** (altcha). The BEFEO 1904 text was read from the archive.org scan (OCR). A checker with a browser should read Finot 1904 pp. 897-977 at persee.fr/doc/befeo_0336-1519_1904_num_4_1_1405 and confirm C011, C013, C014, C017, C018, C023.
2. **The Kaundinya stele's number in the modern corpus** (K12): the ISAW corpus online does not yet include it. EFEO's printed *Inscriptions of Campa* (Griffiths et al.) or Coedes's 1923 list would settle it.
3. **No census table opened** for Cham population and religion (General Statistics Office, 2019 census). Search snippets disagree (167,729 vs 178,948 Cham); not used.
4. **Chinese dynastic histories** (Linyi, Fan Hu-da, the 192 CE start and the 5th/6th century embassies) not read; Majumdar summarises them. Needed only if a script states them.
5. **The 2014 India-Vietnam MoU text** and an Indian second phase (E and F groups from 2025; a 2020 Joint Vision and a 2024 PM statement mention "Block F") were seen only in search snippets of Embassy/PIB pages: not opened. Not claimed.
6. **ASI booklet (Dec 2022)** on the Embassy site returned HTTP 403 to curl: the technical account of the A10 linga find is not read. Add if the Short leans on the linga.
7. **Date of Bhadravarman's stele** is a range (K6). The ISAW edition of the stele (C. 72) is not online; the printed corpus could narrow the date.
8. **Why the Committee changed "introduction" to "indigenous society adapting"** (C005): no debate record opened.
9. **Excavation reports** (Tra Kieu, Hoa Lai; Polish and Italian missions) not read: no archaeology result is claimed beyond ICOMOS's descriptions.
10. **Pronunciation of Mỹ Sơn and Cham names** is unsourced.
11. **Dong Duong** (the Buddhist site, ~20 km from My Son per Finot) has no official coordinate in our files: do not pin.
12. The Vo-canh inscription's date range (2nd to about 400 CE in search snippets) is not narrated and not sourced here.
