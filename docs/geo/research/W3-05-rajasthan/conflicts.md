# W3-05: conflicts and how they were ranked

**K1. Norway: mainland or Kingdom.** Statistics Norway's whole-country figure is the mainland only, 323,805 sq km (S08, note: "The mainland without Svalbard and Jan Mayen"). Statistics Norway's own table note defines the Kingdom as the land area including islands, Svalbard and fresh water (S15). Eurostat's Norway total 384,486 includes Svalbard (60,299) and Jan Mayen (377) (S06). UNSD lists Norway (323,772) and Svalbard and Jan Mayen (62,422) as separate lines (S03). Nobody is wrong; the question "is Rajasthan bigger than Norway" has no answer without choosing. Kept both (C007), hedged. In the UN's own M49 geoscheme Svalbard and Jan Mayen is a separate area, so on the UN basis Norway = mainland and the count is six.

**K2. FAO's "country area" is far above the offices' figures for Norway and Sweden.** FAO 624,500 (Norway) and 528,860 (Sweden) against the offices' 384,486 and 438,574 (UNSD) or 447,424 (Eurostat 2023) (S04 vs S03, S06). FAO's definition note was not opened; the extra area looks like coastal waters. Ranking: the national offices and UNSD are the owners of those numbers and agree with each other; FAO is not used for any count (C012 records the gap). The World Bank repeats FAO (S05), so is not independent.

**K3. Sweden: 438,574 (UNSD, Statistics Sweden's figure as reported) vs 447,424 (Eurostat 2023).** A 2% gap, probably a different survey year or sea inclusion. Both are far above Rajasthan; not material.

**K4. France: 551,500 (UNSD, metropolitan) vs 638,475 (Eurostat, includes overseas regions).** Larger than Rajasthan on both. Say "mainland France" if a figure is shown.

**K5. Finland: 336,884 (UNSD, excludes Åland) vs 338,485 (FAO 2024) vs 338,493 (Statistics Finland 2026) vs 338,411 (Eurostat 2023).** All within 0.5%. Ranking: Statistics Finland (owner, latest). Rajasthan is bigger than every one of them.

**K6. Rajasthan: census 342,239 vs our Natural Earth polygon 343,082.** The 0.25% gap is coastline/outline generalisation and different boundary data. Ranking: the census (Survey of India's figure) for any number shown; the polygon only for drawing.

**K7. Rajasthan's rank among Indian states** is stable (342,239 vs 308,252 for Madhya Pradesh). The 2011 list predates the 2014 Telangana split; it does not affect Rajasthan.
