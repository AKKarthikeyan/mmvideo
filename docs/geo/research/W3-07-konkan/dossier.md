# W3-07 Konkan Railway: "the line they said could not be built" (Indica Atlas Week 3, research dossier, lite mode, 8 Nov 2026)

Sources: S01 KRCL (builder's website, annual report, coffee book), S02 Rajya Sabha written answers 1969 and 1980 (Ministry of Railways), S03 Sida Evaluation 97/25 (Karlis Goppers, 1997). Claim IDs refer to claims.jsonl.

## 0. The honest answer to the pitch
Not as worded. No named person, report or date in these sources says the line could not be built. The dated record says something else: in 1969 the Railway Minister told the Rajya Sabha the line (about Rs 60 crore) could not be considered "at present" for lack of money (C01). The "impossible" in Sida is raising Rs 10 billion inside the railway budget (C10). Only KRCL's own 2023 book says "all others" called it impractical, naming nobody (C09).
Honest wording: "The line India kept saying it couldn't afford" or "For decades the answer was 'not now'. Then it was built in about seven years."

## 1. Dated timeline
- 11 Aug 1969: Government says it cannot consider the Apta-Goa line now; money (C01).
- By Jun 1980: work begun on the first 62 km, Apta-Roha; opened Mar 1986 (C02).
- 19 Jul 1990: KRCL incorporated; 15 Sep 1990: foundation stone at Roha (C03). Target: 4.5 years (C04).
- 1993-97: soft-soil tunnels in Goa become the main cost and delay (C08).
- 26 Jan 1998: whole line Roha-Thokur opens (C05).

## 2. Our computations (method/distance_and_arithmetic.py, output saved)
- 15 Sep 1990 to 26 Jan 1998 = 2,690 days = 7.36 years; target 4.5 years; about 2.9 years late.
- Pace about 100 km a year on the main line vs about 10-11 km a year on Apta-Roha (1980-86): about 9-10x.
- Tunnels 84.496 of 738.941 km = 11.4% of the route; one tunnel every 8.1 km on average.
- Mumbai-Mangaluru great circle 718 km (Natural Earth points); KRCL rail 914 km vs 2,041 km before.

## 3. Fresh angles (G7)
G7: PASS
1. The real "no" was money, not engineering: 1969 Parliament answer (C01), and Sida's "impossible to raise 10 billion" (C10). That is why a company owned by the Centre and four states was created.
2. The first 62 km took about six years; the remaining ~740 km took about 7 years 4 months (C02, C05, method).
3. It was late and everyone knew the deadline was unreal: target 4.5 years, called "unrealistically optimistic" and partly political by Sida, "by no means realistic" by KRCL itself (C04).
4. The hardest ground was soft soil in Goa, not the hard Sahyadri rock (C08).

## 4. Hook and twist candidates (not script)
- Hook: "In 1969 India said: not now, too expensive." Twist: built in about seven years once a company was set up to raise the money.
- Hook: "91 tunnels, over 2,000 bridges" (C06). Twist: the worst trouble was soft mud, not mountains (C08).
- Number hook: 41 hours to 15 hours Mumbai-Mangaluru (C11, KRCL figure).

## 6. What a 90-word Short can safely say
- 1969: the government told Parliament it couldn't consider the line now; money was short (C01).
- The first 62 km took about six years (C02).
- 1990: a new company, KRCL; foundation stone 15 Sep 1990 (C03).
- Given 4.5 years; even its builders called that unrealistic (C04).
- Opened 26 Jan 1998, Republic Day (C05).
- About 740 km, 91 tunnels, over 2,000 bridges (C06).
- A Swedish evaluator: "the biggest, and perhaps most difficult" railway job of the century in that part of the world (C07).
- The worst part: soft-soil tunnels in Goa (C08).
