|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Experts said the Konkan Railway could not be built | Unverifiable | C01, C09, C10 | No named person, report or date says so in the three sources. The dated government "no" (Rajya Sabha, 1969) is about money: "not possible to consider ... at present" because of the ways-and-means position. The "impossible" in Sida is raising Rs 10 billion inside the railway budget. Only KRCL's own 2023 anniversary book says "all others" had called it impractical, naming nobody |
