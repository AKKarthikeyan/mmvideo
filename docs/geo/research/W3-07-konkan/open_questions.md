# W3-07 Konkan Railway: open questions and gaps

## Gaps that matter for the script
- Who, by name, said the line "could not be built"? Not found in the three sources. Unless a dated, named record turns up, the pitch wording must change (see dossier section 0).
- KRCL's "when all others had expressed impracticality" (C09) is unattributed and self-serving; usable only as "the builder's own book says".
- The chairman's "not been done anywhere in the world" (C08) is his claim, reported by Sida; say "its chief engineer said".
