# W3-08 "How a 1341 flood created Kochi's harbour": research dossier (lite, 8 Nov 2026)

Research only. No script. Every sentence carries claim IDs. Sources: `sources.jsonl` (S01-S03); method in `method/`.

## Verdict on the pitch
**Not true as worded.** 1341 is a real date: the start of the Puthuvaippu ("new deposit") era used in old Cochin deeds [C002]. But the records that carry it (Visscher 1723, Menon 1911) tie it to the island of Vypin appearing from the sea or being settled, and Menon thinks settlement more likely [C003, C004]. None of them mentions a flood, the Periyar or Muziris [C006]. The flood story is a later explanation; Cochin Port Authority itself says only "It is believed" [C001]. And the harbour ships use today was made in 1920-1928, when Robert Bristow's dredgers cut the sand bar [C007, C008].
Honest wording: "In 1341 a new island, Vypin, appeared (or was first settled) at Kochi's mouth; people later believed a great Periyar flood did it. The harbour big ships use was cut by dredgers in 1928."

## The real story, in plain words
- Kochi people counted years from 1341 in a "new deposit" era [C002]; a Dutch chaplain heard in 1723 that it began 383 years before [C002].
- The island between the Kochi and Kodungallur mouths, Vypin, is river silt, called "new deposit", formed in the fourteenth century per the 1911 manual [C004].
- The 1911 State Manual says the name Cochin belongs to the town that grew after 1341, and that classical and medieval travellers do not mention it [C005].
- The port's founding story adds a Periyar flood and the silting of Muziris, hedged [C001, C009].
- Until 1928 Kochi was an open roadstead; the bar was cut on 30 March 1928 and SS Padma sailed in on 26 May 1928 [C007, C008].

## Fresh angles (gate G7)
1. **The flood is the legend; the hard record is a calendar.** Kochi's 1341 survives because deeds were dated in a "new deposit" era [C002, C003, C006].
2. **The real harbour is 1928, not 1341.** For 587 years after 1341, big ships anchored outside the bar; a dredger cut the entrance [C007, C008; method/era_check_output.txt].
3. Even the port hedges: "It is believed" [C001].
