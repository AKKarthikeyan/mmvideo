# W3-08 Kochi: the popular version, checked (lite: 3 claims)

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | A great flood of the Periyar in 1341 created Kochi harbour (the pitch) | Unverifiable | C001, C006 | The date 1341 is real (the Puthuvaippu era) but the records that carry it (1723, 1887, 1911) speak of an island appearing or being settled, not a flood. The flood is a later explanation; even the port says "It is believed". |
| M2 | The same 1341 flood silted up and ended Muziris | Unverifiable | C009, C006 | Only the port page says so; no source we read dates Muziris's end to 1341. |
| M3 | Kochi has been a great harbour since 1341 | Embellished | C007, C008 | Until 1928 big ships anchored outside a sand bar ("a mere roadstead" in the port's words); the harbour that ships enter was cut by Bristow, 1920s. |
