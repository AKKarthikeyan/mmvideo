# W3-10 Why Kerala has almost no empty land: research dossier (Shorts lite, 8 Nov 2026)

**Status: research done, G6 pending independent check (verify.md).** Pitch (AK): "Why Kerala has almost no empty land". Short, 25-35 s, 85-100 spoken words. Sources: S01 MoA&FW Land Use Statistics at a Glance 2023-24 (provisional year), S02 MoA&FW land-use definitions, S03 Census 2011 A-01. One basis per comparison: land shares = LUS 2023-24 (p) reporting area; people = Census 2011.

## Verdict on the pitch
**Mostly true, needs a definition.** If "empty" means the official idle classes (barren, pasture, culturable waste, fallow), only 5.6% of Kerala's land is idle vs 21.0% for India (C03). But it is not "almost no empty land" in the plain sense: 27.8% is classed as forest (C04), and Kerala is not the extreme (Punjab 3.0%, West Bengal 3.5%; C10).
Honest wording: "Only about 1 hectare in 18 of Kerala lies idle; across India it is about 1 in 5." Say "idle" or "unused farmland", not "empty".

## Why (what the sources support)
- Crowding: 860 people per sq km in 2011, denser than any state except Bihar and West Bengal (C01, C02).
- Spread-out settlement: 1,017 inhabited villages and only 1 uninhabited village (C07); 520 towns; 47.7% urban on 19.6% of area (C08).
- Building is eating farmland: non-agricultural land +16% in ten years while net sown area fell (C05).
Causes beyond these (land reform, remittances, terrain) are NOT in our sources: do not say them.

## Author gain or loss (rule 6)
S01/S02: MoA&FW compiles state returns (Kerala DES); it gains nothing from Kerala looking full; the 2023-24 year is provisional. S03: ORGI, neutral counting body.

G7: PASS. Fresh angles the popular version leaves out:
1. Kerala is not the least idle state: Punjab and West Bengal have less idle land (C10). The Kerala story is idle land + high density together.
2. Over a quarter of Kerala is "forest" on paper, a legal class that need not be wooded (C04, C06); the recorded figure has not moved in ten years (C09).
3. Only 1 village in Kerala had nobody living in it in 2011 (C07).
4. Concrete is the growing class: +84 thousand ha non-agricultural in a decade (C05).

## Hook and twist candidates
- Hook: "Kerala left just 1 village empty in 2011." (C07)
- Twist: "But Punjab has even less idle land; Kerala's trick is fitting 860 people on every sq km around it." (C10, C01)

## What a 9-beat 85-100 word Short could safely say
1. In 2023-24 only 5.6% of Kerala's land was idle: barren, waste or fallow. (C03)
2. Across India the figure was 21.0%. (C03)
3. About 1 hectare in 18 in Kerala, vs about 1 in 5 in India. (C03)
4. Kerala packed 860 people per sq km in 2011. (C01)
5. Only Bihar and West Bengal were denser among states. (C02)
6. Census 2011 found 1,017 inhabited villages and only 1 uninhabited one. (C07)
7. Nearly half its people (47.7%) lived in towns covering a fifth (19.6%) of the land. (C08)
8. Land under buildings, roads and water grew 16% in ten years. (C05)
9. Farmland sown fell from 2,043 to 1,973 thousand hectares in the same decade. (C05)
10. Still, 27.8% of Kerala is classed as forest, a legal category, wooded or not. (C04, C06)
