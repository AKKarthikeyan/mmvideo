# W3-11 Colachel: popular claims (top 3)

| ID | Popular claim | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | "The first time an Asian/Indian kingdom defeated a European power" | Unverifiable | None of S01-S03 says this; it needs a comparison across all of Asian history that these sources do not make. Do not say it. |

| M3 | "A great Dutch fleet/army was destroyed in a pitched battle" | Embellished | Dutch own count: whole Malabar field force 350 Europeans + 490 Malays (C005); Dutch letters describe a small garrison surrendering after a fire (C006).
