# W3-11 Colachel: open questions

- Original VOC letters (Letters from Quilon 15 Aug 1741, MS. No. 317) not seen in Dutch; we rely on the 1911 Madras translation/summary.
- Exact day (C010) unresolved; Kollam-to-Gregorian conversion of 916 ME not independently computed (the two Travancore sources disagree by 2 days).
- Colachel today in Kanniyakumari district, Tamil Nadu: not checked in these sources (needs a Census/district source if said).
- Indian Army's Colachel page was unreachable (1 Nov); not used.

## Wording risks for AK
1. "An Indian kingdom beat the Dutch": say "Travancore" (a kingdom, 1741; "India" as a state did not exist). "Beat the Dutch at Colachel" is safe; "beat the Dutch" in the war is not: the peace treaty came years later (C009 is DISPUTED: 1748 draft vs 1753 ratification).
2. Never say "first Asian victory over a European power" (M1, unverifiable).
3. Do not say "destroyed the Dutch fleet/navy" or "drove the Dutch out of India" (M2, M3).
4. Do not put a single date on screen; use "1741" (C010).
5. Attribute the numbers: "Dutch letters say..." (C005, C006), "Travancore's histories say..." (C007). Present both versions of what happened without picking one.
6. De Lannoy: call him a captured Dutch officer, not "admiral" or "commander of the Dutch fleet" (no source says so).
7. Avoid religious or nationalist framing of Martanda Varma (e.g. the sword-at-the-shrine story in S03 is a court account; leave it out or attribute it).
8. Kanniyakumari/Tamil Nadu vs Kerala heritage claims are a live regional sensitivity: name the place as "Colachel, on the old Travancore coast" unless a current official source is added.
