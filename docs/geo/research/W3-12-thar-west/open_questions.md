- Kerala comparison (C012) is the handbook's wording only; a Kerala area source would be a 4th source (lite cap). Do not narrate as a measured comparison.
- Barmer (92/sq km) and Bikaner (78/sq km) DCHBs are saved but not cited (lite cap). If the Short names them, cite those pages and accept a 4-source warning.
- Census 2011 is the latest district census; no newer official density exists. Rajasthan has since created new districts, so "33rd of 33" is a 2011 rank only.
- CGWB Table 7 is undated; IMD's own district normal not opened (S01 credits DES Rajasthan).
- The canal's opening year, length and command area were not checked (no Rajasthan WRD / Jal Shakti source opened).

## Wording risks for AK
- Say "sparse" or "one of India's emptiest districts by Rajasthan's own standard", not "nobody lives here": 6.7 lakh people live there.
- Say "in the 2011 census" with every density or rank.
- The district borders Pakistan: show no international border line on the map; do not imply the border causes the emptiness (no source tested that).
- "Less than a tenth of the state average" is safe; "a twelfth" is not (200/17 = 11.8 printed, 11.5 unrounded).
