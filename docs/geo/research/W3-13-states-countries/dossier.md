## Verdict on the premise
**True by people, on a stated basis; not testable by economy.** Put every state and UT in the UN's 2026 table in place of India (India's own 2026 projection for states, UN figures for countries, both 1 July) and 12 of the world's 50 most populous "countries" would be Indian states [C008]. Several states match a whole country almost exactly [C004-C007]. Two cautions: the state figures are projections, not counts [C001], and the UN's India total is 3.5% higher than India's own, so every pair is checked on both bases [C012]. The "state economy = country" version cannot be put on one basis from primary sources and is dropped (myths M3, open_questions 1).

Complementary to W3-01-up: that Short owns UP (and UP vs the UK). This one uses **other** states and **other** countries.

## The facts, in plain words (2026, 1 July)
- **Bihar = Mexico:** 132.9 vs 133.0 million (level) [C005]. Mexico has about 20.7 times Bihar's land [C005]; Bihar about 1,400 people per sq km against Mexico's 68 [C011].
- **Maharashtra > Japan:** 129.6 vs 122.4 million (+5.8%) [C004]. In 2011 Maharashtra was 11.9% behind Japan; the order flipped as Japan shrank [C004].
- **Rajasthan = Germany:** 83.9 vs 83.6 million AND nearly the same land, 343,697 vs 356,379 sq km (our measure) [C006]. The only pair that matches on both.
- **Karnataka = United Kingdom:** 69.1 vs 69.9 million: level, order depends on basis [C007].
- **The ranking (India dissolved):** Bihar 12th, Maharashtra 13th (just ahead of Japan, 14th), West Bengal 19th, Madhya Pradesh 21st, Rajasthan 23rd (Germany 24th) [C008]. UP would be 5th in this frame [C002, not spine].
- **Reserve pairs:** West Bengal = Vietnam, Kerala = Malaysia (level) [C014]; Jharkhand = Canada (41.1 vs 40.5 million) on 1/127 of the land [C013]. 22 of 37 states/UTs exceed the median country or area in the UN list [C009].

## Fresh angles (G7)
G7: PASS. Verified facts the popular "if states were countries" lists leave out or get wrong (myths.md):
1. **Bihar overtook Maharashtra.** Lists built on 2011 numbers put Maharashtra above Bihar; India's official projection has Bihar passing Maharashtra in 2023, and in 2026 Bihar is the Mexico-sized one (132.9 million) [C010, C005]. Hook/"why" candidate: "The list you've seen is out of date."
2. **Rajasthan is a Germany twin twice over:** same people (within 0.3%) and same land (within 4%) [C006]. A clean overlay visual no other pair gives.
3. **The order flipped with Japan.** Maharashtra was 12% behind Japan in 2011 and is 6% ahead in 2026 [C004]: a story of one place growing and one shrinking, not a static fact.
4. **Kerala is not Canada; Jharkhand is,** on one-127th of the land [M2, C013, C014].
5. **Two Indias.** The UN counts 3.5% more Indians than India's own projection, so a "state vs country" table needs its basis stated [C012].
