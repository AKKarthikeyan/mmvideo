# W3-13-states-countries: the popular version, checked (lite: 3 most common claims)

Popular "if Indian states were countries" lines, from AK's working title and the usual list-post wording. No news or blog page was opened as evidence; nothing here enters the script except through claims.jsonl. (The 1 Nov run's longer 10-row table is superseded; the UP "fifth or sixth" myth belongs to sibling W3-01-up.)

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Bihar** has more people than **Japan** (or Germany, Russia) | Embellished | C005, C008, C010 | Japan and Germany: true in the 2026 projection (Bihar 132.9 million vs Japan 122.4). Russia (143.4 million) is still ahead: contradicted. And it is a projection: on 1 July 2011 Bihar (104.8 million, NCP) was well behind Japan (128.1 million, UN) |
| M2 | **Kerala** is about the size of **Canada** (by people) | Contradicted | C014, C013 | Kerala 36.2 million vs Canada 40.5 million in 2026. Kerala's match is Malaysia (36.4); Canada's match is Jharkhand (41.1) |
| M3 | **Maharashtra's economy** is bigger than a named country's (Switzerland, Thailand, ...) | Unverifiable | none | State GSDP and country GDP could not be put on one basis (India's accounts are fiscal-year, April to March, against calendar-year GDP elsewhere; the RBI state table sat behind a bot check on 1 Nov). Not narrated; see open_questions.md |
