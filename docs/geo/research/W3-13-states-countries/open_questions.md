# W3-13-states-countries: open questions

1. **GDP leg (dropped).** "State economy = country X" cannot be made like-for-like from reachable primary sources: India's national accounts are on an April-March year while other countries' GDP is calendar-year, the RBI Handbook state GSDP table returned a bot-check page on 1 Nov, and a rupee-dollar conversion adds a third choice (market rate or PPP). Not narrated. If AK wants it later: MoSPI state GSDP release + IMF WEO, stated as "fiscal 2023-24 vs calendar 2023", hedged.
2. **Projection, not count.** Every 2026 state figure is the NCP projection; Census 2027 (reference date 1 March 2027, verified in W3-01-up) may move these pairs, especially the near-ties (Bihar-Mexico, Rajasthan-Germany, Karnataka-UK).
3. **Units in the NCP table.** It lists Daman & Diu and Dadra & Nagar Haveli separately (merged 2020) and has Ladakh separate. Does not affect any pair; affects only the "37 states and UTs" count in C008/C009.
4. **Natural Earth vs official area.** Generalised geometry; good to about 1.5%. Fine for "about the same land" / "twenty times".
