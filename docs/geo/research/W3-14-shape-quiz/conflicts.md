# W3-14-shape-quiz: conflicts between sources

No real disagreement among S01-S03 on any spine claim.

1. **Spelling.** The 1956 Act writes "Kanya Kumari district" and "Shencottah"; Census 2011 writes "Kanniyakumari" and "Shenkottai". Same places (the four sub-district names match the Act's four taluks exactly, C006). On screen use the current common spelling only after AK picks it.
2. **Areas.** Natural Earth outlines (S03) are generalised; they are not used for any area or rank, so they cannot conflict with Survey of India figures (S01).
