# W4-02 Ganga at Varanasi: dossier (Shorts Lite, 14 Nov 2026)

Pitch tested: "At Varanasi the Ganga flows north, back towards the mountains." ⚑ religion.

**Verdict: TRUE, with honest wording.** Past Varanasi the Ganga turns and runs north for about 15 km (our measurement, overall bearing 15 degrees), and over a 58 km bend it ends up 37 km further north (C05, C06). Two government owners agree without saying it in words: the Central Water Commission puts Varanasi on the river's LEFT bank (C02) and the district puts it on the WEST bank (C03); both are true only if the river flows north there (C04). "Back towards the mountains" is true only as a direction (the Himalaya lie north): the river does not go back up, and it turns east again below the Gomti (C07). Do not say "the only place" (M1).

Sources (3): S01 CWC basin page (P2), S02 Varanasi district website (P2, tourism pages, beliefs credited), S03 Natural Earth 10m rivers + our computation (method/ganga_north.py).

## 1. Spine claims
C01 source and 2,525 km; C02 left bank, 195 km below Allahabad; C03 west bank; C04 therefore north-flowing; C05 ~15 km within 45 degrees of north, bearing 15; C06 58 km run, 37 km northward gain; C07 whole river bears south-east, east after Gomti; C08 117 km straight vs 195 km by river.

## 2. Fresh angles
G7: PASS
1. **The government's own pages prove it, by accident** (C02, C03, C04): CWC says left bank, the district says west bank. Face downstream with the west bank on your left and you are facing north. Popular versions cite legend; this is a check anyone can do.
2. **The belief is right overall, and that is why the bend surprises** (C07, C09): the whole river runs south-east (128 degrees source to delta); the Varanasi bend is a local turn of about 15 km, and northward bends occur elsewhere too, so "only place" is wrong.
3. (spare) Allahabad to Varanasi is 117 km as the crow flies but about 195 km by river (C08).

## 3. Hook and twist candidates
- Hook: "The Ganga flows from the Himalaya down to the sea. Right?" Twist: "At Varanasi, it turns north." (C04, C05)
- Twist proof: "Even the government's own pages say so: left bank, west bank." (C02, C03)

## 4. Reveal Loop material
1. **The belief.** "The Ganga starts in the Himalaya and flows down to the sea, away from the mountains. Right?" Evidence it is held: the official description runs source to Bay of Bengal (C01, S01), and the whole river does bear south-east (C07). I could not open a textbook or quiz that states "south-east" in this run (NCERT Class 9 PDF returned 404), so commonness is assumed, not shown.
2. **The turn.** At Varanasi the Ganga turns and runs north for about 15 km (C04, C05, C06).
3. **The camera move.** Open on the Natural Earth Varanasi pin (25.332 N, 82.998 E; S03). Trace the river line from 25.205 N 82.996 E north through 25.211 N 83.008 E to 25.323 N 83.042 E (NE vertices 460-467, method output), with a measured arrow labelled "about 15 km north, our measurement". Continue the pull-out north-east to 25.535 N 83.213 E (end of the 58 km bend), then tilt north to the Himalaya on shaded relief. Pins, line and arrow only; no borders. Sentinel-2 at this scale should show the ghat crescent on the west bank: check visually that the NE line sits on the water.
4. **Felt comparison (our measurement).** "In 58 km of river around Varanasi, it ends up 37 km further north: almost two kilometres north for every three it flows." (C06) Alternative: "117 km apart as the crow flies, 195 km by river" (C08).
5. **Human detail.** The district's own page puts the city on "the west bank" (C03). Belief detail, credited: the district says the Ganges in Varanasi "is believed to have the power to wash away the sins" (C11). (88 ghats is SECONDARY-ONLY, C10: do not use.)
6. **Loop lines** (opening line: "The Ganga flows from the Himalaya down to the sea."):
   a. "...and then, just below the city, it turns east again, because" -> "The Ganga flows from the Himalaya down to the sea."
   b. "So next time someone tells you" -> "The Ganga flows from the Himalaya down to the sea."
   c. "Which is why it's only half true that" -> "The Ganga flows from the Himalaya down to the sea."
7. **Send line.** People forward this because it is a surprise about the river every Indian knows, and the proof is on the government's own websites (left bank, west bank).
8. **Topic test.**
   - Familiar: PASS. The Ganga at Varanasi's ghats is recognisable in one second with the sound off.
   - Hidden: PASS (narrow). Most viewers assume it runs away from the mountains; but Varanasi residents and pilgrims may already know the bend, so the hook must be the government-pages proof, not the fact alone.
   - Send: PASS. A "did you know" about the sacred river, with a proof anyone can check.
