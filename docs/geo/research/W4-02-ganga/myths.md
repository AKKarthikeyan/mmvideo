# W4-02 Ganga at Varanasi: the popular version, checked (lite: top 3)

Claims come from the pitch and from search-result snippets of tier-X pages (heritage and travel sites, Wikipedia mirrors); none used as evidence.

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Varanasi is the only place where the Ganga flows north | Contradicted | C09, C05 | About a quarter of the Natural Earth line has some northward component; even within 40 km of Varanasi there are other north-quadrant legs (8, 10 km). Varanasi's is notable because the city sits on it, not because it is unique |
| M2 | The Ganga flows from the Himalaya south-east to the sea | Embellished | C07, C05 | True for the river as a whole (source-to-delta bearing 128 degrees), but locally it turns: past Varanasi it runs north for about 15 km |
| M3 | At Varanasi the Ganga flows due north, in a crescent, back to the mountains | Embellished | C05, C06, C04 | It bends: first north-north-west, then north-east along the city (overall 15 degrees). 'Towards the Himalaya' is true only as a direction; it gains 37 km north, then turns east again |
