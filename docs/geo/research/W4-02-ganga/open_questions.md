## Wording risks for AK
- Do not say "the only place the Ganga flows north" (M1, C09): contradicted.
- Do not say "flows back up to the mountains" or "flows backwards": it flows north for a short bend, downhill as always, then east again. "Turns north, towards the Himalaya" is the safe wording.
- Do not explain the bend by religion ("because Shiva..." or "because Kashi is sacred"); give no reason at all unless a geology source is added. Credit any belief to the district page ("the district says it is believed...").
- Do not call the reach "Uttarvahini" or say it is "especially holy" (C12 unverified).
- Avoid "88 ghats" (C10, Wikipedia-derived text on the district page).
- Gangotri's state: do not name it (CWC page still says Uttar Pradesh; K1).
- "Our measurement" and "about" on 15 km and 37 km; never a decimal.
- Map: no border near Nepal on the pull-out.
