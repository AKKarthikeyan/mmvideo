# W4-05-tea: conflicts between sources (lite)

1. **What the Assam plant was.** The 1834 Committee called it "the identical tea of China" [C009]. Fortune (1852) treats "the new China plants" as the fine varieties India should spread [S01, ch. XXII]. Neither uses the later idea of a separate Assam variety. Not resolved here (no botanical source opened).
2. **Tea Board 2024-25 numbers.** The earlier "ESTIMATTED" file gave Assam 656.86 and All India 1294.54 M kg; the FINAL table gives 666.82 and 1315.77. We use FINAL only [C008].
