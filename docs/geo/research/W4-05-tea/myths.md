# W4-05-tea: the popular version, checked (lite: 3 claims)

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | India's tea industry began with tea Robert Fortune took from China (the pitch, read broadly) | Embellished | C001, C002, C005, C006, C008 | Fortune did take 20,000+ plants (his count) for the Company, but to the Kumaon/Garhwal Himalaya. The Company had declared tea native to Upper Assam 14 years earlier, and Assam now grows about half of India's tea. |
| M2 | The British "discovered" tea in Assam | Embellished | C006, C007 | The 1834 letters themselves say Singphos and Khamtis were already drinking it; the British identified it as true tea. |
| M3 | Fortune's China plants founded Darjeeling tea | Unverifiable | C005, C011 | His 1852 book never mentions Darjeeling; it is not settled by our sources either way. |
