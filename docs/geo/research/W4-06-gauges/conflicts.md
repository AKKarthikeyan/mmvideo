# W4-06 Gauges: conflicts between sources

| # | Point | Sources | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Broad gauge width | S01 "1.676m"; S02 "1.6 metre" (also 0.76 and 0.6 for narrow) | S02 rounds. Use S01's 1.676, 1.000, 0.762, 0.610 m |
| K2 | Number of gauges | S01 lists three classes; S02 adds "Standard Gauge - 1.43 metre" | Not a conflict in time: S02 (2026) is later. Say "three classes, four widths" for the historic network; do not build a beat on standard gauge (S02 names no line) |
| K3 | Broad gauge km vs total | S02 70,142 BG km (Mar 2026) is larger than the Economic Survey's total 69.44 thousand route km (2024-25; opened, not cited) | Different dates and bases. Never divide one by the other; no 2026 share is stated |
