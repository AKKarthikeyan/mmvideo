# W4-07 Puducherry: dossier (Shorts Lite, 14 Nov 2026)

Pitch tested: "Puducherry is one place, right? It's four pieces in three states" ⚑ borders (internal).

**Verdict: TRUE, with honest wording.** The Government of Puducherry says the UT comprises four former French establishments, Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam, "scattered in South India" (C01). Puducherry and Karaikal sit on the Tamil Nadu coast, Mahe is surrounded by Kerala, Yanam adjoins Andhra Pradesh (C02-C05). So the four pieces lie within or beside THREE states: Tamil Nadu (two pieces), Kerala, Andhra Pradesh. Careful: the pieces belong to the UT, not to those states ("in three states" = located in). Say "scattered across three states".

Sources (3 P2 + geodata): S01 py.gov.in About, S02 py.gov.in History, S03 statistics.py.gov.in overview, S04 our computation (method/pondy_distances.py). Owners gain nothing from the framing; it is self-description.

## 2. Fresh angles
G7: PASS
1. **One district, three coasts apart** (C07, C08): the official "Puducherry district" contains Puducherry, Mahe and Yanam, which are 911 km apart at the widest (Mahe-Yanam, our measurement). A district whose parts are on opposite coasts.
2. **Mahe is nearer to Kerala's towns than to its own capital** (C03, C08): official page says Mahe is about 70 km from Calicut Airport; the capital is 468 km away (our measurement).
3. (spare) Puducherry itself is bounded by Tamil Nadu "on three sides" (C02).
