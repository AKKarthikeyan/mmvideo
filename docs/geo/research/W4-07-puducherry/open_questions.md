## Wording risks for AK
- Say "three states", not "inside Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Andhra". The UT is its own entity; nothing is a Tamil Nadu district.
- Do not say "enclave" for all four; official page says Mahe is "surrounded by Kerala" and Puducherry bounded "on three sides by Tamil Nadu" (the fourth is the sea).
- Do not draw borders. Pins and town points only; no outlines of the territories unless AK lifts the house rule for the pieces.
- Do not say "French rule ended in 1947" or give 1962/1963 without a treaty source. Safe: "handed to India on 1 Nov 1954 (de facto)".
- Languages Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, English, French (S01) are fine but keep it factual: no claim about communities or colonial legacy beyond the official text.
- Distances are straight lines between town points (our measurement), not road distances.
