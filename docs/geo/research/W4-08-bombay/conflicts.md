# W4-08-bombay: conflicts between sources (lite)

K1. **Which seven.** Gazetteer Vol. I text (Edwardes): Colaba, Old Woman's Island, Bombay, Mazagon, Parel, Varli, Mahim. Its own footnote quotes Govind Narayen (1863): Mahim, Bombay, Colaba, Mahalakshmi, Varli, Mazagon, Sion. MCGM: Colaba, Little Colaba, Bombay, Mazagon, Parel, Worli, Mahim. Not resolved. Say "seven islands" and name only islands that Edwardes and MCGM agree on [C002].
K2. **Sion Causeway:** Gazetteer 1798-1803; MCGM 1802 [C009]. Not used.
K3. **Vellard dates:** Gazetteer "between 1771 and 1784" (Hornby's term); MCGM "1782 ... completed in 1784" [C003]. Use "finished in 1784".
K4. **When the islands became one:** MCGM says unified after Colaba (1838) and the Mahim causeway (1845); the Gazetteer says Colaba was the last remaining island by 1838, and the 1845 causeway joins Mahim to Bandra, i.e. Salsette [C006, C008]. Use 1838.
