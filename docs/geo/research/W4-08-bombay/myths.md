# W4-08-bombay: the popular version, checked (lite: 3 claims)

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Mumbai was seven islands (the pitch) | Confirmed | C001, C002 | Both the 1909 Gazetteer and MCGM say so. The Gazetteer itself prints a different list in a footnote (K1). |
| M2 | The Hornby Vellard (1784) joined the seven islands | Embellished | C003, C004, C006 | It shut out the sea at the breach and let the central flats be reclaimed. Colaba, the last island, was joined only in 1838. |
| M3 | The islands were joined by one big landfill | Embellished | C003, C006, C008 | Joining was a chain of causeways and embankments from 1784 to 1838, then reclamation through the 19th and 20th centuries. |
