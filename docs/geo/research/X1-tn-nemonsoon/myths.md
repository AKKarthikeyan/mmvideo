# X1 Tamil Nadu northeast monsoon: the popular version, checked

The three popular claims come from the pasted brief of 4 Nov 2026. They are listed only to be tested; nothing from them enters the script except through claims.jsonl.

| # | Popular claim | Verdict | Claims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Tamil Nadu is dry in summer and gets its rain only in the winter monsoon | Embellished | C008, C003 | IMD does call the subdivision a rain-shadow region in the southwest monsoon, but its 2023 report puts the normal June-September rain at about 36% (328.5 mm) of the year, more than a third. The honest wording is "a smaller share", never "dry" |
| M2 | The winter monsoon brings Tamil Nadu most of its rain | Embellished | C001, C007 | Normal October-December rain is 48% (442.8 mm of 921.4 mm): the largest share, not most. IMD's own word is "chief rainy season" |
| M3 | The Western Ghats block the summer rain, which is why Tamil Nadu is in a rain shadow | Unverifiable | C003 | True in general geography, but none of our three IMD documents names the Ghats as the cause (they say only "rain-shadow region"). Not in the script. A pinned comment may say it only if AK allows a fourth, non-IMD source |
