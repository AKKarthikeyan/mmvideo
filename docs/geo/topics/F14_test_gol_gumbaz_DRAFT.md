## Conflicts and how the script handles them
1. **Thrust.** Fergusson says the inward weight counters the dome's thrust. Cousens says the dome is "a great rigid concrete shell... with practically no side thrusts of any kind so long as it remains intact". The script says a dome **can** push outward, which both allow, and credits the fix to the masonry as Fergusson does. It does not claim the builders intended it or that it is proven by modern tests.
2. **Tower storeys.** ASI-2 says seven, Fergusson says eight, Cousens gives no count. The script does not mention the towers. They may be drawn (four octagonal corner towers are agreed) but without a floor count on screen.
3. **Date.** Sultan's dates: 1627–57 (both ASI pages). "Built in 1657" (ASI-2) against "Built in 1659 CE" (ASI-1). No source gives a start year. The card says "mid-17th century CE".
4. **Gallery width.** 11 ft (Cousens) against "more than 12 ft" (Fergusson). Not used.

## Wording rules for this script
- Never "unsupported dome". Say "no pillar inside".
- Never "seven echoes" or any exact echo count. "More than ten" is Cousens's wording.
- Never "second largest dome in the world". ASI-2 says it; newer rankings differ and we could not open the paper that settles it.
- "Thirty years" is the reign, not the building time.
