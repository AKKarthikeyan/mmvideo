# GeoTales — Week 1 scripts (2 Shorts a day)

Drafted 20 Oct 2026 for AK's review. **Nothing gets produced until you approve.**

**How to read these**
- `SAY` is the voice line, with numbers spelled out the way they should be spoken. `CAP` is the on-screen caption. `VIS` is the visual.
- Each script runs 9 beats, about 85–95 words, which comes out at 30–35 s at the Siliguri pace. The last line loops back into the first.
- Every claim has a source in the fact table under its script. ⚠ marks a figure that only one source gives, or that sources disagree on: it gets re-checked before voicing.
- The calendar's four time-zone topics were cut to two, to avoid repeats. Xinjiang and Arunachal sunrise are folded into the China and India scripts.
