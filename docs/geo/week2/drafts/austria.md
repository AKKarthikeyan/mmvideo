**Map care:**
- Show the Andaman and Nicobar Islands as Indian territory. If India's outline appears, use the Survey of India official outline (all of Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and Arunachal Pradesh inside India).
- Austria in 1778 was the Habsburg Monarchy, much bigger than today's Austria and with a sea port at Trieste (now in Italy). Beat 1 and 9 talk about today's landlocked Austria, so the glow there is modern Austria. In beat 6, label the port pin "Trieste (Habsburg port)" rather than shading a modern country.
- Pegu is today's Bago in Myanmar; Madras is today's Chennai. Fine to add small "today" labels, but the names in the voice stay historical.
- Sources disagree on the landing date (Novara: 1 April 1778 at Car Nicobar; other accounts say June-July 1778). The script uses only the year.
- The Nicobarese were living on the islands throughout. Say nothing that implies the islands were empty.
