**Map care:**
- Berenike sits just north of the Halaib Triangle, which Egypt and Sudan both claim. Don't draw the Egypt-Sudan border near Berenike. Keep the coast as a plain satellite view with no border lines in that area.
- If India's outline appears, use the Survey of India official outline. Better: a satellite base with no modern borders, and pins only on the Kerala (Malabar) coast and Berenike.
- The pepper jar: Cappers reports that a pottery specialist (R. S. Tomber, by personal communication) thought it "highly likely of Indian origin". The script does not say the jar was Indian-made. Only the pepper is called Indian.
- The "Korran" shard is a Roman (Mediterranean-type) amphora, not an Indian pot. Draw it as a plain broken jar. Don't invent its letters: trace from a public-domain drawing only, or use abstract strokes.
- Malabar coast = Kerala today. The ancient port names (Muziris etc.) are not used, so no need to place them.
