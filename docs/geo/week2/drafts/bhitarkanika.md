- **"7 m / 23 ft crocodiles" and the Guinness record:** Whitaker (CSG Newsletter 2008) says the 7 m stories lack evidence ("we need evidence"). I could not read Guinness's own page. Possible extra beat if AK wants it: "India's largest known saltwater crocodile skull came from this river" (CSG Newsletter 2008: "appears to be the largest C. porosus skull in India"). ### Map care
- Use India's official boundary (Survey of India) for the opening zoom. If Bangladesh's coast shows at the edge of the Bay of Bengal frame, keep the boundary plain, with no labels or callouts.
- Bhitarkanika is in Kendrapara district, Odisha. Label the park, and keep it apart from Gahirmatha (the turtle beach next door), which is not part of this story.
- Draw the Brahmani and Baitarani as the two named rivers. The Dhamra river mouth is nearby (JBNHS 1973 mentions it). If it shows on the map, leave it unlabelled so it isn't read as a third "meeting river".
- Show the 1975 project as a generic badge. Don't use the UN emblem or flag, which has rules on its use.
- Show crocodiles as drawn silhouettes. No photos unless public domain and checked, and no CC BY-SA images. No attack scenes.
