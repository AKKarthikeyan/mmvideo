**⚠ Big caution for AK:** the premise itself is disputed. Jadunath Sarkar (1934), the standard historian of the period, says no Maratha soldier went past the Chenab and calls the Attock claim "an ignorant boast". Hari Ram Gupta (Panjab University, 1961) answers him with contemporary Persian chronicles and Marathi letters, which put Maratha posts at Attock and Peshawar until November 1758. I could read both books, but not the original Persian chronicles or the Peshwa Daftar letters (SPD vols. 2, 21, 27). So the script says "may have" and gives the dispute its own beat (beat 8). If AK wants a story with no dispute, the Lahore campaign of April 1758 is firm in both books.
