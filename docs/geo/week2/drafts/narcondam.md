**Map care:**
- Show the Andaman & Nicobar Islands inside India's official map (Survey of India outline). The full India outline may appear in the pull-back.
- The Coco Islands just north of the Andamans belong to Myanmar. Either leave them unlabelled or label them "Myanmar", and don't colour them as Indian.
- Draw no sea boundaries in this Short; there's no need for them.
- Narcondam's position: N13°27′53″ E94°16′41″ (UNESCO entry). Measure the beat 3 ruler from the east coast of North Andaman to Narcondam, and label it "100+ km", not an exact figure.
- Show nothing about the police outpost on the island. No military or radar imagery.
- Draw the hornbill from reference in code. Don't trace a photo, and use no CC BY-SA images.
