Notes for AK:
- HS 6309 is *all* worn clothing, not only woollens. The script says "used clothes" to stay true. The "woollens" angle in the topic title is not supported by a figure I could read.
- Beat 6 rule comes from a 2025 tribunal order on clothes moving from the Kandla SEZ into India. It describes the rule; it is not a new notification. The current DGFT import schedule for chapter 63 could not be opened (a 2010 DGFT notification lists worn clothing as "Restricted").
