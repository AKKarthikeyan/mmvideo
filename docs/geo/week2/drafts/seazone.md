**Map care:**
- Land: India's official political map (Survey of India), including all of J&K and Ladakh.
- Draw the EEZ as a soft blue glow. Where India's zone meets a neighbour's, don't draw a hard outer line. India has agreed sea boundaries with several neighbours, but not with Pakistan: the Sir Creek and Arabian Sea boundary is unsettled. Fade the zone out well before the Gujarat–Sindh coast and don't draw a line there.
- Don't draw or label any other country's EEZ or claims, and leave out Chagos.
- Use 200 nmi rings only as an illustration of the rule. Where neighbours are closer than 400 nmi (Sri Lanka, Maldives, Myanmar, Indonesia, Thailand, Bangladesh), the real zone stops at the agreed boundary, so let the rings fade out instead of overlapping other countries' land or sea.
- No navy ships, flags or military icons. The cargo ship is a plain merchant vessel.
- On the bar in beat 8, label the islands' land share "under 1%" and draw it as a thin sliver. Don't print a precise percentage.
