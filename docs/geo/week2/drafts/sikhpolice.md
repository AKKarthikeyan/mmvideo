**Map care:**
- Lahore is in Pakistan today. Before 1947 Punjab was one province of British India. Label "Lahore (now Pakistan)". Don't draw the 1947 partition line in this Short.
- If India's modern outline is shown at all, use the Survey of India official outline. Simpler: show city pins and route lines on a satellite base with no modern borders.
- Hong Kong was a British colony; Shanghai's International Settlement was one district of Shanghai, not the whole city. Highlight only a small central area, labelled "International Settlement", not all of Shanghai.
- Bombay is today's Mumbai. Small "today" label is fine.
- Neutral tone: no "loyal martial race" language, no praise or blame. The script states who was hired, when and why the record says so.
- The Shanghai "Sikh" branch also included some Punjabi Muslims in later years (Jackson, *Modern Asian Studies* 2012, footnote, read only in the abstract/footnote view). The script says "Sikh officers" because that is the SMC's own column heading.
