Notes for AK:
- The "why" in the script is the British government's own stated reason (beat 5): taking the king away removes the focus of opposition. It is a quote, not our judgement. I left out the Imperial Gazetteer's harsh words about the king, because they are the colonial view.
- Optional extra detail, not in the script: the first plan was **Ranipet**, near Madras (telegram No. 169). It could replace beat 7 if AK prefers a surprise to a distance.
- The palace: work began in 1907–08 (Bombay report). Maharashtra Tourism says it was built in 1910 ("believed to"). I left the build year out of SAY and CAP.

### Map care
- Use **India's official map** (Survey of India) for India's boundaries. Show the India–Myanmar border as a thin neutral line. Label the country "Burma" with "(today's Myanmar)".
- In 1885–86 Burma was being joined to British India, and from 1937 it was a separate country. Don't draw any 1885 border as if it were today's. Use today's map with the historical place names as labels only.
- The sea route Rangoon → Madras is in the 1885 telegram. The Madras → Ratnagiri leg was not read at a source, so draw it as a plain dotted arc with no ship track or route detail.
- Show the Andaman and Nicobar Islands as part of India where the Bay of Bengal is in frame.
- For the palace, use a drawn silhouette or our own photo. No CC BY-SA images. No photo of the king unless it is in the public domain and checked.
