Notes for AK:
- "Space rockets" here means sounding rockets: research rockets that fly up and fall back, not ones that reach orbit. Beat 8 does not name the rocket or its maker (AK, 25 Oct 2026).
- Title wording: the brief says the rocket "launched from a church". The sources say the church was the lab, office and assembly room, and the launch was from TERLS (the launch station on the same site). So the titles and hook say "assembled in a church" and "began inside a church", not "launched from a church".
- Left out because I could not read them at a primary source: the nose cone carried by bicycle; "90 acres" and "183 families" (news only); the 2025 land-title report (news only); the Bishop's name in the SAY. The name is in Kalam 2005 ("Rev Father Peter Bernard Pereira was the Bishop"), so it could go in a CAP if AK wants it.
- Dates that disagree but are not in the script: TERLS set up in 1962 (VSSC Genesis) or 1963 (ISRO Sounding Rockets). The church was handed over in 1962 (Kalam 2005).
- Tone: the script says only that the parish gave the church and a new one was built. It makes no claim about land titles, compensation disputes or the fishing community's later concerns.

### Map care
- Use India's official boundary (Survey of India) on the opening map. If Sri Lanka shows in the frame, keep the maritime area plain, with no boundary lines or labels.
- **The magnetic equator is not a fixed line.** It drifts over the years. Draw it as an approximate curve labelled "magnetic equator (approx.)" that passes near Thumba, and don't place it exactly on Thumba (VSSC says the station is "slightly off the magnetic equator"). Keep the geographic equator (0°) clearly separate, far to the south of India.
- Thumba is on the coast just north of Thiruvananthapuram city. Launches go out over the Arabian Sea (Aravamudan 2020: "Launch pads were built facing the Arabian Sea").
- Draw the church and the Bishop's House as plain outlines. Don't use photos unless public domain and checked, and no CC BY-SA images. Don't add religious symbols beyond a plain church outline, and keep the tone neutral.
- Don't put the ISRO logo or emblem on screen.
