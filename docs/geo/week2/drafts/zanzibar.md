**Map care:**
- Zanzibar is part of the United Republic of Tanzania. Show it inside Tanzania; don't colour it as a separate country.
- If India's outline appears (Gujarat pin), use the Survey of India official outline. Simpler: satellite base with pins and route lines only.
- Not verified at a primary source, so keep out of CAP and VIS: "Indian doors have rounded tops, Omani doors are rectangular", and "brass studs come from Indian doors that stopped war elephants". Wikipedia, a newspaper and travel sites say these, but I found no primary source. Don't draw the studs as "Indian" or label any door shape as "Indian".
- The Minneapolis Institute of Art's door (acc. 98.46a-f) is marked Public Domain on its record and is a safe reference for drawing. No CC BY-SA photos.
- Stone Town was also a major slave-trading port (ICOMOS). This Short doesn't cover that. If a door with chain motifs is drawn, don't use it as decoration.
