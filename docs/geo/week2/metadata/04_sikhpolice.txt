TITLE:
Why Sikh Police Patrolled Shanghai and Hong Kong

DESCRIPTION:
Under British rule, Hong Kong recruited Sikh policemen from Punjab from the 1860s. Shanghai's International Settlement hired 16 Sikh constables in 1884, and by the end of 1936 its police had 558 Sikh officers.

The figures come from the Shanghai Municipal Council's own annual reports.

Sources: Shanghai Municipal Council annual reports (1885, 1936); Hong Kong Police Force.
