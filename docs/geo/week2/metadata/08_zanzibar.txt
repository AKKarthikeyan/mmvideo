TITLE:
Why This African Town's Doors Look Indian 🚪

DESCRIPTION:
Stone Town on Zanzibar, in Tanzania, has over 500 carved doors. In the 1800s, Indian Ocean trade made its Indian, Swahili, Arab and African merchants rich, and Indian traders built houses like the havelis of Gujarat.

A door showed its owner's wealth. Curving Indian and Omani styles spread in the late 1800s.

Sources: UNESCO World Heritage nomination and ICOMOS evaluation, Stone Town of Zanzibar; Purdy (Penn State).
