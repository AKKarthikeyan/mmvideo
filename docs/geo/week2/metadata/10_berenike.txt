TITLE:
7.5 kg of Indian Pepper, Buried in Roman Egypt

DESCRIPTION:
At Berenike, a Red Sea port of Roman Egypt, archaeologists found a jar holding over 7.5 kg of Indian black pepper. Monsoon winds carried ships here from India's Malabar coast, and caravans took the goods across the desert to the Nile and on to Rome.

Indians lived here too: a jar shard carries a name in Tamil-Brahmi letters.

Sources: Cappers, Roman Foodprints at Berenike (UCLA Cotsen Institute, 2006); Sidebotham et al., German Archaeological Institute (2025); University of Delaware.
