TITLE:
Why America's Old Clothes End Up in India 🧶

DESCRIPTION:
In 2024 India imported about 160,000 tonnes of used clothing, 58% of it from the USA. Many end up in Panipat, about 90 km north of Delhi, a town of weavers now called the "cast-off capital".

Customs makes sure the clothes are cut up first. They are sorted by colour, shredded into fibre and spun into new yarn, an old English method called shoddy.
