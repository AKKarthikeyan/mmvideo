🏝️ This Indian island is 3× closer to the Maldives than to Kerala.

Minicoy is 398 km from Kochi, but the Maldives begin about 130 km to the south. Its people even speak Mahl, the Maldives' language.

## 02 · austria

⛵ Landlocked Austria once reached islands that are now part of India.

In 1778, a ship backed by the Habsburg empire arrived in the Nicobar Islands. The outpost failed after about three years.

Which other surprise colony should we map next? 👇

## 03 · kuttanad

🌾 In Kerala, farmers grow rice below sea level.

Much of Kuttanad's farmland was once the floor of Vembanad Lake. Walls of mud keep the water out, much like the Dutch polders. The UN's FAO lists it as world farming heritage.

Would you farm two metres below the sea? 👇

Poll: Yes! / No way

## 05 · seazone

🌊 India has rights over more than 2 million km² of sea.

A UN treaty gives every coast up to 200 nautical miles. Islands count too: the Andamans, Nicobars and Lakshadweep add a huge share.

Did you know islands give India this much sea? 👇

## 06 · nainsingh

📿 In 1866 a man entered Lhasa counting every step on prayer beads.

Nain Singh's rosary had 100 beads, not 108. Each bead meant 100 paces. His count helped fix Lhasa on the map.

Could you count every step for a year? 👇

## 07 · thumba

🚀 India's first space rockets were assembled inside a church.

Thumba in Kerala sits near the magnetic equator, perfect for rocket science. The parish gave its church to science, and the first rocket flew on 21 Nov 1963. The church is now a space museum.

Been to this space museum? 👇

Poll: Yes / Not yet

## 08 · zanzibar

🚪 This African town has over 500 carved doors, and some look Indian.

In Stone Town, Zanzibar, Indian traders built houses like Gujarat's havelis. A grand door showed a rich owner.

Have you seen a door like this in Gujarat? 👇

## 09 · barmer

🛢️ In 2004, a desert gave India its biggest onshore oil find in decades.

Under Barmer, Rajasthan, an ancient rift lake's mud slowly turned into oil. It is waxy, the fingerprint of an old lake.

Did you know Rajasthan had oil? 👇

Poll: Yes / No

## 10 · berenike

🏺 A jar buried in Roman Egypt held over 7.5 kg of Indian pepper.

Monsoon winds carried ships from the Malabar coast to Berenike on the Red Sea. A shard there even carries a name in Tamil-Brahmi letters.

Which Indian spice would you have shipped to Rome? 👇

Poll: Pepper / Cardamom / Cinnamon / Turmeric

## 11 · panipat

🧶 In 2024, India imported about 160,000 tonnes of used clothes, 58% from the USA.

Many reach Panipat, the "cast-off capital", where they are cut, shredded and spun into yarn for new blankets.

Where do your old clothes go? 👇

## 12 · bhitarkanika

🐊 In 1973, a survey in Bhitarkanika saw just 10 saltwater crocodiles.

Today over 1,800 live there, thanks to a rescue project that began in 1975 and decades of protection.

Would you take a boat through these creeks? 👇

## 13 · thibaw

👑 Burma's last king died in exile in a small Indian seaside town.

After 1885, King Thibaw was sent to Ratnagiri in Maharashtra, about 2,400 km from Mandalay. He died there in 1916.
