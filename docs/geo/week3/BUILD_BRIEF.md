## 1. Script (most important)

Then write the script:

The topic's original pitch in `docs/geo/week3/TOPICS.json` is NOT a fact: if the research says it is false or overstated, the Short tells the honest version, and that honest version can be the hook.
- **Length:** 6-9 beats, 85-95 spoken words. The voiced and trimmed length must be 25-32 s.
- **Hook:** beat 1 is the hook, a surprising verified fact in 1-2 s.

- **Ending:** the last beat loops back to the hook.
- **Numbers spoken:** write years and numbers in words in `say` (e.g. "nineteen twenty-eight"). Captions use digits.
- **Audience:** 70% India and 30% global, so a non-Indian must follow. Say "Kerala, in south-west India" the first time where it helps.
- **Format:** pick one from the playbook `docs/geo/INDICA_ATLAS_PLAYBOOK.md` (sections 3 and 6) and the story formats F1-F13 named there.

## 3. Scene spec
Rules:
- Read the "Rules" section of `docs/geo/week2/SPEC_BRIEF.md`: map care, coordinates, screen safety, motion, credit.
- Read the `Spec`/`Layer` types at the top of `src/geo/engine/GeoShort.tsx`.

- `src/geo/cyclone/odisha.ts`.
- Use `.push` for icons. Icon names are in `ICONS` in `src/geo/engine/motion.tsx`.

Lessons from today's renders:
- **Map edges:** satellite imagery covers 0–150°E and 25°S–75°N only. Anything west of 0°E, or north of 75°N, is black. Europe west of 0° (UK, Spain, Portugal) cannot be shown on the map.
- **Camera span:** `span` is the number of degrees of longitude across the 1080 px width. So x = 540 + (lon − cam.lon) × 1080 / span.
  - Keep every label and pin inside x 80–1000, and work it out for the camera of that beat.
  - Labels are about 24 px per character wide, centred on `at`.
- **Close-ups:** there is no close-up imagery for Week 3 (`hi: false`). The texture is 40 px per degree, so keep `span` at 4 or more, or it goes soft.
  - Things smaller than a few km (e.g. Baarle's enclaves) cannot be seen on the satellite map. Draw them as a labelled SCHEMATIC from official geodata in the research folder, or show them in panels and text.
- **Bigtext:** never put "=" in it. A decorative bar crosses it, and it reads as "≠".
- **Fills:** low-alpha dashed fills are invisible. Use alpha 0.25 or more with no dash.
- **Bars:** the `bars` layer wraps long unit strings. Use the `timeline` panel (`{y: value, t: label}`) to show exact figures.
- **Numbers:** every number on screen must be in a caption or a claim. Credit each one to its source when sources disagree.
