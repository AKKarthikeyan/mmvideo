TITLE:
The Border Treaty That Says "The Limit Is Interrupted" 🗺️

DESCRIPTION:
The 1843 treaty that set the border between the Netherlands and Belgium stops at one town, Baarle. There it says the limit "is interrupted", because an 1842 treaty had promised the status quo would stay. Today the official maps of both countries show 30 enclaves: 22 Belgian pieces inside Dutch land, and 8 Dutch pieces inside Belgian land. Six of those Dutch pieces sit inside the largest Belgian enclave.

In 1892 the two governments agreed a land swap to end the puzzle, but the Belgian bill to approve it lapsed in 1894. Baarle-Hertog (Belgium) and Baarle-Nassau (Netherlands) are still two municipalities. The enclave map in this Short is a schematic, enlarged from official geodata.

Sources: Netherlands–Belgium boundary convention of 8 August 1843 (Tractatenblad / wetten.overheid.nl); treaty of 5 November 1842, Art. 14; Kadaster (PDOK) municipal boundaries; Digitaal Vlaanderen (VRBG); Belgian Chamber of Representatives, bill 1892-1893 no. 197.
