TITLE:
Colachel 1741: The Dutch Captive Who Led Travancore's Army ⚔️

DESCRIPTION:
In 1741 the Dutch East India Company occupied Colachel, on the coast of the kingdom of Travancore. That year Travancore's forces attacked the Dutch post. Dutch letters say a red-hot ball fired their gunpowder and the garrison surrendered; Travancore's histories tell it as a battle won.

The two sides' records differ, even on the day. One captured Dutch officer, Eustachius De Lannoy, went on to train and lead Travancore's army; his tomb says he served the king for about 37 years. The peace treaty came years later.

Sources: Galletti, The Dutch in Malabar (1911, Dutch letters translated); Nagam Aiya, Travancore State Manual (1906); Shungoonny Menon, History of Travancore (1878).
