TITLE:
Only 1 Hectare in 18 of Kerala Lies Idle. India? 1 in 5 🌴

DESCRIPTION:
Kerala, on India's south-west coast, leaves very little land idle. In the 2023-24 land-use statistics (provisional), only 5.6% of its reported land was barren, pasture, culturable waste or fallow, about 1 hectare in 18. Across India the figure was 21.0%, about 1 in 5. Punjab (3.0%) and West Bengal (3.5%) have even less.

In the 2011 census, Kerala had 860 people per square kilometre and just one village where nobody lived. Land under non-agricultural uses (buildings, roads, water) grew about 16% from 2014-15 to 2023-24 (p), and 27.8% of the state is classed as forest, a legal class that need not be wooded.

Sources: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Land Use Statistics at a Glance 2023-24 (provisional) and land-use definitions; Census of India 2011, Table A-01.
