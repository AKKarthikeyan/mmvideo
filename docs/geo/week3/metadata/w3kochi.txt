TITLE:
Kochi's 1341 "New Deposit": A Date in Old Deeds, Not a Proven Flood 📜

DESCRIPTION:
Old deeds in Kochi, Kerala, were dated in the Puthuvaippu ("new deposit") era, which began in AD 1341. The records tie it to Vypin, an island of river silt, appearing from the sea, or perhaps only being first settled. Cochin Port's own history says it "is believed" heavy Periyar floods formed the harbour then; the old records we read do not mention a flood.

The deep harbour came much later. Harbour engineer Robert Bristow arrived on 13 April 1920 and is credited with the channel through the sand bar. The bar was cut on 30 March 1928, and the first ship, SS Padma, came through on 26 May 1928.

Sources: Cochin Port Authority (history, timeline, milestones); Jacob Canter Visscher, Letters from Malabar (1723); C. Achyuta Menon, The Cochin State Manual (1911).
