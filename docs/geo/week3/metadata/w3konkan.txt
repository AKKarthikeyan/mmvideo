TITLE:
India Couldn't Afford This Coast Railway in 1969. It Opened in 1998 🚆

DESCRIPTION:
In August 1969, India's Railway Minister told Parliament that a Konkan line from Apta to Goa, about Rs 60 crore, could not be considered "at present" because money was short. The first 62 km, Apta to Roha, opened only in March 1986.

In 1990 a new company, the Konkan Railway Corporation, was formed; Sweden's aid agency Sida says it was set up to raise the money. Given four and a half years, it took about seven years and four months, slowed most by soft-soil tunnels in Goa. The whole line, Roha to Thokur near Mangaluru (738.941 km, 91 tunnels), opened on Republic Day, 26 January 1998. Route on the map is simplified.

Sources: Rajya Sabha written answers (11 Aug 1969; 27 Jun 1980), Ministry of Railways; Konkan Railway Corporation Ltd (The Beginning, Salient Features, Annual Report); Sida Evaluation 97/25 (Karlis Goppers, 1997).
