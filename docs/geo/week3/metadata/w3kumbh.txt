TITLE:
Kumbh Mela "Every 4 Years"? The Real Gaps Follow Jupiter 🪐

DESCRIPTION:
UNESCO's description says the Kumbh Mela is held at Prayagraj (Allahabad until 2018), Haridwar, Ujjain and Nashik "every four years by rotation". The recent run was 2013, 2015–16, 2016, 2021 and 2025: gaps of two, one, five and four years.

Indian government pages give the rule they follow: the city is set by Jupiter's sign, with the Sun's. Haridwar's Kumbh needs Jupiter in Aquarius; Nashik and Ujjain share Jupiter in Leo, which is why both are called Simhastha. We counted Jupiter's position with NASA JPL data in the Indian (sidereal) zodiac, and the last five dates match those rules. We report the rule as the pages state it; we take no view on it.
