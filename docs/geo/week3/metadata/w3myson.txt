TITLE:
Shiva Temples in Vietnam: What My Son's Records Say 🛕

DESCRIPTION:
My Son, in central Vietnam, holds Shiva temples of the Cham kings of Champa. UNESCO dates this temple culture from the 4th to the 13th century. Among the oldest inscriptions known to Louis Finot (1904) and R. C. Majumdar (1927), a king founds a temple to Shiva; Finot reads it as probably wooden, burned, then rebuilt and renamed by a later king.

When Shiva worship reached the Cham is not known, says ICOMOS. ICOMOS recommended the word "introduction"; UNESCO's World Heritage Committee (1999) wrote "an indigenous society adapting". From 2017 to 2022, India's Archaeological Survey restored three groups of temples (Government of India; My Son Management Board).

Sources: ICOMOS Advisory Body Evaluation 949 (1999); UNESCO World Heritage Centre, My Son Sanctuary, and Committee decision (1999); L. Finot, BEFEO (1904); R. C.
