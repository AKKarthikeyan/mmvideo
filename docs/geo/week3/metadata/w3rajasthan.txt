TITLE:
Rajasthan Is Bigger Than All but 6 Countries in Europe 🏜️

DESCRIPTION:
Rajasthan, India's largest state, covers 342,239 km² (Census of India 2011). On the UN's list of Europe, only Russia, Ukraine, France, Spain, Sweden and Germany are larger.

Germany is about 4.5% bigger and Finland about 1.1% smaller, and no European country falls between them. Norway depends on the definition: its mainland is smaller, but with Svalbard and Jan Mayen it is bigger. Every area is total area (land plus inland water), measured on one basis.

Sources: Census of India 2011 (Survey of India area); UN Statistics Division, Demographic Yearbook 2022 and M49 regions; Destatis; Statistics Finland; Statistics Norway; Eurostat.
