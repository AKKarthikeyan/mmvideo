TITLE:
If India's States Were Countries: Bihar ≈ Mexico, Rajasthan ≈ Germany

DESCRIPTION:
Split India into its states and set them beside the world's countries, and 12 of the 50 most populous would be Indian states (2026, projected). Bihar, in eastern India, has about as many people as Mexico, on about one-twentieth of the land.

Maharashtra is projected to have more people than Japan; in 2011 it was the other way round, as Japan has been shrinking. Rajasthan matches Germany almost exactly in people and in land. State figures are India's official projections for 1 July 2026, not census counts.

Sources: National Commission on Population, Technical Group population projections (2019), Table 11; UN DESA World Population Prospects 2024 (medium variant, 1 July 2026); land areas measured on Natural Earth.
