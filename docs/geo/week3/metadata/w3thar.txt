TITLE:
11% of Rajasthan's Land, Under 1% of Its People: Why? 🏜️

DESCRIPTION:
Jaisalmer, in the Thar Desert of north-west India, is Rajasthan's largest district. In the 2011 census it had 17 people per sq km, less than a tenth of the state average of 200, and it was the least dense of Rajasthan's 33 districts (2011 boundaries). It is sparse, not empty: 669,919 people lived there in 2011.

The main limit is water. Normal rainfall is only about 185 mm a year, no river flows all year, and groundwater can lie up to about 124 m down, often saltier the deeper you go (Central Ground Water Board).

Sources: Census of India 2011, District Census Handbook Jaisalmer and Table A-02 (Rajasthan); Central Ground Water Board, Jaisalmer aquifer report (2017).
