TITLE:
Uttar Pradesh: UK-Sized Land, the World's 6th-Biggest Crowd 🇮🇳

DESCRIPTION:
Uttar Pradesh, in north India, covers about 240,928 km², within about 1% of the United Kingdom's area. But India's official projection puts its 2026 population at about 243 million, roughly 3.5 times the UK's.

Counted as a country on one basis (1 July 2026, UN World Population Prospects 2024 for countries), it would rank sixth in the world: just below Pakistan, about level with Nigeria. In the 2011 census, one in six Indians lived in Uttar Pradesh. India's next census is due in 2027, so every 2026 figure here is a projection.

Sources: Technical Group on Population Projections (National Commission on Population, 2019); UN World Population Prospects 2024; Census of India 2011; World Bank / FAO surface area.
