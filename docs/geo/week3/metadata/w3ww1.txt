TITLE:
A Million Indians Went to WW1. Most Didn't Go to France

DESCRIPTION:
By the Government of India's own count, 1,096,013 Indians were sent overseas in the First World War (to the end of 1919). About 43% were not soldiers but labourers and followers. Over 61% went to Mesopotamia, now Iraq; about 13% to France.

More than half of the dead in the Government of India's table were in Mesopotamia. How many died depends on who counted: 61,041 (Government of India, 1923), 62,056 (War Office, 1922) or about 74,000 (Commonwealth War Graves Commission).

Sources: Government of India, India's Contribution to the Great War (1923); War Office, Statistics of the Military Effort of the British Empire (1922); Commonwealth War Graves Commission.
