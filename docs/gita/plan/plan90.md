# Gita in Ink: 90-day content plan (27 Oct 2026 – 23 Jan 2027)

Two long videos a week and three Shorts cut from each (78 Shorts, one a day except Saturday). Every idea was rated by Jev (jev-latest) on search demand, evergreen value, Gen Z appeal, format fit, Shorts potential, crowding and controversy risk; raw scores are in `jev_longs.jsonl` and `jev_shorts.jsonl`, and the calendar is `plan90.csv`.

**Tuesday: the chapter series.** The 15-minute overview (ready, v2) opens the channel, then one chapter a week, chapters 2–13. The overview's end card promises this series ("starting with the one that answers overthinking"); chapters 14–18 follow after day 90.
**Saturday: the strongest evergreen topics**, chosen by demand = 0.5 search + 0.3 evergreen + 0.2 Gen Z appeal, among ideas with a format fit of at least 0.75. Ideas that repeat a chapter episode inside the 90 days were left out.
**Shorts:** four candidates were written for each long video, each anchored on one verse with chapter.verse in the title (the format with the best engagement in the research). Jev kept the best three on hook, stand-alone strength and search interest, with one rule on top: no verse gets a second Short in these 90 days (78 Shorts, 78 different verses). Shorts post Wed–Fri after a Tuesday long and Sun–Tue after a Saturday long.

Every verse cited in this plan was checked by code against the Sanskrit corpus (sanskritdocuments.org, 701-verse edition: chapter 13 numbers run one ahead of Gita Press).
