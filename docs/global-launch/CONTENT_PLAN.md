# Moat Atlas: content plan (first 12 weeks)

Drafted 18 Oct 2026.

**Niche:** where the money in AI and chips actually goes.

**Audience:** English-speaking investors in the US, UK and Europe.

**Rhythm:** 1 long video a week (Tuesday) plus 3 Shorts cut from it (Wednesday, Friday, Sunday). Results weeks add a second long video within 48 hours of the report.

Earnings dates below are each company's usual timing. **Confirm every one on the company's investor-relations calendar** before booking the week.

## 1. Four pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Share of long videos | Main formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earnings Breakdown** | One company's quarter, out within 48 h: where the money went, what changed, what management guided. | ~40% | Sankey or waterfall, $1 split, guidance range |
| **Follow the Dollar** (flagship) | One dollar traced across companies. Example: a cloud company's AI spending → Nvidia → TSMC → ASML / SK Hynix. | ~30% | Cross-company Sankey, mix bar, indexed lines |
| **Atlas Entry** | One company's moat, from its filings: what protects it, what it earns from that, and what could break it. Later carries a global MoatSCORE. | ~30% | Mix bar, waterfall, peer dumbbell, trend lines |
| **One Number** (Shorts) | One surprising number from that week's long video, in under 45 s, ending on a question that the long video answers. | 3 a week | $1 split, mix bar, guidance range (vertical versions) |

## 3. Titles and thumbnails

- **One number and one question.** "TSMC keeps 51¢ of every dollar it sells. How?", not "TSMC Q3 2026 earnings analysis".
- **No hype words** (COLLAPSE, SHOCKING, CRASH), no buy/sell language, and no price targets.
- **Thumbnails:** company name, one number, four words at most, plus the $1 bar or one chart shape. Use the same layout every week so viewers recognise it.
- **Calendar quarters in titles** ("Q3 2026"), with the fiscal quarter in the description, so Nvidia's "FY27" doesn't confuse anyone.
- **Every video description:** sources, the exchange rate used, a link to the data, and "Research and script: Moat & Margin research team. Narration: synthetic voice. Information, not investment advice."
