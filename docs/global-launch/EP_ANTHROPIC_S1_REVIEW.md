## S-1 facts missing that matter (add or consciously drop)
1. Cost split by function: cost of revenue 14.6% of revenue, R&D 59.8%, sales and marketing 23.4%, G&A 6.5% (L21977-22030). Best single fact for the margin question.
2. Q2 2026 operating income was positive (+974 on 11,555); Q1 2026 was a 1,667 loss on 4,731 (L8896-8959). The H1 "-4%" hides this.
3. Growth expected to decline, "may not be sustainable" (L3743, L3910, L7337).
4. Founders' Class F: 50.1% of votes; "controlled company"; trust plus founders elect all directors (L150-152, L162, L884).
5. Cloud providers are suppliers, investors, customers, and (Google) rivals; moving workloads would be costly (L4206-4225).
6. Take-or-pay, fixed, ordered 12 to 18 months ahead (L2029-2033, L7687).
7. Upcoming stock-pay catch-up: RSUs with a performance condition that vests when the registration statement becomes effective; the amount is blank (L1595-1600, L1350-1352). This is the likely reason GAAP margin gets worse at listing; one line next to the adjusted-margin beat would help.
8. Revenue through cloud marketplaces is booked gross (L20081), so cloud fees sit in costs, not netted from revenue. Minor, but explains part of the 55 cents.
9. Safety-release risk: the S-1 says it has delayed model releases (Mythos, Project Glasswing) and that such measures "could slow our capability gains relative to competitors" (L1852). Directly relevant to a speed moat.
10. Model-price deflation: Opus 5.5 cut customer cost per task by about 40% vs Opus 5 (L741-742); relevant to NRR and margin.
