# Moat Atlas: YouTube keywords (global companies, for investors)

Drafted 20 Oct 2026. **No search volumes yet.** This environment can't reach YouTube autocomplete, Google Trends or the keyword tools: suggestqueries.google.com, youtube.com and trends.google.com are blocked by the network policy. Web searches turned up guides, not finance volume lists. So this is a keyword *map* built from the niche, the calendar and the US trends report. `keywords.csv` lists all 412 keywords with empty `yt_volume` and `competition` columns, for vidIQ to fill (section 7).

## 1. The niche in one line

*How the world's big companies make money, explained simply from their filings: for people who invest, or want to.*

Viewers search in three ways, and every video should catch at least two of them:
1. **Learn:** "how does Costco make money", "Netflix business model", "what is an economic moat".
2. **News:** "Nvidia earnings", "SpaceX IPO", "Anthropic IPO". These spike for a few days around each event.
3. **Compare:** "Visa vs Mastercard", "Tesla vs Toyota".

The channel avoids the fourth way people search, "should I buy X". It's crowded, it's advice, and it's outside YouTube's rules for a synthetic voice.

## 4. Title formulas that carry the keyword

Put the keyword first, in the words people type, then the Moat Atlas hook:

- **How does *X* make money? Explained Simply** (the Moat 60 house style)
- **Where Does Your *Y* Money Go?** (Follow the Dollar)
- ***A* vs *B*: Who Keeps More of Every $100?** (head to head)
- ***X* Earnings: Where Every Dollar Went (Q3 2026)** (results week)
- **What Is an Economic Moat? *X* Explained** (Atlas Entry)

Stay within the launch-plan rules: no hype words, no buy/sell language, at most 70 characters, and the keyword inside the first 40.

## 5. Tags and hashtags

**Channel keywords** (Studio, Settings): how companies make money, business model explained, economic moat, company analysis, earnings explained, income statement explained, global companies, biggest companies in the world, investing education, follow the dollar, Moat Atlas

**Tag template per video** (8 to 12 tags, most specific first):
1. primary keyword;
2. 2–3 secondary keywords;
3. company name;
4. company + earnings;
5. company + business model;
6. cluster tag (economic moat / follow the dollar);
7. how companies make money;
8. Moat Atlas.

**Shorts hashtags** (3 at most): #howcompaniesmakemoney #*company* #earnings, or #moat on Atlas Entry Shorts.

## 6. Keywords to avoid

- "stocks to buy now", "*X* price prediction", "*X* stock forecast", "best stocks 2026". These are advice-led, very crowded, and ruled out by the channel's no-advice line.
- "follow the money": a "Follow the Money" channel already exists, so use "follow the dollar".
- Hype words in keywords (crash, collapse, shocking), even if they rank.

## 7. Check the volumes yourself (about an hour)

1. **vidIQ or Keyword Tool:** paste the `keyword` column of `keywords.csv` in bulk, set the region to United States, and fill `yt_volume` and `competition`.
2. **Google Trends** (United States, past 12 months, **YouTube Search** filter): compare the P1 company explainers in batches of five, with "how does costco make money" as the anchor. Also run the five-year seasonality check on retail terms ahead of Black Friday.
3. **YouTube autocomplete:** type each primary keyword and note the suggestions. Add any new phrasing to the CSV.
4. **Keep, swap or demote.** Keep a keyword at P1 if it has a steady volume and competition below the vidIQ midpoint (vidIQ's guide suggests new channels aim for about 300–5,000 monthly searches with difficulty under 40). Swap the title keyword if a variant beats it. Demote anything near zero to tags only.
