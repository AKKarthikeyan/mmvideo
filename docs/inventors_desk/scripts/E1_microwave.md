## Self-check
- **Followability:** one line (1945 → Spencer → popcorn → how → myth → try-it → today). About 7 numbers (1945, 12, 1947, billions, few cm, ~20 s, 96/100). One term, "magnetron", explained in the same breath. A reminder at b11. Pauses after the key facts. *Retell test:* "A self-taught engineer noticed radar waves melted his chocolate. Microwaves flip water molecules so fast they heat up. You can even measure light's speed with chocolate." ✓
- **Nolan:**
  - Pledge (melted chocolate) → turn (popcorn) → prestige (the speed of light from chocolate).
  - The chocolate is the hairpin (b01 ↔ b11–13).
  - Rule: "it isn't fire, it's waves".
  - Twist: the inside-out myth.
- **Bhagyaraj:** an ordinary hero (a mill boy, self-taught) who stays ordinary; the plant/payoff of the chocolate; warmth in the comment question. No impossible choice; a light evergreen doesn't need one.
