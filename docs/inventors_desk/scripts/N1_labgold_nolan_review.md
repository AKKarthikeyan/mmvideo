## Three highest-leverage fixes (all visual, no new voice read)

**Fix 1: the hairpin loop (tools 11 and 14). Highest leverage.**
- In the last ~1.5 s, after "…someone you love?", the **MADE ring melts into a silver bead**.
- The bead rolls left into the thermometer bulb.
- The final frame matches frame 0 of b01 ("MERCURY into GOLD?").

The Short now loops seamlessly: the viewer's choice dissolves back into the question that started it. Cost: one art transition in `TwoRings`, plus matching the last frame to `ThermoBead`'s start pose.

**Fix 2: two clocks that converge (tools 1, 4, 12).**
- From b04 to b12, run a thin strip under the top bar: **THEN** (a tiny flickering furnace, with a year counter running 300 → 1600 → 1941) | **NOW** (a tiny reactor ring, 2026).
- At "Mercury becomes gold" (b12), both halves flash gold together, and the strip collapses into the main shot.

The 2,000-year race and today's claim become one shape that meets at the turn. Cost: one small strip component; the cues already exist (`at("b04")`, `at("b12", "Mercury becomes gold")`).

**Fix 3: plant the clock early (tools 5 and 10).**
- In b01, add a small mono line under the date: "SAFE TO HANDLE: ????". It stays dim through the chase.
- At b13 ("…around twenty forty four") it fills in as **2044**.

This is a fair-play plant: the viewer saw the question in second 1 and gets the answer at the twist. Cost: one Mono element with two states.
