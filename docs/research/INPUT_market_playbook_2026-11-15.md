Four unmet needs: fatigue with pop-gurus (r/AdvaitaVedanta recommends Swami Sarvapriyananda and Swami Tadatmananda); the gap between pseudoscience clickbait and dismissive mockery; comparative and sectarian confusion (Advaita vs Kashmir Shaivism, Atman vs Buddhist non-self, Mimamsa, Dvaita/Vishishtadvaita/Advaita; meditation and Advaita for OCD/anxiety/ADHD); unedited podium lectures and long multi-part series being hard to follow (Epified's 45+ episode Mahabharata).

Five pillars:
1. Primary scriptural and evidence rigour (exact citations, on-screen Devanagari).

3. Under-represented texts (Ashtavakra Gita, Avadhuta Gita, Drg-Drshya Viveka, Mandukya Karika, Katha and Chandogya Upanishads, Chitrakavya).
