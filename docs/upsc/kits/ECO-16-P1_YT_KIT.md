## Reply under the pinned comment (post a day later)
```
Answer: (c) 2 and 3 only.
1 is wrong: notes and coins are legal tender and cannot be refused; a cheque can be refused, so a deposit is money but not legal tender (NCERT Class 12 ch.3).
2 is correct: NCERT counts demand deposits, which can be withdrawn on demand and paid by cheque, as money.
3 is correct: NCERT defines M4 as M3 plus post office deposits (except NSCs), so it is wider than M3.
```
