## Reply under the pinned comment (post a day later)
```
Answer: (b) 1 and 2 only.
1 is correct: NCERT 3.3 says a loan opens a new deposit in the borrower's name, so the money supply rises by that deposit.
2 is correct: NCERT's example uses a 20% reserve ratio, and the multiplier is 1 ÷ 0.2 = 5.
3 is wrong: RBI's rates panel showed a CRR of 3.00% on 3 November 2026 (NCERT Class 10's "about 5 per cent" is a dated textbook figure for cash held by banks).
```
