## Reply under the pinned comment (post a day later)
```
Answer: (b) 1 and 3 only.
1 is correct: NCERT says whether credit is useful depends on the risks in the situation and on whether there is some support in case of loss.
2 is wrong: NCERT's text says "about half", but its own Graph 1 shows formal lenders at 66% (51 + 10 + 5), and the NSO survey's rural figure is 66%.
3 is correct: NCERT says that after a year or two of regular saving, a group becomes eligible for a loan sanctioned in the group's name.
```
