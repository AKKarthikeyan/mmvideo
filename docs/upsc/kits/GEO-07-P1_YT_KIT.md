2. NCERT Class 11 says a single theory fully explains the monsoon.

## Reply under the pinned comment (post a day later)
```
Answer: (b) 1 and 3.
1 is correct: NCERT Class 11 (India: Physical Environment, ch.4) describes the south-east trades crossing the equator and turning into the south-west monsoon.
2 is wrong: NCERT says no single theory has fully explained the monsoon.
3 is correct: Gadgil (2018) shows hotter land in June-September went with weaker monsoon rain over 1950-2014.
```
