Consider these statements:
1. IMD's present normal date for the monsoon to cover the whole country is about 8 July.
2. Tamil Nadu gets most of its annual rain from the south-west monsoon (June to September).
3. Every El Niño year has brought a drought in India.
Which are correct? (a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer and reasons in the replies. Sources are in the description.

## Reply under the pinned comment (post a day later)
```
Answer: (a) 1 only.
1 is correct: IMD's new normal (2020) puts it at 8 July; NCERT's "mid-July" is the old 1901-1940 normal.
2 is wrong: by IMD's figures Tamil Nadu gets about a third of its rain in June-September and almost half in October-December, from the north-east monsoon.
3 is wrong: 1997 was a strong El Niño year, yet India's monsoon was normal.
```
