**What a newcomer can realistically reach (real benchmarks)**

- **Small channels can break out.** Channels with 1K–30K subscribers got 40K to 1.24M views on single videos about states, infrastructure and geography. Examples:
  - Angel One Economics: Karnataka vs Tamil Nadu, 1.24M views at 30.5K subscribers.
  - Next Infra: freight corridor, 310K views at 4.7K subscribers.
  - Uplearn: Gujarat, 596K views at 19.9K subscribers.
- **Map channels grow fast.**
  - XploreMap Nitish reached 80K subscribers in about 5.5 months on Shorts.
  - Base IAS grew 1,169% in a year with map-led UPSC videos.
  - The Graphic Earth has 349K subscribers from only 81 long videos, averaging about 562K views each.
- **Money:**
  - Long-form ads pay about ₹40–160 per 1,000 Indian views, so 1M views a month earns about ₹0.4–1.5 lakh. Shorts pay almost nothing.
  - Sponsorships at 100K–500K subscribers run about ₹1–6 lakh per integration.
  - The real income is sponsors plus your own products: notes, map packs and a newsletter.

## 2. Gaps to fill: opportunities for a newcomer

The big players are huge but serve other needs. Exam brands (StudyIQ about 20.5M subscribers, Drishti about 12.8M, Mrunal about 2.05M) make long lectures that lead into paid courses. School channels (Magnet Brains about 14.4M, not growing) teach on whiteboards. General explainers (Dhruv Rathee, Think School) are personality-led and political or business-focused.

| # | Gap | Evidence it's open | How a newcomer wins it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hindi and regional data-and-map explainers** | Hindi results for "Indian economy" are TV-news clips and 3–11-hour exam marathons. No Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi or Gujarati economy or geography explainer of any size was found. | 8–15-minute visual explainers in Hindi, then Tamil and others. Use multi-language audio on the same video, or separate language channels. |
| 2 | **Geography joined to economics ("why here?")** | UPSC channels cover the physical side only; broker channels cover the economic side only. Western Ghats got 978K views; BBC Hindi's Himalaya-and-economy video got 2.9M. | Link cause to effect: monsoon, crops, rural demand; Western Ghats, rain, coffee, power; coal belt, poverty. |
| 3 | **State-vs-state and region economics outside English** | English broker channels proved it: state videos get 13–50× their other content. Rivalry videos draw 2K+ comments. | Data-led head-to-heads in Hindi and Tamil, on pairs Angel One hasn't done. |
| 4 | **The under-covered East and North-East** | Guwahati 265K views (102× that channel's usual), Odisha 539K, Bihar videos break out on ThePrint and exam channels. | A regular series on Odisha, Bengal, Bihar, Jharkhand and the North-East. |
| 5 | **The 2026–27 data reset** | New GDP base (Feb 2026, back series by Dec 2026), NFHS-6 for 715 districts (May 2026), Census 2027 count (Feb 2027). Incumbents cover these as text notes and slide talks. | Be first with clear, sourced video versions; they become reference videos for years. |
| 6 | **New NCERT syllabus as short visual chapters** | NCERT merged Class 9 Economics and Geography into one new book for 2026-27, so old chapter videos are out of step. A 1.8K-subscriber channel got 100K views on one new chapter. Class 10 chapters get 44K–117K searches a month. | 6–12-minute map-and-data videos titled with the exact chapter name and class. |
| 7 | **Neutral, sourced credibility** | Top explainers are read through a political lens; AI map channels are already among the breakouts. | Real data with the source on screen, no party politics, no stock tips, a public corrections log. |

**Avoid:**
- the exam-lecture race (PhysicsWallah alone runs 206 channels)
- Shorts-only channels (big reach, almost no income)
- political framing (blocking, legal notices, sponsors staying away)

**Map rule:** every India map must match Survey of India boundaries. A non-conforming map is a criminal offence.

## 3. Content calendar

**Weekly rhythm**
- Sunday flagship, 10–16 minutes.
- Wednesday syllabus or search explainer, 6–10 minutes, at 7 pm IST.
- 3–4 Shorts cut from each long video.
- "Data Day" videos within 24–48 hours of RBI decisions, GDP releases, the Economic Survey and the Budget.

**Series**
- **Built from Space**: satellite before/after of infrastructure.
- **Rival States**: data-led state head-to-heads.
- **Why Here?**: geography explaining economics.
- **NCERT in Maps**: syllabus chapters.
