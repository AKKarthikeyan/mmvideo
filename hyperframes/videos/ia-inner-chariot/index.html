WHAT THE TEXT SAYS, WHAT TRADITION SAYS
1
THE CHARIOT IS A GIFT
Vibhishana offers the Pushpaka; at the end Rama sends it back to Kubera (Yuddha 121, 127).
2
THE ROUTE HAS STOPS
The vimana lands at Bharadwaja's hermitage and then at Bharata's at Nandigrama (Yuddha 124–127).
3
THE DATES ARE FESTIVAL
Dussehra to Diwali is how the festivals tell it. It is tradition, not a day count we can read off these chapters.
