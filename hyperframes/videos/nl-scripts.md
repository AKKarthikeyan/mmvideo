# Then vs Now Shorts: narration scripts (English)

Silent Shorts, 2012 vs 2025, NASA Black Marble annual night lights. Keep to about 2.5 words a second. Timings are mm:ss from the start of each Short.
The wording makes **no claim about how much the lights changed**: the Suomi NPP data steps up in 2021-23 and the size of the change depends on the satellite (see `hyperframes/README.md`). Do not add a percentage or "doubled".
The RBI line is about the **correlation** between night-light growth and the state's official economic growth, from the RBI Bulletin, October 2026, Table 3. Say "RBI researchers", not "the RBI says".
Hold Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh until their boundaries are checked.
