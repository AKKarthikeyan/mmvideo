# mm-gold-lenders: conflicts between sources and the script

Each item: the versions, with source and tier, and how the script should handle it. Nothing here is resolved by the research role.

- **K1. Did gold "fall hard" before September?** Script ch.1, 3, 7 say so. Muthoot (P4, company claim): its valuation price was Rs 10,566 (Sep-25), 12,201, 13,441, 12,942 (Jun-26), 13,547 (Sep-26): up 28% on the year, Sep-26 the highest of the five [C7]. WGC (P4): Q3 domestic average Rs 1,48,891 per 10 g, down 1% on Q2, up 45% on a year; "continued to ease from its January peak" [W3, W4]. IBJA (P4) [F1]: Rs 1,46,406 per 10 g on 19 Oct, Rs 1,25,123 on 23 Nov (about -14.5%), so the November fall is real and the 30 Sep figures predate it. A January peak price was not readable. Handling: say "in recent weeks" (F1); the "this year" fall stays UNVERIFIED (U1).
- **K2. "At a record" in Q3.** Script ch.9 vs WGC "continued to ease from its January peak" [W4]. Handling: reword.
- **K3. Is gold-loan growth slowing?** RBI (P2): bank gold-loan growth decelerated but stayed strong [R6]; RBI (P2): NBFC gold-loan growth accelerated [R6b]. Muthoot (company claim): guidance raised by a further 10% [C12]. IIFL (company claim): gold AUM +13% in the quarter, "primary growth driver", while saying growth "moderates" [C9]. Manappuram (company claim): "outlook for the gold loan business remains positive" [S03 PDF p.2]. Handling: ch.7 "stage one" must say banks, and the NBFC data points the other way for September. Do not clip the IIFL quote.
