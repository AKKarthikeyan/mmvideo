## Notes on specific files

- `jensen/with_eu_borrell_2024.jpg`: With Josep Borrell, then EU foreign-policy chief (2024). Name him if he is visible on screen.
- `jensen/gtc2010_cuda_x86.jpg`: The slide announces PGI's CUDA-x86 compiler (a partner product, not NVIDIA's). Use it as a GTC 2010 stage shot from the CUDA years; don't caption it as an NVIDIA launch.
- `nvidia/gtc2010_flops_slide.jpg`: NVIDIA's own 2010 GPU roadmap slide (Tesla, Fermi, Kepler, Maxwell) photographed at GTC 2010. Dark: lift exposure before use.
- `gpu/nv1_1995_chip.jpg`: Die photo of the NV1 (STG2000X), NVIDIA's first chip (1995). Check the year before captioning.
- `datacenter/titan_supercomputer.jpg`: Titan supercomputer, Oak Ridge National Laboratory. Check its GPU details before captioning.
- `story/dennys_restaurant.jpg`: A Denny's restaurant, not the San Jose one where NVIDIA was founded in 1993. Label it "a Denny's" on screen.
