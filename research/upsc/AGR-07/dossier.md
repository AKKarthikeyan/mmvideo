# AGR-07 dossier: MSP, the price that decides what farmers grow

Research only. Read 2 Nov 2026. Claim ids point to `claims.csv`. Current figures are in `docs/upsc/living/AGR-07.csv`.

## 1. What the topic really is, and the order to meet it

MSP, the minimum support price, is a price the Government announces before sowing for a listed crop. Government agencies stand ready to buy that crop at that price, so the farmer has a floor under a price that would otherwise swing. The price also steers choices: a crop with a surer, higher price gets planted. That is the episode's title idea, and CACP says it has tried to tilt price incentives toward pulses and oilseeds for this reason (Summary S3 of its 2025-26 report; D04).

Order a learner must meet the ideas in:
1. **The problem:** prices fall after a good harvest, and a farmer cannot wait. NCERT's setting: marketed surplus and the Green Revolution (N04). The NSS defines MSP as market intervention to insure producers against a sharp fall (para 2.68, L14).
2. **Who sets it:** the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP), an advisory body set up by a 1965 Ministry resolution, recommends; the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs decides (L01-L04).
3. **What it covers:** 22 crops: 7 cereals, 5 pulses, 7 oilseeds, 3 commercial. Sugarcane has a separate Fair and Remunerative Price. Vegetables and fruit have none (L02, L06, L09).
4. **How the number is built:** cost concepts A2, A2+FL, C2; the 2018-19 Budget principle of at least 50% over cost; CACP's other factors (F01-F10).
5. **How a price becomes a purchase:** FAQ quality standard, FCI and State agencies, open-ended paddy and wheat buying, then the Price Support Scheme for pulses and oilseeds (P01-P11).
6. **What it reaches:** how much is bought, where, from how many farmers (P12-P18, C01-C12).
7. **What it does to the system:** stocks, bonuses, crop choice, and the debates (D01-D10).

## 2. Hard parts and misconceptions

* **MSP is not a market price and not a promise to buy everything.** Paddy and wheat from registered farmers are bought open-endedly if they meet FAQ (P02). Pulses, oilseeds and copra are bought only when market price falls below MSP, at a State's request, within a ceiling of 25% of national production (tur, urad and masur excepted) (P05). In rice, 38.8% of production was bought in TE2024-25 (P16). In June 2026 wheat, gram and lentil sold *below* MSP in the market (D05).
* **Which cost?** The Government's stated "1.5 times" applies to **A2+FL** (paid-out costs plus the value of family labour). CACP computes **C2** too (adds rent on owned land and interest on fixed capital) but, per the Ministry's 2020 reply, uses it only as a benchmark (F03). Worked out from CACP's tables: paddy KMS 2026-27 MSP is 150% of A2+FL and about 113% of C2; wheat RMS 2027-28 is about 206-207% and about 141% (PIB prints a 106% margin, CACP Table S.1 prints 207%, our division of rounded figures gives 206.5%) (F08-F09). This is our arithmetic; label it so.
* **One national price, different States.** The cost used is an all-India weighted average (F06). State costs differ widely (paddy A2+FL from Rs 1,030 to Rs 3,707 a quintal, F10), and fees and bonuses differ (D02-D03).
* **"Only 6% of farmers benefit"** is the most quoted number and the weakest. It is the Shanta Kumar committee's own sum, for wheat and paddy sales to agencies, from 2012-13 data in a January 2015 report (C01-C02). It is `CORRECTED` (C12): newer official measures count different things (paddy farmers 17.2% of paddy holdings in 2024-25; 14.5-18.5% of paddy-selling households and 9.7% of wheat-selling households in 2018-19). The Ministry itself says a true count is "difficult to assess" (C08). Do not swap in a new single number.
* **Survey and records disagree** on the same facts (C09): in Punjab paddy, FCI records show 88.4% of production bought in 2018-19, the NSS estimate 31.1%. CACP wants NSS data checked against records.
* **OCR and units:** rice (milled) and paddy figures sit in different sources (P12 and P13). Never mix.

## 3. Debates, with who holds which view (named positions read at source)

* **Government (Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare):** MSP for 22 crops, 50% minimum margin on all-India average cost, procurement and PM-AASHA; asked in Lok Sabha on 28 Jul 2026 whether it will legislate a guarantee, the reply listed existing measures and did not announce a law (L13). It also recorded that the National Commission on Farmers' 50% recommendation was not carried into the National Policy for Farmers 2007 (F11). *Quote, do not characterise.*
* **CACP (advisory commission):** recommends reviewing open-ended paddy procurement, capping it in surplus States that pay bonus, ending crop-specific bonuses, and asking for trade policy that keeps pulse and oil prices remunerative (D01-D02, D05).
* **Shanta Kumar committee, Jan 2015:** price support works mainly in wheat and rice in a few States; hand procurement to experienced States, focus FCI on the eastern belt, use warehouse receipts and cash transfers (D06-D07). Recommendations only; the Government's response not read.
* **National Commission on Farmers (Swaminathan), 2006:** MSP at least 50% above weighted average cost of production (F11). What cost concept it meant is **not settled at source** (F12).
* **Farmers' organisations and political parties on a legal guarantee:** **not read at source, so not recorded** (O05).

## 4. What changed since NCERT was written

NCERT (revised 2023, reprinted 2026) has one paragraph in Class XI and one sentence each in two Class X books. Since then: MSP is tied to 1.5 times cost since 2018-19 (F01-F02); PM-AASHA was created on 12 Sep 2018 and continued to 2025-26 (P07-P08); three farm laws were passed in 2020 and repealed in 2021 (L10); a committee on MSP was announced on 19 Nov 2021 (L11); 100% purchase of tur, urad and masur for four years from 2025-26 (P06).

**Current position at 2 Nov 2026:** paddy (common) MSP Rs 2,441 a quintal for KMS 2026-27 (13 May 2026); wheat Rs 2,585 for RMS 2026-27 (bought in 2026) and Rs 2,610 for RMS 2027-28 (announced 30 Sep 2026). Paddy procurement 835.10 LMT in 2025-26 as reported on 28 Jul 2026; wheat 33.8 mt by 15 Jun 2026. Next announcements are not made yet: kharif MSP expected about May-June 2027, rabi about end September-early October 2027 (pattern from the last two years only).
