## Cross-cutting

1. **b14.s5 is the one FAIL:** the dropped Article 279B was a proposed GST Dispute Settlement Authority, not extra power for the Council. The ledger (D03) has it right; the script drifted. The picture card and caption need the same fix.
2. **'Recommends' vs 'decides/sets':** b08.s1 and b09.s3 describe the Council as deciding. The Constitution says 'recommend' (279A(4), (5)) and Mohit Minerals holds the recommendations persuasive, so the script contradicts its own punchline at b14 unless these are fixed.
3. **The Court's words:** 'cooperative and collaborative federalism' is not the Court's own pair for GST; 'cooperative' is. The Court's pair is 'cooperative and uncooperative'. b15 and b16 both need it.
4. **Dates:** b03 gives the right pair (assent 8 Sep 2016, 279A in force 12 Sep, other Constitution changes 16 Sep). Only the word 'rest' over-reaches. b12 has the right midnight date (30 Jun/1 Jul 2017).
5. **Our reasoning labelled:** b10 'our working' is labelled and the arithmetic holds. b02 and b16.s2 are our summaries and need the label.
6. **Short quotes:** the voice and captions quote nothing longer than a few words ('harmonised national market', NCERT's box title).
