## What NCERT does not teach
Tax incidence and shifting (impact vs incidence, elasticity); the structure of Articles 246A, 269A, 279A and the Seventh Schedule edits; GST Council voting rules; Mohit Minerals; the Compensation Act (14%, base year 2015-16, Fund, options, back-to-back loans); the input-tax-credit set-off order; agricultural income and the capital asset exclusion; the current GST rates; Budget numbers for GST. All of these come from the publishers (see claims).

## NCERT statements that disagree with the publishers or are out of date (see claims)
- Box 5.3 "six standard rates (0, 3, 5, 12, 18, 28)" is out of date since 22 Sep 2025 (E12-H06, CORRECTED).
- p.80 "indirect taxes are regressive in nature - they impact all income groups equally": loose reason (E12-A10, CONFLICT).
- Box 5.3 "tobacco attracts both GST and central excise": current position not checked (open item).
- 5.1.2 lists service tax, excise and customs as the indirect taxes; footnote 1 corrects part of it (E12-A07).
- 5.1.3 still uses the plan / non-plan classification (E12-B05).
- Box 5.3 "GDP is expected to rise by about 2%" is an expectation from 2017, not a measured result (E12-E10).
- Typos seen: "101th Constitution Amendment Act" (Box 5.3), "legistation" (footnote on p.70).
