**PICTURE:** A loan agreement on a desk: four stamped boxes light in turn: INTEREST RATE, DOCUMENTS, REPAYMENT, COLLATERAL (a small house and a tractor sit beside the last, with a padlock).  
**VOICE:** NCERT defines credit, or a loan, as an agreement. The lender supplies money, goods or services. The borrower promises to pay later. Every loan has terms of credit. The interest rate. **PICTURE:** Swapna's field in two seasons: a thin harvest with a moneylender at the gate; a small safety net stretched under the field in the second panel; a balance tips from RISK to SUPPORT.

**PICTURE:** A circle of women and men on a floor mat with a savings box and a passbook; coins drop in; a bank officer opens a file marked GROUP LOAN; a counter shows the NABARD figures at the end.  
**VOICE:** A typical self-help group has fifteen to twenty members. Each saves a small amount regularly, from twenty-five rupees to a hundred or more. After a year or two of regular saving, the group becomes eligible for a bank loan in the group's name. NCERT says banks lend to such groups even without collateral, because the group is responsible for repayment. On the thirty-first of March 2024, NABARD counted about one hundred and forty-four lakh groups with savings linked to banks. About seventy-seven lakh were also linked to bank credit.  
**SCREEN:** a typical self-help group: 15–20 members · saves ₹25–₹100 or more each · after a year or two, eligible for a bank loan in the group's name · banks lend without collateral; the group is responsible for repayment (NCERT) · NABARD, 31 Mar 2024: ~144 lakh groups savings-linked · ~77 lakh credit-linked

### b14 · *THEREFORE* · rung 3 · new term: UPI
**PICTURE:** A four-box chain of icons with a flowing coin: PAYER'S APP, PAYER'S BANK, NPCI (a lock for the PIN check), PAYEE'S BANK; a phone with a QR scan at the left.  
**VOICE:** NCERT describes UPI, the Unified Payments Interface. The National Payments Corporation of India, NPCI, launched it in 2016. It moves money between bank accounts using a QR code or a phone number. The steps: the payer's app, the payer's bank, then NPCI, which checks the UPI PIN and processes the payment. Last comes the receiver's bank.  
**SCREEN:** UPI: launched 2016 by NPCI · QR code or phone number · payer's app → payer's bank → NPCI (checks UPI PIN, processes) → payee's bank (NCERT Class 7)  
**CLAIMS:** ECO16-N52 (CONFIRMED)
