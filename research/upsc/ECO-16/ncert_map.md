## What NCERT does not teach (needs order 2 or 3 sources)

- Today's CRR, SLR and what banks may count as SLR assets; NCERT uses a made-up 20% and says Class X banks hold "about 5 per cent" cash (ECO16-N15, N32 against R05 to R07).
- The currency-deposit ratio (named in Key Concepts, never taught): ECO16-N27, taught in IGNOU (T07).
- The real money multiplier formula with currency: IGNOU (T07).
- That central-bank staff now argue that lending creates deposits and the multiplier story is the wrong way round: ECO16-T01 to T06 (CONFLICT, name who holds which view).
- Who said what in the quantity theory and in Keynes on demand for money: IGNOU and the original texts (T10 to T15). Shared with ECO-15.
- Deposit insurance (DICGC), RBI's promise clause, syndicated lending (past paper), and every current figure (R, I and P rows).
