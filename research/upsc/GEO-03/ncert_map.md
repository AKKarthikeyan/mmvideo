## What NCERT does not teach (needs the publishers)
Moment magnitude; the current IS 1893 map; NCS and the National Seismological Network; ITEWC's name and role in the older books; any Bhuj toll beyond the school box; the Disaster Management Act as an Act; tsunami levels (Warning/Alert/Watch).

## NCERT statements that disagree with the publishers (see claims)
- Richter scale 0-10 (G3-A9). Bhuj 6.9 (G3-A60). 2004 magnitude 9.0 (G3-A45).
- Class 11 p.59: wave speed "more in shallow water" (G3-A37, wrong way round); India "volunteered to join" an international system, and Class 7 p.35 "no early warning in the Indian Ocean": both out of date (G3-A51).
- "tidal wave" (G3-A33). Largest tsunami "150 m" (G3-A40). India deaths ">10,000" (G3-A48). Class 11 "more than 300,000" (G3-A47). "Disaster Management Bill, 2005" (G3-A63). Five zones against BIS four (G3-A23).
- Indira Point "completely submerged": no source (G3-A49).
- Typos: "Banda Ache" (Aceh), "Lightening" (Table 6.1), "Aquatc".
