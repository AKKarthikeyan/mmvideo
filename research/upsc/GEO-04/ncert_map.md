# GEO-04 NCERT map: Volcanoes

Read 3 Nov 2026. Page numbers are printed page numbers. PDFs saved in `sources/ncert/` (SSD only, LOCAL_ONLY.md). Each was compared with NCERT's live file on 3 Nov 2026 (same byte size).

## Main chapters
**Class XI, Fundamentals of Physical Geography (kegy2, code 11092).** First edition March 2006; **Rationalised Edition December 2022**; reprinted March 2024, June 2024, December 2024; page footers read "Reprint 2026-27" (front pages, kegy2.ps).
- **Ch3 Interior of the Earth (kegy203), printed pp.18-26.** Direct and indirect sources; earthquake and waves; structure (crust, mantle, "Volcanoes", core); Volcanoes and Volcanic Landforms (shield, composite, caldera, flood basalt, mid-ocean ridge; intrusive forms); exercises. The volcano material is pp.23-25.
- **Ch5 Geomorphic Processes (kegy205), printed pp.37-46** (Unit III opener on p.36). Endogenic processes (diastrophism, volcanism pp.38-39); exogenic processes, weathering, mass movements, erosion and deposition, soil formation.
- Rationalisation (BookletClass11.txt, 25 May 2022): in Ch3 only the activity "Locating an Epicentre" was dropped. The old Ch5 "Minerals and Rocks" (full chapter) and the Class XI soils chapter ("Chapter 6: Soils", India: Physical Environment) were removed. Ch5 pp.38-39 still says volcanism and igneous rocks were "dealt in detail ... in the preceding chapter": a stale reference.

## What NCERT does not teach
Hotspots (word search "hot spot": 0 hits in kegy202, kegy203, kegy204, iest102), how magma forms (melting), water vapour as the main volcanic gas, eruption size scales (VEI), lava domes, lahars, Narcondam, an official definition of "active", tephra size classes, any year-by-year eruption record.

## NCERT statements that disagree with the publishers (see claims)
Kola "in Arctic Ocean" (V039); ridge ">70,000 km" (V017); "Integrated Ocean Drilling Project" (V041); lava v magma wording (V003); gas list (V005); asthenosphere "to 400 km, main source of magma" (V036, UNVERIFIED); typos "chlorene", "lacolith", "Pinatoba", "El Cion".
