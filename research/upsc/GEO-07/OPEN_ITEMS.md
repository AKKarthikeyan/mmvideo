## A. Facts to find

1. **Jet stream and the monsoon burst (M022).** NCERT Class XI (*India: Physical Environment*, ch. 4, p. 31) says the westerly jet withdraws from north India, an easterly jet sets in along 15°N, and this is held responsible for the burst of the monsoon. Is this cause accepted today? Our only reading so far: Schiemann et al. 2009 put the median date of the westerly jet's move north of the Tibetan Plateau at 28 April, a month before Kerala onset. Need a review paper on jets and onset.
2. **New normal dates (M030).** On what date did IMD formally adopt the new onset and withdrawal normals? Its notice returned 404.
3. **Kerala onset criteria (M035).** When did IMD adopt them: 2006 (IMD report RR 3/2020) or 2016 (IMD FAQ)?
4. **Rainfall records (M057).** What are IMD's station normals for Cherrapunji, Mawsynram and Jaisalmer? Do IMD or WMO call Mawsynram the wettest place in the world? NCERT says over 1,080 cm for the first two, highest in the world for Mawsynram, and rarely more than 9 cm for Jaisalmer.
5. **El Niño (M076).** NCERT *India: Physical Environment* p. 31 says the warm current is replaced by the cold Peruvian current. NCERT *Fundamentals of Physical Geography* p. 80 says warm water replaces the cool Peruvian current. Which is right? Also, is NCERT's 10°C rise off Peru correct, and was onset delayed by 5-12 days in 1990-91?
6. **Indian Ocean Dipole (M074-M075).** Can a positive IOD offset El Niño's effect on the monsoon? Also need the original paper, Saji et al. 1999.
7. **IPCC AR6 (M084).** Check against the published WG1 Chapter 8, not the draft:
   - high confidence that the South Asian monsoon weakened in the late 20th century, dominated by human-made aerosols
   - medium confidence that its rain will increase
   - high confidence that observed changes were limited over much of the 20th century, because greenhouse gas increases and aerosol decreases offset
8. **Agriculture's share (M085).** What share of workers does the latest MoSPI PLFS report give? IMD says "nearly half" of the population, NCERT Class XI says about 64%, and NCERT Class X says two-thirds.
9. **Extreme rain in central India (M083).** We have one source for a 75% rise. Is there a second, such as an IMD paper or a *Mausam* article?
10. **Drought year.** How does IMD define an all-India drought year?
11. **Kolkata.** What is IMD's new normal onset date for Kolkata? It is not in the IMD city table we read.
12. **Old Indian texts (M089).** NCERT Grade 9 (p. 52) says Kalidasa's *Meghaduta* (5th century CE) notes the onset date over central India and traces the monsoon clouds. It also cites *Krishiparashara* and *Brihatsamhita* on rainfall prediction. Do the original texts support this?
13. **Deleted NCERT text (M092).** What exactly did the 2022 rationalisation remove from Class XI ch. 4? This needs the 2006 edition.
14. **Class 9 Part 2.** Does NCERT Grade 9 *Understanding Society* Part 2 (`iest2`) add anything on the monsoon? We have not opened it.

## B. Disputes: which version should we teach?

15. **Cause of the monsoon (M010).** Land-sea heating (NCERT) or seasonal migration of the ITCZ (Gadgil 2018, Geen et al. 2020)?
16. **Role of Tibet (M024).** Plateau heating (IMD reports) or the mountains acting as a barrier (Boos and Kuang 2010)? We have not read Boos and Kuang 2013 or Wu et al. 2012.
17. **What "normal" means (M049-M050).** IMD's 2025 report calls 108% of the long-period average "normal", but IMD's April 2026 forecast table puts 105-110% in "above normal". We also have 90-110 against 96-104 as the normal range. Which is current?
18. **North-east monsoon season (M064).** October to December (IMD) or October to February (NCERT Grade 9)?
19. **Season months (M065).** NCERT Grade 9: winter December to early April, summer April to June, monsoon June to September, post-monsoon October to December. IMD statistics: winter January-February, pre-monsoon March-May, monsoon June-September, post-monsoon October-December. Which do we teach?

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jets and the burst | Not a proven cause. The jets change with onset (Ding 2007). Kept as CONFLICT |
| 2 | New normal dates | Closed. Issued 15 Apr 2020, in use from 1 June 2020 (PIB). Some IMD pages still print the old 15 July |
| 3 | Kerala onset criteria | Closed. 2006 (Circular 1/2006). The FAQ's 2016 is the Andaman and Nicobar criteria |
| 4 | Rainfall records | Closed and CORRECTED. Cherrapunji 11,175.8 mm (1991-2020). Jaisalmer 236.9 mm, so NCERT's "rarely more than 9 cm" is wrong. WMO lists Mawsynram only as Asia's record for average annual rain, so do not say "wettest place in the world" |
| 5 | El Niño | CORRECTED. Warm water replaces the cool current. Drop the 10°C and the 1990-91 delay: no source |
