## Cross-cutting

1. **Who holds the view.** The recurring risk is a plurality, a lead opinion or one judge presented as 'the Court': b08 (arbitrariness is Bhagwati J.'s lead opinion in Maneka Gandhi), b13 (Indra Sawhney's lead judgment, not a tallied bench), b15 (the 50% reasoning is Maheshwari J. alone; Trivedi and Pardiwala JJ. do not discuss it), b06 (Kesavananda: seven judges). b16 is handled well (Gavai J. named, 'his opinion', three agreeing).
2. **Wrong law attached to Art 17 (b10 s4, FAIL).** The Atrocities Act 1989 is not the law that punishes untouchability; the Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955 is. The PCR text is already saved (`sources/indiacode_pcr_1955.txt`) but has no ledger claim. Add one. The same slip would repeat in any exam card.
3. **The 50% rule's origin (b14).** The 1993 OM says on its face it follows Indra Sawhney, and Art 16(4B) of the Constitution itself mentions 'the ceiling of fifty per cent'. The script should credit the Court, not the order.
4. **Dropped qualifiers that change meaning:** 'public' in Art 15(2) (b00, b09); 'in the opinion of the State' in Art 16(4) (b11, b17); 'Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes' in Art 335 and the CJI's view that Art 335 is not a limitation (b12); 'only' in Art 15(1).
5. **Our readings must be labelled ours:** b12 s1-s2 ('start level'), b17 s2 ('the text says no'). The production notes already say so; the voice does not.
6. **Sensitive material (caste, reservation) stays inside the record** wherever the sentences name the judge or bench (b14, b15 s5-s6, b16 s4-s5). No sentence asserts a social fact about any community beyond what the judgments say. The 59.5% sum is safe: the Court (Pardiwala J. para 74) prints the same total.
7. **NCERT attribution** is right wherever 'NCERT' is used (b02, b08 s3-s5, b11 s4-s5, b17 s4). Two lines state an NCERT-only fact as plain fact: b03 s3 (1928) and b18 s2 (Machal Lalung).
8. **Ledger gaps to close before voice:** PCR 1955 claim (b10); Indra Sawhney paras 93-96 and conclusion (4) as the 50% claim (b14); Art 15(5) for 'seats' (b01); a tally for Indra Sawhney if 'the Court upheld' is kept (b13). Janhit's 3:2 date 7 Nov 2022 appears only on screen (year), fine.
