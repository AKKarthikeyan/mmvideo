## Cross-cutting

3. **Our readings stated as fact:** b04.s6 ('no one can be forced to sing'), b07.s4, b09.s3-s4 ('the Constitution's own answer'), b14.s5 and b18.s9 ('constitutional right'), b15.s2 ('Neither').

6. **Partial lists** (b03 Art 25(1), b11 Art 33, b13 Art 41) are fair and left as PASS.
