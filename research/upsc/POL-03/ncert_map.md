## Where NCERT editions differ, are dated, or are wrong (each has a claim)

1. **Persons vs citizens (A12).** hees2 p.147 says fundamental rights are for 'every Indian citizen'. The text of Part III is mixed: Arts 14, 20, 21, 22, 25 and others are for every person; Arts 15, 16, 19, 29 are for citizens. CORRECTED.
2. **Right to Education year (D17).** iest1 p.141 says Art. 21A was added in 2009. It was the 86th Amendment of 2002, in force 1 Apr 2010 (the RTE Act is of 2009). CORRECTED.
3. **Rule of law gloss (D11).** iest1 p.140 glosses "procedure established by law" as strict adherence to valid laws. Since Maneka Gandhi (1978) the procedure itself must be fair, just and reasonable. CORRECTED.
4. **Right to water (D24).** hess3 ch.7 p.89 says the Constitution recognises the right to water under Art. 21. Art. 21's text is silent; the Court has read the right in. CORRECTED. The 2007 Andhra Pradesh High Court example was not looked up.
5. **Right to property (J02, D04).** keps2 p.46 says the Supreme Court held in 1973 that the right was not part of the basic structure, and that the 44th Amendment was of '1978'. The 1973 statement is a CONFLICT with POL-06's reading of Kesavananda; the Act is of 1978 but took effect on 20 Jun 1979.
6. **Fundamental duties (I07).** keps2 p.44 says ten duties; there are eleven since the 86th Amendment (in force 1 Apr 2010). hees2 p.155 is correct.
7. **'Shelter and livelihood' (D19).** keps2 p.35 says the Court held both are part of the right to life. Livelihood is in Olga Tellis; 'shelter' was not found there. UNVERIFIED for shelter.
8. **Old law in NCERT.** keps2 p.35 (assembly of five or more persons, the old CrPC power) and p.38 (children below 14 in 'dangerous jobs') describe older law; current statutes were BLOCKED (X03, X05).
9. **Certiorari (H01).** keps2 p.41 describes certiorari as transferring a case to a higher court. The ordinary meaning is quashing an order. Flagged for POL-04; not verified here.
10. **Dated scheme.** keps1 p.74 and keps2 p.43 mention a rural employment guarantee scheme; its current legal form was not checked.
11. **hess3 and gess3 are the older Social and Political Life books** (printed 2024-25). The new curriculum books for Class 7 and Class 8 are gees2 and hees2 (2025-26 and 2026-27). The 2022 rationalisation list trims parts of the old Class VII ch.1 and Class VIII chapters (booklet pp.19-20); the changes were not matched line by line, and the 2024-25 printing still teaches the equality, marginalisation and public-facility sections used here.

## What the topic needs that NCERT does not cover

- The text of Arts 12-35, 36-51A, 300A as published by the Legislative Department (10 Sep 2026 edition).
- The cases: Maneka Gandhi 1978, Puttaswamy 2017, Olga Tellis 1985, D.K. Basu 1996, PUDR 1982, Shreya Singhal 2015, Indra Sawhney 1992, Jarnail Singh 2018, Jaishri Patil 2021, Janhit Abhiyan 2022, Davinder Singh 2024, Devaru 1957, Bijoe Emmanuel 1986.
- The 77th, 81st, 85th, 93rd, 102nd, 103rd, 105th, 106th Amendments for reservation.
- Privacy (nothing in keps2). EWS and sub-classification are not in any NCERT book read.
