# Subtopic maps: decisions (3 Nov 2026)

**Maps:** Polity 602 rows, History 1,290, Geography 1,124, Economics 734 (NCERT headings, syllabus phrases, past-paper ideas).
Each subject's summary lists books, editions, gaps and unverified items.

**Part counts.** The brief's formula (1,500 words a part, words summed over every book) counted the same idea once per book
and gave about twice the parts the researched pilots needed. Rule now: per topic, the deepest NCERT book only, then
max(1, ceil(words / 4,000), ceil(subtopics / 12)), taking the highest across the four maps. Checked against the pilots:
Basic Structure 2 (research 2), Plassey 2 (2), monsoon 3 (2), repo rate 2 (3). Researched topics keep their researched
parts. Every count stays an estimate until the topic's own depth checklist is built. `parts.json` holds the numbers.

**New topics added from the gaps:** POL-39 special provisions and scheduled areas, POL-40 Union Territories and the
Eighth Schedule, POL-41 what democracy is, POL-42 police, FIR and courts; GEO-42 latitude, longitude and time, GEO-43
map reading, GEO-44 the water cycle, GEO-45 land use; ECO-29 shares and bonds, ECO-30 demand and supply, ECO-31
industrial policy, ECO-32 scarcity and choice; HIS-70 anti-caste reform before 1947, HIS-71 forests and tribes, HIS-72 print.

**Left out on purpose:** world history before the 18th century (outside the syllabus); Geography practicals beyond map
reading; gap rows that belong to another subject (cross-border terrorism, India's role in world affairs) stay with that subject.

**Still open:** the remaining smaller gap groups in each summary (for example Economics' other proposed topics, History's

Topics with no NCERT chapter (listed in each summary) take their content from the publisher of record.
