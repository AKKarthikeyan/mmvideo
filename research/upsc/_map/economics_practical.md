**New topics (19):** ECO-33 to ECO-51. The Economics serial is now seven seasons: basics; money and banking in daily life;
saving, investing and insurance; the government's money; India and the world economy; the story of India's economy;
development and people (`docs/upsc/SERIES.md`).

**Guardrail for the saving and investing season:** explain how each instrument works and who regulates it. No advice, no
"best fund", no return promises, no share-price charts (house rule 6). Interest rates and limits are living facts, dated and
checked against the government or regulator page.
