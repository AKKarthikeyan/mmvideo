# What UPSC itself says about Mains answers

Source: UPSC Examination Notice, Civil Services Examination 2026 (Notif-CSP-2026-Engl-060226Rev.pdf, local copy in
`research/upsc/_notices/sources/`), Rules and "Part B: Main Examination", pp. 32-34. Read 10 Nov 2026.

1. **Purpose:** the Main Examination "is intended to assess the overall intellectual traits and depth of understanding of
   candidates rather than merely the range of their information and memory."
2. **GS questions:** test "basic understanding of all relevant issues, and ability to analyze, and take a view on conflicting
   socio-economic goals, objectives and demands." Candidates "must give relevant, meaningful and succinct answers."
   A well-educated person should be able to answer them "without any specialized study".
3. **Optionals:** honours-degree level (above a bachelor's, below a master's).
4. **Marking rules for every paper:**
   - "Marks will not be allotted for mere superficial knowledge."
   - "Credit will be given for orderly, effective and exact expression combined with due economy of words."
   - Handwriting that is not easily legible earns a deduction.
   - Irrelevant matter or marks that could reveal identity earn a penalty or the script is not evaluated.
5. **On the question papers:** word limits, where printed, are to be followed; graphs and illustrations may be drawn in
   the space given for the answer (cover pages of the Mains papers we hold, e.g. Economics Paper I 2025).
6. **Essay:** keep closely to the subject, arrange ideas in orderly fashion, write concisely; credit for effective and exact expression.

UPSC publishes no model answers and no marking scheme. Everything beyond this list (structure, intros, conclusions,
diagrams) is practice reported by toppers, examiners' public remarks or coaching institutes, and must be labelled as such.
