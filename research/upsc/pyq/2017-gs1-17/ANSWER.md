# PYQ-2017-GS1-17: Monsoon Asia and Half of Humanity: A 250-Word Answer (UPSC GS I 2017)

**Question** (UPSC CSE (Main) 2017, General Studies Paper I, Q17, 15 marks):
> What characteristics can be assigned to monsoon climate that succeeds in feeding more than 50 percent of the world population residing in Monsoon Asia ?

Checked against the PDF: research/upsc/_pyq/mains/2017_gs1.pdf page 4.

**Working budget:** 200-250 words, about 11 minutes in the hall (UPSC's printed limit is 250 words (printed on the paper); 11 minutes is our arithmetic from 3 hours for 250 marks). Sheet: 224 words.

## Demands

- **D1** The characteristics of the monsoon climate
- **D2** How that climate feeds so many people (the link to food)
- **D3** Test the premise (more than 50 percent) and name the risks

## The answer sheet

_Monsoon climate_ means a seasonal reversal of winds. East, South-East and South Asia hold 54.1% of humanity, so 'about half' is the safe reading. _(demands D1; claims M001, 2017-GS1-17-02)_

**1. Characteristics of the climate** _(demands D1; claims )_

- _Seasonal reversal_: a hot monsoonal climate across south and south-east Asia (NCERT).
- _Two systems_: South and South-East Asia June-September; East Asia June-August (IPCC AR6); Japan's _Baiu_ runs early June to late July (JMA).
- _Concentrated rain_: June-September gives 74.9% of India's annual rain, with Kerala onset about 1 June.
- _Variable_: the standard deviation is about 10% of the mean, and El Niño can cut it. _(demands D1; claims M001, 2017-GS1-17-11, 2017-GS1-17-12, M046, M027, M081, M073)_

**2. How it feeds the people** _(demands D2; claims )_

- _Rice_ is the bridge: Asia grows 89.4% of the world's rice (FAOSTAT 2024), and FAO calls it central to the food security of about half of humanity.
- The top five producers, all in the monsoon belt, make 71.2% of world rice.
- Rice needs over 100 cm of rain or irrigation; _kharif_ crops are sown with the monsoon. _(demands D2; claims 2017-GS1-17-07, 2017-GS1-17-06, 2017-GS1-17-08, M087)_

[Diagram: Fig. 1: People and rice] Four bars: share of world rice grown in Asia; share from the top five producers; share of world people in the three UN regions; share of Asia's rice that depends on rain alone. _(demands D2, D3; claims 2017-GS1-17-07, 2017-GS1-17-08, 2017-GS1-17-02, 2017-GS1-17-09)_

**3. The premise and the risks** _(demands D3; claims )_

- 54.1% counts whole UN regions, an upper bound; it was already 52.2% in 1950.
- About 40% of Asia's rice depends on rain alone, and FAO names _rainfall instability_ a main risk.
- IPCC AR6: these regions' people depend heavily on freshwater for agriculture. _(demands D3; claims 2017-GS1-17-02, 2017-GS1-17-04, 2017-GS1-17-09, 2017-GS1-17-10, 2017-GS1-17-13)_

So the monsoon feeds Asia as a seasonal, rain-fed rice engine; its variability, not its size, is the risk. _(demands D3; claims 2017-GS1-17-10)_

## The weaker answer (ours, for comparison)

- Monsoon Asia has more than half the world's people, exactly as the question says. → **that holds only for whole UN regions (54.1%, an upper bound); say 'about half'**
- The monsoon is regular, so food is always secure. → **rainfall instability is a main rice risk, and about 40% of Asia's rice depends on rain alone**
- Rice is grown only in India and China. → **five monsoon countries make 71.2% of the world's rice, including Bangladesh, Indonesia and Viet Nam**
- Monsoon means only the Indian summer rain. → **East Asia has its own system, with different months**
- (no figures: 'a lot of people eat rice') → **no named source and no number**
