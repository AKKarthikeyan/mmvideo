# 2017 GS1 Q17 (15 marks, 250 words, "can")
Q: What characteristics can be assigned to monsoon climate that succeeds in feeding more than 50 percent of the world population residing in Monsoon Asia?
Topic ledger: research/upsc/GEO-07/claims.csv (M-ids). New claims: 2017-GS1-17-01 to -14 (read 2026-11-10).

## Part A: characteristics of the monsoon climate
- Seasonal wind reversal, hot monsoonal climate across south and south-east Asia: M001, M002, M041, M098.
- Two Asian sub-systems and their seasons: new -11 (S/SE Asia June-Sept; East Asia June-Aug), -12 (Japan Baiu, early June-late July; Okinawa a month earlier).
- Onset, advance, withdrawal dates and rain concentration: M027, M029, M033, M046 (74.9% of rain in June-Sept), M044, M047.
- Variability and failure risks: M081 (SD about 10%), M066/M067 (breaks), M073/M074 (ENSO), M052/M093 (drought definitions), new -10 (FAO: rainfall instability a main rice risk).
- Long-run change: M083, M084 (weakening then projected rise, IPCC).

## Part B: how it feeds the population
- Population base: -01 (world 8.16 bn; Asia 58.9%), -02 (East + South-East + South Asia 4.415 bn = 54.1%), -03 (India + China 35.2%), -04 (52.2% already in 1950).
- Rice link: -06 (FAO: Asia over 90% of rice, central to food security of about half of humanity), -07 (FAOSTAT 2024: Asia 89.4% of production), -08 (top five producers 71.2%, all monsoon belt), -09 (about 40% of Asia's rice rainfed), M087 (rice needs over 100 cm), M116/M117/M118 (kharif share of Indian foodgrains, monsoon and harvest).
- Dependence on freshwater: -13 (IPCC AR6).

## Premise flag
- "More than 50 percent of the world population in Monsoon Asia": holds only if whole UN regions of East, South-East and South Asia are counted (54.1%, upper bound because it includes non-monsoon land). Geen et al. 2020 give "over one third" for ALL monsoons worldwide (claim -05, CONFLICT). UN DESA does not define Monsoon Asia. Safe wording: "about half of humanity lives in Asia's monsoon belt".

## Not supported
- A single official figure for "population living under the Asian monsoon" (no publisher of record found). FAO's per-country rice-consumption figures were not pulled; FAOSTAT production shares stand in.
- Southeast Asian monsoon specifics (Indochina onset dates): not read at a primary source.
