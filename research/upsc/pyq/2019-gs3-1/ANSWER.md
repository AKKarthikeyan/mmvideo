**Question** (UPSC CSE (Main) 2019, General Studies Paper III, Q1, 10 marks):
> Enumerate the indirect taxes which have been subsumed in the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India. Also, comment on the revenue implications of the GST introduced in India since July 2017.

## Demands

- **D1** Enumerate the Union and State indirect taxes subsumed in GST
- **D2** Comment on the revenue implications since July 2017

## The answer sheet

GST began on 1 July 2017 and merged many Union and State taxes into one. _(demands D1; claims 2019-GS3-1-03)_

**1. Taxes subsumed** _(demands D1; claims 2019-GS3-1-01, 2019-GS3-1-02)_

- Union (8 heads): _central excise_, other excise duties, CVD, SAD, _service tax_, related cesses.
- States (9 heads): _VAT_, central sales tax, purchase, luxury, entry, entertainment and advertisement taxes, lottery and betting taxes, cesses.
- Outside (CBIC): alcohol; five petroleum goods until a date the Council recommends. _(demands D1; claims 2019-GS3-1-01, 2019-GS3-1-02, 2019-GS3-1-03)_

**2. Revenue implications** _(demands D2; claims 2019-GS3-1-04)_

- Basis: gross, with cess.
- Rose from Rs 7.41 lakh crore (nine months, 2017-18) to 20.18 lakh crore in 2023-24 and 22.09 lakh crore in 2024-25.
- One fall: 12.22 to 11.37 lakh crore in 2020-21, the pandemic year (our reading).
- _Compensation_ protected States at 14% growth on 2015-16; PIB: about Rs 49,000 crore, 83,000 crore and 1.65 lakh crore released for 2017-18 to 2019-20. _(demands D2; claims 2019-GS3-1-04, 2019-GS3-1-09, 2019-GS3-1-11, 2019-GS3-1-12)_

Revenue rose, with one fall in 2020-21 (our reading: the pandemic). We found no official like-for-like comparison with the replaced taxes. _(demands D2; claims 2019-GS3-1-04)_

## The weaker answer (ours, for comparison)

- GST collected more than the taxes it replaced. → **we found no official like-for-like figure; collections alone do not show it**
- Collections rose every year. → **they fell in 2020-21, from 12.22 to 11.37 lakh crore**
- Alcohol and petrol are taxed under GST today. → **alcohol is outside; for the five petroleum goods CBIC says the Council will recommend a date**
- Mixes figures with and without cess in one series. → **one basis only; the two differ (22.09 lakh crore with cess for 2024-25)**
