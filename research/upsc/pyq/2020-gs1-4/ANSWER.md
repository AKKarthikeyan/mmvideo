**Question** (UPSC CSE (Main) 2020, General Studies Paper I, Q4, 10 marks):
> Discuss the geophysical characteristics of Circum-Pacific Zone.

Checked against the PDF: research/upsc/_pyq/mains/2020_gs1.pdf page 2.

**Working budget:** 120-150 words, about 7 minutes in the hall (UPSC's printed limit is 150 words (printed on the paper); 7 minutes is our arithmetic from 3 hours for 250 marks). Sheet: 139 words.

## Demands

- **D1** What the zone is and how far it extends
- **D2** Its plate-tectonic setting and relief (subduction, trenches, exceptions)
- **D3** Seismicity: depth of foci and great earthquakes
- **D4** Volcanism

## The answer sheet

The _Ring of Fire_ is a horseshoe of 450+ volcanoes about 40,250 km long, the most active zone on Earth. _(demands D1; claims 2020-GS1-4-01, 2020-GS1-4-02)_

**1. Setting and relief** _(demands D2; claims )_

- Mostly _convergent_: plates subduct, e.g. Nazca under South America, raising the Andes.
- _Trenches_ 8-10 km deep; Challenger Deep, 10,935 m.
- Exceptions: the San Andreas _transform_ fault, about 5 cm a year; Hawaii, a _hotspot_ inside the plate.
- The Pacific is shrinking as the Atlantic widens. _(demands D2; claims 2020-GS1-4-03, 2020-GS1-4-04, 2020-GS1-4-12, 2020-GS1-4-13, 2020-GS1-4-11)_

[Diagram: Fig. 1: One process, four features] A flow: subduction → trench → earthquake foci → volcanic arc. _(demands D2, D3, D4; claims 2020-GS1-4-03, 2020-GS1-4-05, 2020-GS1-4-06)_

**2. Earthquakes** _(demands D3; claims )_

- Foci deepen along the _Wadati-Benioff zone_, from 0-70 km to over 300 km (NCERT: rim quakes are deep-seated).
- 7 of the 8 quakes of M 8.8 or more since 1900 struck the rim, e.g. Chile 1960, M9.5. _(demands D3; claims 2020-GS1-4-06, 2020-GS1-4-09, 2020-GS1-4-08)_

**3. Volcanism** _(demands D4; claims )_

- Arcs rise above the 100 km contour; more than half of active volcanoes ring the Pacific (USGS). _(demands D4; claims 2020-GS1-4-06, V026)_

One process, four features: plates dive, trenches form, quakes deepen, volcanoes rise. _(demands D4; claims 2020-GS1-4-05)_
