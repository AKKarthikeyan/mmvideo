## The answer sheet

The 101st Amendment (s.18) promised States compensation for GST revenue loss for five years; the Compensation Act, 2017 made it a formula. _(demands D1; claims 2020-GS3-12-01, 2020-GS3-12-02)_

**1. Rationale** _(demands D1; claims 2020-GS3-12-03)_

- _Protected revenue_: 14% yearly growth on the 2015-16 base (ss.3-4, 6).
- Compensation is projected minus actual revenue, on _CAG_-audited figures (s.7).
- Paid from a _cess_ into a non-lapsable _Compensation Fund_ (ss.8, 10), for five years 'or such period as may be prescribed'.
- The Joint Secretary (DoR) told the 41st Council the Chairperson had said earlier that cess, not the Consolidated Fund, would pay. _(demands D1; claims 2020-GS3-12-03, 2020-GS3-12-04, 2020-GS3-12-05, 2020-GS3-12-06, 2020-GS3-12-07)_

**2. COVID and the Fund** _(demands D2; claims 2020-GS3-12-08)_

- On 31 July 2020 the Fund held about Rs 11,000 crore.
- Finance Secretary: around Rs 3,00,000 crore needed; Rs 1,65,000 crore GST implementation, Rs 1,35,000 crore COVID (41st Council). After Rs 68,700 crore of Fund and cess, GST alone left Rs 96,477 crore.
- _Special Window_: the Centre borrowed Rs 1.1 lakh crore (2020-21) and Rs 1.59 lakh crore (2021-22), passed on as back-to-back loans.
- The cess was extended beyond June 2022 till the shortfall is covered. _(demands D2; claims 2020-GS3-12-08, 2020-GS3-12-10, 2020-GS3-12-30, 2020-GS3-12-19, 2020-GS3-12-13)_

**3. Federal tensions** _(demands D3; claims 2020-GS3-12-15)_

- Punjab's Minister: the options were not in accordance with law; _Article 279A(11)_ offered a dispute mechanism.
- Assam's Minister: about 21 States had no dispute.
- Attorney General's opinion: the entitlement is 'hard coded in the Act'; no express duty on the Centre to raise resources. _(demands D3; claims 2020-GS3-12-15, 2020-GS3-12-16, 2020-GS3-12-09, 2020-GS3-12-19)_

The bargain held: the Ministry says all compensation due to May 2022 was released (PIB), part through borrowed loans. We saw no record of the 279A(11) mechanism being set up. _(demands D1, D2, D3; claims 2020-GS3-12-15, 2020-GS3-12-19)_
