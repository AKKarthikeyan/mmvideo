**Question** (UPSC CSE (Main) 2021, General Studies Paper I, Q7, 10 marks):
> Mention the global occurrence of volcanic eruptions in 2021 and their impact on regional environment.

Checked against the PDF: research/upsc/_pyq/mains/2021_gs1.pdf page 2.

**Working budget:** 120-150 words, about 7 minutes in the hall (UPSC's printed limit is 150 words (printed on the paper); 7 minutes is our arithmetic from 3 hours for 250 marks). Sheet: 144 words.

## Demands

- **D1** Mention the global occurrence of volcanic eruptions in 2021 (where, how many, how big)
- **D2** Give their impact on the regional environment (region by region)

## The answer sheet

NOAA's database lists _seven significant eruptions_ in 2021, none above VEI 4: regional in effect, not global. _(demands D1; claims 2021-GS1-7-01, 2021-GS1-7-02)_

**1. Where they occurred** _(demands D1; claims 2021-GS1-7-01)_

- Americas 3 (Ecuador, Guatemala, _St Vincent_), Europe 2 (Italy, _La Palma_), Africa 1 (Nyiragongo), Asia 1 (Semeru).
- Only St Vincent and Semeru reached VEI 4.
- Hunga Tonga is 2022, not 2021. _(demands D1; claims 2021-GS1-7-01, 2021-GS1-7-02, 2021-GS1-7-16)_

**2. Impact on regional environment** _(demands D2; claims 2021-GS1-7-05)_

- _Caribbean_: St Vincent's ash covered the island; about 16,000 were evacuated, water and power failed, banana farming was hit.
- _Central Africa_: Nyiragongo destroyed about 5,000 houses and disrupted water and power.
- _Europe_: La Palma erupted for 85 days, covering 12 km2 in lava; 1.8 Mt SO2 drifted over Africa, Europe and the Caribbean.
- _Pacific_: Fukutoku-Oka-no-Ba's pumice raft drifted 1,300 km, stranding on Okinawa's reefs and mangroves. _(demands D2; claims 2021-GS1-7-03, 2021-GS1-7-05, 2021-GS1-7-06, 2021-GS1-7-07, 2021-GS1-7-08, 2021-GS1-7-09, 2021-GS1-7-13, 2021-GS1-7-14)_

So the harm was regional: ash, water, land, air. La Palma's SO2 was a tenth of Pinatubo's; no global cooling followed. _(demands D2; claims 2021-GS1-7-10)_
