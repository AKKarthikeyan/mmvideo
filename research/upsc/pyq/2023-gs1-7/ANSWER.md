**Question** (UPSC CSE (Main) 2023, General Studies Paper I, Q7, 10 marks):
> Why is the South-West Monsoon called ‘Purvaiya’ (easterly) in Bhojpur Region? How has this directional seasonal wind system influenced the cultural ethos of the region?

## Demands

- **D1** Why the South-West Monsoon is called Purvaiya (easterly) in the Bhojpur region
- **D2** How this wind system influenced the region's cultural ethos

## The answer sheet

_Purvaiya_ is the easterly (_purva_) wind of Bhojpuri song; the monsoon's Bay branch reaches the Ganga plain from the east. _(demands D1; claims 2023-GS1-7-01, 2023-GS1-7-02, M042)_

**1. Why easterly** _(demands D1; claims )_

- The Bay of Bengal branch, bent by the Arakan Hills, moves _westward_ along the Ganga plain (NCERT).
- IMD: north of the _monsoon trough_ (Ganganagar-Prayagraj-Kolkata) winds are easterly or south-easterly; placing Bhojpur there is our inference. _(demands D1; claims M042, 2023-GS1-7-03, 2023-GS1-7-04, 2023-GS1-7-05)_

[Diagram: Fig. 1: From the Bay of Bengal to the song] A flow: the Bay branch, the Ganga plain, the Purva wind, the Kajari song. _(demands D1, D2; claims M042, 2023-GS1-7-03, 2023-GS1-7-05, 2023-GS1-7-14)_

**2. Cultural ethos** _(demands D2; claims )_

- _Farming_: paddy is sown from the third week of June, and rain calls transplanting in song (Jassal).
- _Kajari_, sung by women in Sawan (July-August); the slack season brings visits to natal homes.
- _Migration_ shaped Biraha, Bidesiya and Kajari: the same wind pains wives left behind (Chauhan and Mishra).
- 50,579,447 speak Bhojpuri (Census 2011). _(demands D2; claims 2023-GS1-7-09, 2023-GS1-7-12, 2023-GS1-7-14, 2023-GS1-7-10, 2023-GS1-7-11, 2023-GS1-7-15, 2023-GS1-7-02, 2023-GS1-7-07)_

So the easterly sets the work, the songs and the ache: a boon for fields, a pain for the parted. _(demands D2; claims 2023-GS1-7-17)_

## The weaker answer (ours, for comparison)

- Purvaiya comes from a Bhojpuri suffix meaning 'from'. → **no scholarly source gives that etymology; the word appears in songs as the easterly wind**
- A south-west monsoon cannot be easterly. → **north of the trough, winds are easterly or south-easterly**
- IMD names Bhojpur's wind as easterly. → **IMD names the trough, not Bhojpur: that step is our inference**
- Festivals like Hariyali Teej come from the easterly wind. → **no official or university-press source ties them to it**
- The wind only brings joy. → **the songs also call it an enemy of wives left behind**
