# 2023 GS1 Q7 (10 marks, 150 words, "how")
Q: Why is the South-West Monsoon called 'Purvaiya' (easterly) in the Bhojpur region? How has this directional seasonal wind system influenced the cultural ethos of the region?
Topic ledger: research/upsc/GEO-07/claims.csv (M-ids). New claims: 2023-GS1-7-01 to -20 (read 2026-11-10).

## Part A: why 'Purvaiya' (easterly)
- Word itself: -01 (Jassal, Duke UP: puruvaiya = "gusts of easterly winds"), -02 (Rupkatha 2024: Purva = easterly wind).
- Physics: ledger M016/M017 (monsoon trough), M042 (Bay of Bengal branch turns west up the Ganga plain, NCERT), new -03 (IMD 2025 report: trough Ganganagar-Prayagraj-Kolkata, easterlies/south-easterlies north of it), -04 (IMD FAQ), -05.
- Also M013/M095 (ITCZ and belts follow the sun) if space allows.
- Caveat: the step "Bhojpur sits at or just north of the trough, so its low-level wind is easterly" is OUR inference (claim -03 note). IMD and NCERT never name Bhojpur. Say "the monsoon reaches the Ganga plain as a south-easterly/easterly stream" rather than "IMD says Bhojpur wind is easterly".
- Etymology ("-aiya" suffix) and the proverb: UNVERIFIED (-19). Drop.

## Part B: cultural ethos
- Region and size: -06 (eastern UP and western Bihar), -07 (50.58 million Bhojpuri speakers, Census 2011).
- Farming calendar: -08 (906 mm, onset 2nd week of June), -09 (rice sowing 3rd wk June-4th wk July; 60.6% canal), -10 (Varsha = Sravana-Bhadra = July-Aug), -12 (rain calls transplanting song), -11 (slack season, natal-home visits). Ledger M087 (kharif rice), M027 (Kerala onset 1 June).
- Songs: -13 and -14 (Kajari: Sawan, dark clouds, women, swings/natal visits), -02 and -01 (the Purva wind inside the songs), -17 (rain blessing for fields, pain for separated wives).
- Migration ethos: -15 (migration shaped Biraha, Bidesiya, Kajari), -16 (pardes destinations).
- Official recognition: -18 (Sangeet Natak Akademi documents Bhojpuri songs; says nothing on monsoon).
