# Earnings-visual creators (Sankeys, results infographics, data-led results content) for global large caps: competitor scan as of Oct 2026

**How this was researched, and what that means for the numbers.** WebFetch could not resolve DNS for any site I tried (appeconomyinsights.com, substack.com subdomains, sidestack.io, passionfroot.me, stockmarketnerd.com), and running curl through Bash was blocked. So every finding below comes from **web-search result summaries and snippets**, not from pages I read in full. Follower and subscriber counts are snapshots whose dates the search tool often could not pin down. Treat them as approximate and check them live before publishing. Where the search tool gave a date, it is stated. Third-party estimates are marked **[3P est.]**. Research date: 2026-10-18.

## Q1. Competitor profiles: platforms, audience, cadence, engagement, format, voice, business model

### Takeaway
App Economy Insights (AEI) is the category leader. It has roughly 300-345K newsletter subscribers (its own claims, which vary by page), about 234-244K followers on X, and about 24K on LinkedIn. It sells Premium and PRO tiers and takes sponsorships. Visual Capitalist is far bigger (20M+ monthly readers by its own claim) but is a general data-viz publisher funded by sponsors, not an earnings specialist. Quartr (about 147-153K on X) and Fiscal.ai (about 93K on X) are SaaS companies that use earnings visuals as marketing. None of the players found produces narrated or animated earnings Sankeys as a core format; all are static or carousel-first.

## Q4. Audience criticisms of these visuals

### Takeaway
I found **no documented criticism of specific AEI Sankeys** (errors, "doesn't add up"). Public reaction found was mostly praise ("every company should report earnings via a Sankey"). General critiques of Sankey charts do exist: lumped flows can mislead, widths can render wrong in some tools, and a Sankey squashes time into one static picture. Fiscal-year labelling is a real risk: SankeyArt labels Nvidia's quarter "Q1 FY27" but Amazon and Tesla's "Q1 FY26", a mismatch an audience could easily misread.

### Cited Findings
- Praise: "EVERY company should report earnings via a Sankey diagram" — [X @mrjain](https://x.com/mrjain/status/1717484396125761983); "Sankey diagrams = muuuuuuch better for earnings reports" — [X @stephr_wong](https://x.com/stephr_wong/status/1586389153926639619)
- Missing-context complaint: a reader on AEI's income-statement guide asked why capex was not mentioned and where it fits in — [AEI comments](https://www.appeconomyinsights.com/p/how-to-analyze-an-income-statement/comments)
- SankeyMATIC's creator says showing "Provision for income taxes" as one lump sum "would be a bit misleading", so they split it — [SankeyMATIC Apple data](https://sankeymatic.com/data/apple/)
- A BI vendor argues that a Sankey collapses time into one static picture, and that if the widths don't tell the story you don't need a Sankey — [Astrato](https://www.astrato.io/blog/sankey-use-cases)
- In one tool (Power BI), ribbon thickness did not reflect the weighting — [MS Fabric community](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Desktop/Sankey-weighting-not-reflected-accurately-in-visual/m-p/228988)
- Junk Charts called a Spotify P&L Sankey "a job well done", noting how it handled negative flows — [Harvard tagteam/Junk Charts](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/1979/feed_items/3377175/content)
- A September 2026 blog post titled "The Accursed Sankey Chart" (content not retrieved; possibly a political rather than earnings Sankey) — [Balloon Juice](https://balloon-juice.com/2026/09/16/late-night-creepshow-open-thread-the-accursed-sankey-chart/)
- A third-party blog credits AEI with popularizing income-statement Sankeys — [Visualign](https://visualign.org/2023/10/27/sankey-diagrams-for-income-statements/)
- Fiscal-year labels differ in SankeyArt posts: NVIDIA "Q1 FY27" vs Amazon and Tesla "Q1 FY26" — [X @SankeyArt](https://x.com/sankeyart?lang=en)

### Inferences
- The likely problem areas for a global channel are: fiscal vs calendar quarters (Nvidia, Microsoft and Apple fiscal years do not match the calendar); currency (TSMC reports in NT$ but guides in USD, as AEI's "about 44% in USD" phrasing shows); missing capex and cash flow (Sankeys show the P&L only); and lumped or negative items (tax, other income). These are inferred from the general critiques and labelling examples above, not from documented audience complaints.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads were found (r/dataisugly, r/investing) criticising earnings Sankeys. A direct Reddit search was not possible.
- No documented examples of factual errors in AEI or SankeyArt charts were found.

## Q5. How saturated is the static Sankey format? How many copycats?

### Takeaway
It is heavily saturated. At least six or seven tools now generate earnings Sankeys automatically from SEC filings (SankeyArt, Sankify, Sankey Earnings Generator, Paperglass, SankeyMATIC, JV Invest, GitHub sankey-finance), several claiming "AI". SankeyArt keeps a public gallery covering 2020-2026 for most mega caps. A static Sankey is now a commodity that anyone can make the same day. The difference is no longer in the image; it lies in speed, explanation, video and trust.

### Cited Findings
- SankeyArt public gallery of finance Sankeys (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Berkshire, Eli Lilly, Exxon, JPMorgan, Microsoft, Netflix, Tencent, UnitedHealth, Walmart, Nvidia, TSMC) based on SEC filings, 2020-2026 — [SankeyArt gallery](https://www.sankeyart.com/sankeys/public/); [Apple 2026 Q1](https://www.sankeyart.com/sankeys/public/157546/); [Microsoft 2026 Q1](https://www.sankeyart.com/sankeys/public/149088/)
- Sankify: income-statement Sankeys from SEC filings (Broadcom, Nvidia vs AMD, JPMorgan, Goldman) — [Sankify blog](https://www.sankify.ai/blog)
- Sankey Earnings Generator: "Visualize Company Earnings with AI" from SEC filings — [sankeyearnings.com](https://sankeyearnings.com/)
- Paperglass earnings Sankey generator (enter a ticker) — [Paperglass](https://paperglass.co/factory/earning-sankey)
- Open-source generator — [GitHub anarion80/sankey-finance](https://github.com/anarion80/sankey-finance)
- Excel how-to for income-statement Sankeys — [Excel Dashboard School](https://exceldashboardschool.com/sankey-diagram/income-statement/)
- SankeyMATIC posts quarterly financial-results data (Nvidia, Apple) — [SankeyMATIC financial results](https://sankeymatic.com/news/tag/financial-results/)

### Inferences
- With free and AI tools producing a Sankey from a ticker, a static Sankey alone offers no edge. AEI's moat is its brand, an early start (it was popularizing the format by 2022), and written analysis.
- For a newcomer, the openings are: narrated video, global or non-US coverage (TSMC, Samsung, ASML, Indian ADRs) with careful currency and fiscal-year handling, and speed.

### Gaps
- The number of individual X, Instagram or LinkedIn accounts posting earnings Sankeys was not counted (no search tool for this). Copycat follower sizes: not found.
- Usage numbers for the generator tools: not found.
