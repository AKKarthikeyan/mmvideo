## Q2. Which companies recur across channels, and which angles work?

### Takeaway
The companies that recur across sources in this window are **Anthropic, SpaceX and OpenAI** (the 2026 mega-IPO wave), with **Nvidia** as the "cheap versus expensive AI" foil. **Tesla, Meta and Netflix** are the live earnings hooks for the week of 19–23 Oct. The angles I saw are valuation sanity checks ("is X really worth $N?"), "what the filing really says", collapse/scandal, and "how X makes money".

### Cited Findings
- Anthropic: Patrick Boyle's $2T valuation video (27 Sep 2026). Reports put the planned IPO at up to $100B raised, at a valuation of around $2T — [Finance Labs](https://financelabssei.substack.com/p/patrick-boyles-2-trillion-ai-question); [Motley Fool community](https://discussion.fool.com/t/anthropic-ipo-and-ai-stock-bubble/126572)
- SpaceX: covered by ColdFusion (a filing/TAM angle) and in a Patrick Boyle IPO episode. A third-party channel made a rebuttal video ("The Math ColdFusion Missed"), which is a sign of engagement. The IPO priced at $135 to raise $75B and closed at $161 (+19%) on 12 Jun 2026 — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/spacex-raises-75-billion-in-record-setting-ipo-ahead-of-nasdaq-debut.html); [YouTube rebuttal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c1eip93s1eA); [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6lIMoXBFIQ1szp8W8AEsBS)
- Nvidia: framed as "the cheapest it has been relative to expected profits in over a decade" (Bloomberg, cited in summaries of the Boyle video). NVDA fell 1.4% to $214.13 on 19 Oct 2026, and the Nasdaq had its worst week since June on an AI sell-off — [Finance Labs](https://financelabssei.substack.com/p/patrick-boyles-2-trillion-ai-question); search summary of [heygotrade week-ahead](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/weekly-economic-outlook-2026-10-19/)
- Tesla: reports Q3 on Wed 21 Oct 2026 after the close. Q3 deliveries were 486,532 against about 461K expected, and consensus EPS is about −14.5% year on year — [heygotrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/weekly-economic-outlook-2026-10-19/); [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/meta-tesla-netflix-earnings-week-70-companies-2026/)
- Meta, Tesla and Netflix lead a roughly 70-company earnings week — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/meta-tesla-netflix-earnings-week-70-companies-2026/)
- Musk documentary ("Musk") reviewed by Bloomberg on 13 Oct 2026 as showing his "dark side" — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-13/elon-musk-documentary-shows-his-dark-side-in-business-and-personal-life)

### Inferences
- Moat Atlas's filings-first approach fits the current IPO-filing moment. Explainers of the SpaceX S-1 (AI share of TAM), the Anthropic valuation versus revenue, and an eventual OpenAI filing are drawing coverage and rebuttals.
- The "is X worth $N?" valuation format (Boyle) and the "what the filing really says" format (ColdFusion) look more distinctive right now than generic "how X makes money" videos, which many small channels also make.
- Tesla (21 Oct), Meta and Netflix give near-term earnings hooks. Earnings-reaction videos from Joseph Carlson and Meet Kevin probably exist, but I could not confirm any.

## Q4. What do the winners' titles and thumbnails have in common?

### Takeaway
Thumbnails could not be seen. From titles alone, the winning formats are: a direct question about a big number ("Is Anthropic Worth Two Trillion Dollars?"), "The Decline of [familiar brand]", and "How Does [brand] Make Money… The [X] Nobody Sees". All three use a household name plus either a huge number or a hidden-truth hook.

### Cited Findings
- "Is Anthropic Worth Two Trillion Dollars?": a question plus a specific big number — [Pandora](https://www.pandora.com/podcast/patrick-boyle-on-finance/PC:56136)
- "Turkey's $20 Billion Fund Collapse Explained": a dollar figure plus collapse plus "Explained" — [Podcast Addict](https://podcastaddict.com/podcast/patrick-boyle-on-finance/5422909)
- "The Decline of Pizza Hut" / "The Decline of KFC": a familiar brand plus decline (top all-time Company Man videos) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@companyman114/)
- "How Does Netflix Make Money in 2026? The Business Model Nobody Sees.": a brand plus "make money" plus a hidden angle — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6MRJVzl8bRs)
- "12 Businesses COLLAPSING in 2026 (The Numbers Are Worse Than You Think)": caps, a list, and a "worse than you think" hook — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O3oBDSYIwKA)
- "SpaceX Secretly Filed as an AI Company": "secretly" plus a reveal from the filing — [BigGo summary](https://finance.biggo.com/podcast/ce1b91076c7279b0)
- Tutorials describe the MagnatesMedia style as smooth, cinematic documentary editing rather than flashy transitions (note: these are tutorials about the style, not data) — [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kuX_HBTjTmg)

### Inferences
- For Moat Atlas, a title pattern such as "[Company] Says It's a ___ Company. Its Filing Says Otherwise", or "Is [Company] Really Worth $[N]? The Filing Math", combines the valuation question with the filing reveal, the two hooks that appear in the in-window evidence.
