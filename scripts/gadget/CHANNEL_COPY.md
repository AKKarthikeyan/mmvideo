# The Inventor's Desk: channel copy (drafted 23 Oct 2026, AK asked "Discription for channel")

## YouTube "About" description (650 characters; limit 1,000)
Every everyday thing has a story.

Who made the first electric fan? How does a fridge keep food cold? How can a radio hear a voice with no wire?

The Inventor's Desk explains the things you use every day (fans, fridges, radios, TVs, ACs and more) in short videos anyone can follow. Each episode tells two stories: how it was invented, and how it works, explained like you're five.

We stick to real sources: the original patents, museum records and official data, shown right on screen. No jargon. No myths.

Watch one, and the next time you switch something on, you'll know the story behind it.

Tell us in the comments what we should explain next!
