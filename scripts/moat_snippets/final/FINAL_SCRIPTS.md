# Moat Snippets: final scripts (production draft)

Ten vertical shorts, each under 30 s at about 2.8 words a second. Hook, analogy, sourced fact, dark joke, closing question.
Every fact below was checked against the best source we could open on 4 Nov 2026; the status of each is in `VERIFICATION_LOG.md`.
Jokes and analogies are opinion. Dark humour is aimed at companies and situations, never people.
Every video ends with the disclaimer card: **Educational research, not investment advice · not SEBI-registered · Sources: moatmarginresearch.com**.

Production notes: on-screen source lines are exactly the `Source` text below. Anything marked *as reported* is secondary and must say so on screen. No brand logos are used; products are drawn.
