## Spoken-aloud check

> The jack your headphones plug into is older than radio. It began on a telegraph desk in eighteen seventy-seven, as a spring-jack: a wedge pushed between two pins to break a line. Telephone exchanges borrowed it. But the wedge had to go in a certain way, so inventors made a plain round plug. By the century's end, operators plugged their head receivers into jacks. Marconi's radio patent came in eighteen ninety-six, nearly twenty years later. Then radio came back for it. In nineteen sixty-nine, a standard for radio receivers described the small sockets your wired earbuds still use. Older than radio, then written into a radio standard. Which old part is hiding in your pocket?
